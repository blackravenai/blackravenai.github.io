Before we
begin.

Can you work on an existing product?

Yes. A project can begin with a particular problem: an awkward workflow, a dated interface, a slow experience, or an AI feature that needs to become useful. We’ll look at what you have and define a focused scope.

Can you take a product from idea to release?

We can work across strategy, design, engineering, and release preparation. We agree on responsibilities, scope, and third-party requirements at the start. App-store review and external approvals remain with their respective platforms.

How do you scope cost and timing?

We start with the product’s needs and your constraints. From there, we can propose a defined first phase and explain the tradeoffs. You won’t need a fully written specification to begin a conversation.

Do you only make AI products?

No. Our own work spans mobile games, astronomy tools, and studio software. We use AI where it contributes to the experience. The starting point is always the person using the product.
