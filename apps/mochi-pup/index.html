A dog-training game

Mochi Pup.

Your real dog, as a game.

Snap a photo and your dog becomes a squishy Mochi twin living in a den you decorate. Everything you really do together feeds it: training lessons, walks, meals, cuddles and games.

Training you can see

Lessons that actually
make sense.

Every lesson opens with a looping cartoon of the exact step. Then you practise together, using a giant YES button as your clicker. If you miss twice in a row, Mochi Pup shows you how to make it easier. Training is always force-free.

The Trick Path

Five lands.
36 tricks and life skills.

Start with the basics and work up to party tricks. Your plan follows what you want help with, whether that’s pulling on the lead or a perfect bow. Finish a trick and you win its Trick Card.

So much to do

A tiny world that’s
really about your dog.

There are daily quests and streaks, a chest every time you level up, the Wheel of Treats, eight kinds of cuddles and eighteen real-life games, from Muffin Tin Puzzle to Living-Room Agility. In the Mochi Market you’ll find more than 120 hats, capes, beds and wall decorations, plus Buddies who follow your pup around the den.

Care & safety, always free

The serious stuff

  • Lost Dog Mode with a printable flyer

Plainly stated

Free to play, for every dog in the house.

The free version includes your Mochi twin, the den, quests, streaks, the daily spin, the first land of the Trick Path plus Sit, Come and Place, eight real-life games, the photo diary and every safety tool. No account is needed. Your dog’s data stays on your phone, and in your family’s iCloud if you share your dog. The free version shows a short ad now and then, but never during a lesson.

How subscriptions work

These are planned US prices; your store shows your local price. Any free trial is an introductory offer on a yearly plan and is shown before you confirm, and the app reminds you two days before it ends. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period, and you can manage or cancel them in your store account settings. If a subscription lapses, nothing is deleted.
