CIRCUIT QUEST — PRESS FACT SHEET

Circuit Quest: Learn & Build is an independent electronics-learning app from BlackRaven AI LLC. It combines an Arduino circuit simulator, 3D breadboard design, guided builds and pixel-art quests.

Available on iPhone and iPad, iOS 15 or later:
https://apps.apple.com/us/app/circuit-quest-learn-build/id6819515995

Free download with optional in-app purchases. The core designer, simulator, lessons, progression and 46 templates are free. No account or hardware is required to begin. There are no ads. Circuit Quest Plus offers monthly/yearly subscriptions for advanced missions and included bonus and Showcase builds; prices and eligible offers appear in the app.

Try a first puzzle: Hub > Playground > Repair Shop > It never lit.

Physical builds require compatible components, sold separately. Follow component specifications and appropriate supervision. Simulation is a learning aid, not a guarantee of electrical safety. The iOS app does not directly program a physical Arduino board. Circuit Quest is not affiliated with Arduino.
