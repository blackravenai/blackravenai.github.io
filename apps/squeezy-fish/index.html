Free to play. Optional Ad-Free Monthly.

Squeezy Fish is planned as a free download with ads. The optional Ad-Free Monthly subscription removes ads while active. The US price is $4.99 per month; prices vary by region and your local price is shown in the app.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation. The subscription automatically renews unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel it in your Apple Account subscription settings. Restore Purchases is available in Reef Shop.
