Plainly stated

Free to keep. Private by design.

Wax & Wane is free to download, with unlimited pages. There are no ads and no tracking, and there is no account. Your journal stays on your device. We can’t read it, and we never sell it or use it to train AI.

An optional upgrade, the Almanac, adds the full Year Ahead, deeper readings, pattern insights and more. It costs $59.99/year with a 7-day free trial, $9.99/month, or $129.99 once. The full Year Ahead is also sold on its own for $11.99. These are US prices; your local price is shown in the app.

How subscriptions work

Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm, or when a free trial ends. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel in your Apple Account subscription settings. Restore Purchases is in the app’s Study.

Wax & Wane is being prepared for the App Store. It isn’t available yet; the download link will appear here at launch.
