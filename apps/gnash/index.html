GNASH!

ONE PHONE. TWO MOUTHS.

A ninety-second duel for two people and one phone. Lay it flat on the table, take an end each, and swat the temple pearl into the other guardian’s jaws before they get it into yours.

NO ADS, EVERWORKS OFFLINENO ACCOUNTFREE TO PLAY
Coming soon to iPhone, iPad and Android · Not yet available to download

Three seconds to learn.

1 · PHONE FLAT

Put it on the table between you. Both press your own end to start.

2 · DRAG TO SWAT

Your paw follows your thumb. Where the pearl lands on it decides where it goes.

3 · INTO THEIR JAWS

Score and your jaws grow wider. Lead, and you have more to guard. Nobody is ever out of it.

Built for the table.

Every word is drawn twice, so the person across the table never reads upside down. Pick first to 3, 5 or 7. Name your players and keep a running series. Give a younger player a head start with smaller jaws to guard. Six solo rivals and a daily challenge for when you are on your own, and a party tournament for up to eight people taking turns.
