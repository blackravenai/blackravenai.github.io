Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What is free and what is Pro. Weighing one action end to end is free forever, and so is the answer: friction and balance weight per key, the fitted target curve, every refusal with its reason, and the complete prescription — lead diameter, lead mass and bore position in millimetres on all 88 keys. Export of your measurements and of the prescription is free on every action at every tier. Pro adds an unlimited action library, reference curves by make and model, CSV import with column mapping, saved target curves, and duplicating an action.
  • Subscription terms. Pro is $99.00 a year and opens with a 21-day free trial (annual only, once per account), or $14.99 a month with no trial, or $249.00 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted or hidden. Every action, every reading and every prescription stays readable, editable, exportable and deletable. A lapse lowers a ceiling on new records; it never takes a record away.
  • The phone measures nothing. GRAMLINE solves over the readings you enter. You still take downweight, upweight and strike weight at the bench with gram weights or an electronic key-measuring device; the app does the arithmetic, the curve fit and the lead choice.
  • Why it refuses some keys. Lead makes a key heavier to accelerate as well as easier to press. Where a reading indicates excessive friction, an implausible key ratio, a strike weight out of band, a front weight over the ceiling or no legal lead group inside the bore window, GRAMLINE refuses to prescribe and says which condition fired. Boring lead into a friction fault buries the fault; that is the failure the refusals exist to prevent.
  • The front-weight ceiling is derived, not published. It is derived from published lead-count guidance rather than taken from a published ceiling chart, is labelled derived in the app, is editable, and warns rather than refuses by default.
  • Check the bore before you drill it. GRAMLINE states a lead diameter, a mass and a distance in millimetres from the balance hole. It does not measure your keys and does not run your drill. Verify every position on the key itself before you bore a customer’s keyboard.
  • Restore purchases. Open Settings and tap Restore purchases; entitlements are re-checked with the store.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.

Sources & attribution

GRAMLINE’s touchweight mathematics are implemented from Mario Igrec, Pianos Inside Out, chapter 9 — the balance, front-weight, lead-weight and lead-count equations — and are cited line by line, with page and equation numbers, in the app’s design record; the shipped solver is proved against 31 of that chapter’s printed worked numbers. The target curve is a robust (Huber) trend fitted to your own measured balance weights, and the lead choice is a discrete constrained fit over real lead stock diameters inside the legal mortise window.
