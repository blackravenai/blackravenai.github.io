Need help with Cloche? You're in the right place. BlackRaven AI LLC makes Cloche, and we read every message.

Contact us

Email support@blackravenai.com and we'll help. We aim to reply within 2–3 business days. To speed things up, tell us your device and OS version and describe what happened.

Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What is free and what is Pro. The whole engine and every verdict is free forever on 2 beds — THE WEEK in full, today’s backstop, all 106 crops, “show the working”, both themes, the sample garden, import, and CSV, JSON and text export. Pro adds unlimited beds, the backstop at any date over the whole library, succession runs, every season on the season screen, and the refit applied automatically.
  • Subscription and trial. Pro is $29.99 per year with 28 days free the first time (once per account), $5.99 per month, or $79.99 as a single lifetime payment that never renews. A subscription renews automatically at the same price until you cancel; cancel any time in your App Store or Google Play account settings, at least a day before it renews. Uninstalling does not cancel it. See Terms for the full wording.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is removed or locked. Every bed, sowing, observed frost and measured maturity stays readable, editable, exportable and deletable; only the free limits come back.
  • Where do the maturity times come from. The crop library ships typical packet values, labelled as such on every screen that uses them. Your own packet always wins: type its days-to-maturity on the sowing and Cloche plans from that, then from what you actually measure.
  • Where do the frost dates come from. The zone table is a conventional rule of thumb, shown as zone-typical until you replace it. Cloche plans from the dates you enter and then from the frost you observe. It is not a measured climate dataset.
  • Restore purchases. Open Settings and tap Restore purchases; entitlements are re-checked with the store.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.

Sources & attribution

Cloche computes day length from solar geometry (the Spencer declination series) for the latitude you enter. Its autumn slow-down and winter dormancy models follow documented horticultural practice — the growers’ “+14 day fall factor” rule of thumb and Eliot Coleman’s ten-hour Persephone period — implemented from first principles rather than recited, with the three fitted constants shown to you on the “show the working” sheet. Cloche states dates and day counts computed from your own inputs; it makes no harvest, yield, nutrition or food-safety claim.
