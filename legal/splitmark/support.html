Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What SPLITMARK does, and what it does not decide. SPLITMARK reconciles the documents you are about to publish — the cue sheet, the aid-station plan, the mile markers, the marshal list and the advertised distance — against the GPS track of the course you measured, and prints the arithmetic behind every finding. It never certifies a course, never states that a course is accurate, safe or legal, and never advises on road closures, marshal safety or permits. Those remain matters for you, your measurer and your local authority. A course that raises no findings has not been approved by this app; it has been checked against the track and the sheet you supplied, and nothing more.
  • UNCHECKED is not a pass. A cue line SPLITMARK cannot read is reported as UNCHECKED, by line number, on the face of the report — never guessed at and never silently skipped. The counts always reconcile, so you can see exactly how much of your sheet was actually checked.
  • Where the arithmetic comes from. The measurement arithmetic follows the published USATF/RRTC Course Measurement Procedures Manual, and the app reproduces 97 of 97 of that manual’s worked examples exactly. The short course prevention factor is cited to that manual, never asserted as this app’s own rule. No third-party course data, cue text or map tile is bundled or reproduced anywhere.
  • What is free and what is Pro. The instrument is free forever, at full accuracy: unlimited checks, the whole ribbon, every finding with its printed working, every UNCHECKED line with its number, the worked example, the Prove-it screen, two courses on the shelf — each holding every year and every check you run on it — and every export: the text report, the GPX and the whole shelf as JSON, plus import. Pro adds courses beyond the second, the year-to-year cue diff, house conventions saved per course, and the season report. Pro never buys a better number — the engine is the same engine on both tiers, and your export is never gated.
  • Subscription terms. SPLITMARK Pro is $34.99 a year and opens with a 21-day free introductory offer (annual plan only, offered once per store account), or $6.99 a month with no trial, or $99.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next charge and the period you have paid for runs to its end. We cannot cancel it for you, and the app will not ask you to stay.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted, hidden or truncated. Every course on the shelf, every year’s edition and every recorded check comes back exactly as it was and stays readable, editable, deletable and exportable — however many courses there are. What stops is adding a course beyond the second, opening the year-to-year diff, saving house conventions and reading the season report. A lapse lowers a ceiling on new work; it never takes a record away.
  • Prove it. Open the “Prove it” screen and SPLITMARK re-runs shipped cases whose answers were recorded before the engine existed, on your own handset, showing its answers beside the expected ones — so you can check the instrument rather than trust it.
  • Which files can it read? GPX, TCX, KML and JSON tracks, exactly as a GPS watch, a bike computer or a route planner exports them, and the cue sheet as plain text in any of five common dialects. A track file you pick is read on the device and never copied out of the app.
  • Does it need a signal at the start line? No. The whole engine is arithmetic and geometry over data already on the handset, so the app opens, checks, draws the ribbon and exports with the phone in aeroplane mode.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.
