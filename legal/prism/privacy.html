Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-10

Prism is a camera app made by BlackRaven AI LLC with live lenses that render your camera in real time. It runs entirely on your device. There is no account and no sign-in, and we designed it to need as little of your information as possible.

The short version

  • No account, no login, no name or email required.
  • Your data is saved on your device, not on our servers.
  • We don't run our own analytics or tracking.
  • Prism has no server of its own. Every lens runs on your phone's GPU, and photos, clips and Live Loops are made on the device. The only network activity is Apple's own: when you buy, restore or check Pro, the App Store and StoreKit on your device talk to Apple. No purchase data, and nothing you entered, is sent to us or to any third party.
  • Prism shows no ads. Purchases are made through Apple's App Store directly; there is no third-party purchase or subscription service.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or photos. We have no user accounts and no server-side profile of you. The only time we see your email is if you choose to email our support address.

Data stored on your device

The photos, clips and Live Loops you make are kept in Prism's in-app Darkroom in the app's private storage, along with your lens and app settings. The camera is used only while the viewfinder is open. If you allow it, Prism saves what you make to your Photos library with add-only access — it cannot see or read your existing photos. Editing a photo from your library uses Apple's photo picker, which gives Prism only the photo you choose. Video is recorded without sound; Prism does not use the microphone. Anything you share goes only where you send it. None of it is uploaded or transmitted by the app, and deleting the app deletes the Darkroom (items already saved to Photos stay there). Like any app data, it is included in your device's own iCloud or computer backup if you have those turned on; that backup is handled by Apple under your account, not by us.

Advertising

Prism does not show ads and does not include any advertising SDK.

Purchases

Prism's in-app purchases and subscriptions are sold and processed entirely by Apple through the App Store, using Apple's StoreKit on your device. We never see or store your payment details, and no third-party purchase service is involved: the app asks Apple's StoreKit on your device whether you own Pro, and Apple signs that answer. We receive only the aggregated, anonymous sales reports Apple gives every developer. See the Apple Privacy Policy.

Analytics

Prism does not include our own third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. Any limited, aggregated diagnostics you receive come from the app stores' standard reporting (for example crash counts), which is governed by Apple's and Google's policies, not ours.

Children

Prism is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will address it.

Your choices and rights

Because we don't hold a personal profile of you, most control lives on your device: you can clear the app's local data. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA over data our purchase partners process; you can exercise those directly with those partners (linked above) or contact us and we'll help point you to the right place.

International users

BlackRaven AI LLC operates the app and support from Wyoming, United States. Our purchase partners may process data in other countries under their own safeguards.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy we'll change the date below and post the new version at this URL. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
