Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What is free and what is Pro. Auditing any chart, all nine checks, the full arithmetic behind every flag, the readability statement, the Proof Sheet, the Prove-it run and every export are free forever, on every tier, with three charts on the Shelf and their whole audit history. Pro adds an unlimited Shelf, batch auditing of a whole release drop in one pass, house profiles, and the Proof Sheet as an image. Pro never buys a better audit.
  • Subscription terms. Pro is $39.99 a year with a 21-day free trial (annual plan only, offered once per customer), or $5.99 a month with no trial, or $99.99 once for lifetime. The annual and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next charge and the period you have paid for runs to its end.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted or hidden. Every chart on the Shelf and every audit kept against it stays readable and stays exportable, however many there are. A lapse lowers the ceiling on adding a new chart; it never takes a record away. Past the free limit an audit still runs, displays in full and exports in full — it is simply not shelved.
  • What WASTEKNOT does, and does not do. WASTEKNOT reconciles a chart PDF against its own legend and its own stated figures: symbols drawn against symbols keyed, thread numbers against the DMC list, stated counts against the grid as drawn, page overlap against the pages, skein figures against a cited rule. It does not judge your design, redraw your chart, or promise that a chart it passes is free of errors. Anything it cannot read it reports as unchecked rather than as clear.
  • Where the skein figures come from. Skein estimates use a stitches-per-skein figure you can edit in Settings. Published sources disagree — the commonly quoted range runs from about 1,700 to 1,960 stitches per skein — and the app says so wherever it prints one.
  • Damaged or unusual files. If a PDF is truncated or malformed, WASTEKNOT says the file is damaged rather than reporting the chart as faulty. If it cannot recover part of the grid, those cells are counted as unreadable and named in the report.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.

Sources & attribution

WASTEKNOT decompresses and interprets the PDF itself — a raw DEFLATE decoder and a content-stream interpreter written for this app — then recovers the stitch lattice geometrically and runs nine reconciliation checks. Every threshold was frozen in an answer key before the engine existed, and the accuracy figures the app reports are measured against that key by the bundled Prove-it run. Thread numbering follows the published DMC range; no third-party chart, artwork or swatch imagery is used.
