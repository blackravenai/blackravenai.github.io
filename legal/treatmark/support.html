Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What TREATMARK does, and does not do. It solves a label from the batch you weighed: which product name the recipe permits, the guarantee panel in the format your state has adopted, the calorie statement, the required elements and the smallest legal type size. It is not a regulator, it is not a consultant, and it does not submit anything to anyone. It carries no claim that a label it produces will be accepted, and it says so on every screen that matters. The state you register with has the final word.
  • Why it will not name an AAFCO ingredient for you. The AAFCO Official Publication is a paid publication and its ingredient definitions are deliberately not bundled in this app. TREATMARK orders your ingredient statement by weight from the names you typed and prints the regulation’s own instruction beside it, along with a line asking you to verify each name against the current official definition. That is an honest limit, stated rather than papered over.
  • Which states are verified. Texas, Oklahoma and Colorado carry a citation and a fetch date in the app, taken from the state’s own published rules — Texas Administrative Code Title 4 Chapter 63, Oklahoma Administrative Code 35:30-27-51 onward, and the Colorado Department of Agriculture’s pet-treat labelling guidance. Every other state falls back to the classic guaranteed analysis format and is flagged “not verified” on the screen and in Settings rather than being quietly presented as checked.
  • Two label formats, and why yours may differ from a neighbour’s. Texas is on the classic Guaranteed Analysis. Oklahoma adopted the Pet Nutrition Facts box effective 11 July 2025. TREATMARK renders whichever one the state you selected has actually adopted, and names the rule beside every row.
  • Where the calorie number comes from. The calorie statement uses the Modified Atwater factors the pet-food regulation prescribes — 3.5, 8.5 and 3.5 kcal per gram — not the human-food factors. Using 4, 9 and 4 instead overstates metabolizable energy by roughly 11 to 13 percent, which is a plausible-looking wrong number printed on a package.
  • What is free and what is Pro. One batch is free forever and nothing about it is cut down: the full name verdict with the rule and the citation that produced it, both label formats, the calculated carbohydrate, the calorie statement, the element checklist, the type-size band, dated label versions, registrations, the whole label as text and the whole register as JSON. Pro adds every batch after the first, the register across all of them with its renewal calendar, the tag exported as SVG at true print scale, and the multi-state comparison. Pro never buys a different answer — the solver is identical on both tiers, and your own data is never gated.
  • Subscription terms. TREATMARK Pro is $19.99 a year and opens with a 21-day free trial (yearly plan only, offered once per store account), or $3.99 a month with no trial, or $59.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next charge and the period you have paid for runs to its end.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted or hidden. Every batch you entered on Pro still opens, still solves, still shows its dated versions and its registrations, still exports as label text and as JSON, and still deletes — including registers far longer than the free tier would let you build today. What stops is adding a second new batch, the renewal calendar, SVG export and the multi-state comparison. A lapse lowers a ceiling on new work; it never takes a record away.
  • It refused to draw my panel. That is the instrument declining rather than guessing. If the dietary fibre you entered exceeds the total carbohydrate it is declared as a component of, the Pet Nutrition Facts panel cannot be honest, so TREATMARK refuses it and names the reason and the rule instead of printing an impossible box.
  • Prove it. The app carries the state-published worked label and its own case set on the handset. Open Prove it and TREATMARK re-solves them in front of you and shows its answers against the known ones, so you can check the instrument rather than trust it.
  • Printing at true scale. The SVG tag is written at the panel dimensions you entered, in real inches. Print at 100% scale with page fitting off and measure the panel before it goes to a printer as artwork.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.
