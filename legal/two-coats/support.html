Support

Last updated 2026-10-10

Need help with Two Coats? You're in the right place. BlackRaven AI LLC makes Two Coats, and we read every message.

Contact us

Email support@blackravenai.com and we'll help. We aim to reply within 2–3 business days. To speed things up, tell us your device and OS version and describe what happened.

Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What is free and what is Pro. Free: one project with unlimited rooms measured by quick dimensions or wall by wall, paint estimates and the shopping list. Two Coats Pro adds LiDAR room scanning, unlimited projects, flooring and trim estimates, cost tracking, branded PDF estimates and saved product presets.
  • Subscription terms. Two Coats Pro is $19.99 per year, $4.99 per month or $39.99 once for lifetime access, in US dollars; local prices may differ. The yearly plan opens with a one-week free trial, offered once per Apple account; if you don't cancel before it ends, it becomes a paid yearly subscription. Subscriptions renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel any time in iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions (or from the app's Settings). Cancelling stops the next charge; the period you paid for runs to its end. The lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews and has nothing to cancel.
  • Are the quantities exact? They are estimates from your measurements and the coverage and waste figures shown in the app, which you can edit. Paint coverage varies with the product and surface; check the label and buy from one batch. Prices are the ones you enter, not live store prices.
  • Which phones can scan? LiDAR scanning needs an iPhone or iPad with a LiDAR sensor (Pro models). Every other device can measure by hand.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.
