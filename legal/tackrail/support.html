Support

Last updated 2026-08-28

Need help with TACKRAIL? You're in the right place. BlackRaven AI LLC makes TACKRAIL, and we read every message.

Contact us

Email support@blackravenai.com and we'll help. We aim to reply within 2–3 business days. To speed things up, tell us your device and OS version and describe what happened.

Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What is free and what is Pro. The whole solver on straight-repeat goods — vertical and horizontal repeat, per-panel centring, match groups, the verifier, the drawn bolt, the cut sheet, the 12 starter frames and full JSON backup — is free forever, with 2 saved cuts. Pro adds half-drop and quarter-drop repeats, the nap lock, unlimited cuts, your own frames, the frame book’s correction, CSV and SVG export and the full ledger with its waste trend.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted or locked. Every cut, frame and ledger row stays readable, exportable and deletable; the free limits simply come back.
  • The yardage looks high. Each matched or centred panel must reserve a whole pattern repeat in height, so a large repeat costs real cloth. Record the actual yards you use and the frame book corrects the estimate for that frame after three cuts.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.
