Support

Last updated 2026-09-25

Need help with Looseleaf? You're in the right place. BlackRaven AI LLC makes Looseleaf, and we read every message.

Contact us

Email support@blackravenai.com and we'll help. We aim to reply within 2–3 business days. To speed things up, tell us your device and OS version and describe what happened.

Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • Where are my songs stored? Only on this device. Use Settings → Export backup to save a copy you can keep or move to a new phone, and Settings → Restore from backup to bring it back.
  • The microphone will not record. Allow microphone access for Looseleaf in your phone’s Settings, then try again. Recordings never leave your device.
  • Where do ads appear? In the free version, only on the Binder (your song list) and occasionally when you close a song — never while you are writing. Looseleaf Pro removes all ads.
  • Restore Pro. Settings → Restore purchases, signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account you bought with.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.
