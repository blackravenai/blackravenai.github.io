Support

Last updated September 27, 2026 — version 1.0

Need help with Wax & Wane? Email support@blackravenai.com — a person reads every message. Please don’t send journal entries; we don’t need them to help.

Where is my journal stored?

Only on your device. There is no account and no copy on our servers — which is why we can’t read it, and also why we can’t restore it for you.

How do I back up or move to a new phone?

Your device’s own backup (iCloud or Google) may include the app’s data. You can also open the Study (the sun-shaped Settings button on Tonight) → Export everything, and keep the file somewhere safe.

How do I delete everything?

Study → Hold to erase everything. It is permanent. Deleting the app also removes its data from your device.

I don’t know my birth time.

That’s common. Choose “I don’t know my birth time” and your chart is read by the sun (without a rising sign or houses). You can add the time later in Study → Birth details.

What’s free, and what is the Almanac?

Unlimited pages, the nightly rite, mood check-ins, the daily reading, a daily card, your chart and placements, rituals, Kindred and more are free. The Almanac adds the full Year Ahead, deeper readings, pattern insights, aspect explanations, unlimited sealed letters and two rare seal finishes. See the Terms for prices.

How do I restore or cancel a purchase?

Restore: Study → Restore purchases (or Restore on the Almanac page). Cancel: on iPhone, Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Wax & Wane. On Android, Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. Uninstalling doesn’t cancel.

How do I add the widgets?

Touch and hold your Home Screen → Edit → Add Widget → Wax & Wane. Open the app once first so the widget has tonight’s sky. Lock Screen widgets are added from the Lock Screen’s Customize screen.

What happens if I miss a night?

Nothing bad. Two Cloud Covers hold your place each lunar cycle, your moth just dozes, and Sabbatical (in the Study) pauses everything.

How does Kindred work?

Chart → Pair a sky → Send an invitation. The link carries your name in the app and your planetary positions (not your birth date, time or place). Positions can hint at a birth date, so send it only to people you trust.

The nightly note isn’t arriving.

Check that it’s on in the Study and that notifications are allowed for Wax & Wane in your device settings. Notes pause during Sabbatical.

Is this fortune telling?

No. Wax & Wane is a journal that uses the sky as a mirror. Nothing in it predicts events.
