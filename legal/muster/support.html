Need help with MUSTER? You're in the right place. BlackRaven AI LLC makes MUSTER, and we read every message.

Contact us

Email support@blackravenai.com and we'll help. We aim to reply within 2–3 business days. To speed things up, tell us your device and OS version and describe what happened.

Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What is free and what is Pro. The full exact solve, the muster sheet and every export are free forever, on every tier, with 10 items on the rack, 1 booked visit and the last 12 months of the book on screen. Pro adds unlimited kit, the whole book with no window, the gap audit and its CSV, and unlimited booked visits. Pro never buys a better answer.
  • Subscription terms. Pro is $39.99 a year with a 21-day free trial (yearly plan only, offered once per customer), or $6.99 a month with no trial, or $79.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next charge and the period you have paid for runs to its end.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted or hidden. Every item and every stamped visit stays exactly where it is, stays readable and stays exportable, including records outside the 12-month book window. A lapse lowers a ceiling on new records; it never takes a record away.
  • What MUSTER does, and does not do. MUSTER records the dates and intervals you enter and works out when they fall due and which visit reaches them. It does not examine equipment, issue or hold certificates, judge whether an item is fit for use, or state what any regulation requires of you. Those remain matters for you and your examiner.
  • Where the intervals come from. You supply every interval and grace period yourself. MUSTER ships none, deliberately — the schedule your equipment is on is yours and your examiner’s to set, not ours.
  • About the plan. Each solving round is exact against the dates you entered, and the plan across the year is a rolling policy — the fewest visits we can find that keep every item in date. Stamping a visit re-solves the rest of the year.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.

Sources & attribution

MUSTER’s round solver is an exact dynamic program over candidate visit dates, checked against brute-force enumeration of every set partition; the method and every constant are cited line by line in the app’s design record. Date arithmetic is ISO-8601 day arithmetic with month-end clamping.
