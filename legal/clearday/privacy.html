Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-10

Clearday is a debt payoff planner made by BlackRaven AI LLC: enter your debts and it shows your debt-free date and the fastest, cheapest order to pay them off. It runs entirely on your device. There is no account, no sign-in and no bank linking, and we designed it to need as little of your information as possible.

The short version

  • No account, no login, no name or email required.
  • Your data is saved on your device, not on our servers.
  • We don't run our own analytics or tracking.
  • Clearday has no server of its own and never connects to your bank. Every payoff plan is computed on the phone and works offline. The only network activity is Apple's own: when you buy, restore or check Pro, the App Store and StoreKit on your device talk to Apple. No purchase data, and nothing you entered, is sent to us or to any third party.
  • Clearday shows no ads. Purchases are made through Apple's App Store directly; there is no third-party purchase or subscription service.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or photos. We have no user accounts and no server-side profile of you. The only time we see your email is if you choose to email our support address.

Data stored on your device

The debts you enter (name, type, balance, interest rate, minimum payment, due day and any promotional rate), the payments and lump sums you log, your strategy, extra budget, reminder settings and app settings are stored locally on your device in the app's private database. A small summary is shared with Clearday's own widgets through an App Group on the same device. Payment reminders are scheduled locally by your phone. Plans you export as CSV or PDF go only where you send them. None of it is uploaded or transmitted by the app, and deleting the app deletes it. Financial information you enter is never shared, sold or used for advertising. Like any app data, it is included in your device's own iCloud or computer backup if you have those turned on; that backup is handled by Apple under your account, not by us.

Advertising

Clearday does not show ads and does not include any advertising SDK.

Purchases

Clearday's in-app purchases and subscriptions are sold and processed entirely by Apple through the App Store, using Apple's StoreKit on your device. We never see or store your payment details, and no third-party purchase service is involved: the app asks Apple's StoreKit on your device whether you own Pro, and Apple signs that answer. We receive only the aggregated, anonymous sales reports Apple gives every developer. See the Apple Privacy Policy.

Analytics

Clearday does not include our own third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. Any limited, aggregated diagnostics you receive come from the app stores' standard reporting (for example crash counts), which is governed by Apple's and Google's policies, not ours.

Children

Clearday is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will address it.

Your choices and rights

Because we don't hold a personal profile of you, most control lives on your device: you can clear the app's local data. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA over data our purchase partners process; you can exercise those directly with those partners (linked above) or contact us and we'll help point you to the right place.

International users

BlackRaven AI LLC operates the app and support from Wyoming, United States. Our purchase partners may process data in other countries under their own safeguards.

Changes to this policy
