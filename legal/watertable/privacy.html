Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-09-04

WATERTABLE is a siding course-layout tool for siding carpenters, made by BlackRaven AI LLC. It runs entirely on your device. There is no account and no sign-in, and we designed it to need as little of your information as possible.

The short version

  • No account, no login, no name or email required.
  • Your data is saved on your device, not on our servers.
  • We don't run our own analytics or tracking.
  • WATERTABLE has no server of its own. It searches the exposure, solves the courses against your window heads, sills and door heads, carries the scheme round the corners, draws the pole, writes the story pole sheet and the CSV, and re-runs its own proof cases on the device, and it works with the network switched off. The only exception is buying or restoring a subscription: that is handled by Apple’s or Google’s own purchase system, and the receipt it returns is checked by our subscription provider (RevenueCat) so the app knows whether Pro is active. That check carries an anonymous store purchase identifier and the receipt — never your houses, your walls, your openings, your notes or anything you typed.
  • WATERTABLE shows no ads. Store versions may offer purchases (via RevenueCat and the app store); those partners have their own privacy policies, linked below.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or photos. We have no user accounts and no server-side profile of you. The only time we see your email is if you choose to email our support address.

Data stored on your device

Your houses — the name and notes you type, every wall with its height from the water table to the frieze, every opening with its sill and head, the corners you grouped, the siding product and the exposure band you entered with the source you cited for it, your deviation cap, the mode and corner rule you chose, and whether the house has been marked hung — together with the landing record accrued across your houses and your app settings, are stored locally on your device, in the app’s local storage under the key `watertable.save.v1`. Course plans are never stored: they are solved again from your figures every time you open a wall. You can export any of it — or all of it — as a story pole sheet you can copy, a CSV of every course line, or a complete JSON backup, at any time, on any tier, including after a subscription lapses. None of it is uploaded or transmitted by the app, and deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising

WATERTABLE does not show ads and does not include any advertising SDK.

Purchases

If WATERTABLE offers in-app purchases, the transaction is handled by the app store (Apple App Store or Google Play) and processed for us by RevenueCat, which manages entitlements and receipts. We never see or store your payment card details. RevenueCat may process a purchase/receipt identifier to unlock what you bought and restore it across your devices. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy, the Apple Privacy Policy, and the Google Privacy Policy.

Analytics

WATERTABLE does not include our own third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. Any limited, aggregated diagnostics you receive come from the app stores' standard reporting (for example crash counts), which is governed by Apple's and Google's policies, not ours.

Children

WATERTABLE is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will address it.

Your choices and rights

Because we don't hold a personal profile of you, most control lives on your device: you can clear the app's local data. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA over data our purchase partners process; you can exercise those directly with those partners (linked above) or contact us and we'll help point you to the right place.

International users

BlackRaven AI LLC operates the app and support from Wyoming, United States. Our purchase partners may process data in other countries under their own safeguards.
