Support

Need help with Wisp? You're in the right place. BlackRaven AI LLC makes Wisp, and we read every message.

Contact us

Email support@blackravenai.com and we'll help. We aim to reply within 2–3 business days. To speed things up, tell us your device and OS version and describe what happened.

Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What is free and what is Wisp Plus. Free forever: Calm now, both calm sessions (Settle a worry and Strengthen your calm), every way to follow (Watch, Eyes closed, Tap along), core breathing (box, 4-7-8, coherent), Ground, Journal, mood check-ins, crisis lines and JSON export. Wisp Plus adds the sleep wind-down, soundscapes, the calm-places library and 1-minute tap in, “What helps you” insights and 30/90-day mood views, the “Bring it to therapy” summary, extra light colors and tones, the physiological sigh and 10-minute breathing sessions.
  • Subscription terms. Wisp Plus is $29.99 a year, $4.99 a month, or $49.99 once for lifetime (US prices; the store shows your local price). The yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial, shown in the store before you confirm and offered once per account. Subscriptions renew automatically at the same price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends. Cancel any time — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.
  • My Plus lapsed. Nothing is deleted or hidden. Every mood, journal entry, session and calm place stays on the device and stays yours, and export keeps working; only the Plus extras lock again.
  • Wisp is not therapy. Wisp is a wellness companion for everyday stress. It is not therapy or medical care, and it does not diagnose or treat any condition. It never guides you through trauma memories on your own; for heavier or older material, please work with a qualified therapist. If you are in crisis in the US, call or text 988 or text HOME to 741741; elsewhere, find a line at findahelpline.com. If you are in danger, call your local emergency number.
  • Sound only in one ear, or out of sync with the light. Run the headphone ear check again in Settings to confirm left and right. If both ears sound the same, turn off Mono Audio in your phone’s accessibility settings. Bluetooth headphones delay sound; move the Bluetooth sync slider in Settings until each tone lands with the light.
  • The audio stops. Keep Wisp open during a session — the screen stays awake while a session runs so the sound does not cut out. Locking the phone or switching apps can pause it.
  • Restore purchases. Open Settings in Wisp and tap Restore purchases; your entitlement is re-checked with the store.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.

Sources & attribution

Wisp’s bilateral stimulation — alternating left-right visual, audio and tactile cues — is the same kind of stimulation EMDR therapists use for calming and resourcing. Wisp follows the conservative line on self-administered use: regulation and resourcing only (settling everyday stress, strengthening a calm place), never self-guided processing of traumatic memories. EMDR therapy itself should be delivered by a trained clinician. Breathing patterns (box, 4-7-8, coherent breathing, the physiological sigh), 5-4-3-2-1 grounding and the thought record are widely used self-care techniques. None of this replaces professional care.
