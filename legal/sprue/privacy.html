Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-09-06

SPRUE is a release-audit tool for people who author and sell multi-part 3D-print model kits, made by BlackRaven AI LLC. It runs entirely on your device. There is no account and no sign-in, and we designed it to need as little of your information as possible.

The short version

  • No account, no login, no name or email required.
  • Your data is saved on your device, not on our servers.
  • We don't run our own analytics or tracking.
  • SPRUE has no server of its own. It reads the release folder or ZIP you point it at — the STL and 3MF meshes, the README, the parts list, the hardware list and the step guide — parses every one of them, walks each mesh’s half-edge graph for watertightness, manifoldness, winding, shell count, bounding box and overhang, reconciles the documents against the files, and keeps your shelf of models and audits, all on the device. It works with the network switched off, and it makes no network request of any kind while it audits. There is no artificial-intelligence service behind it: nothing you open is uploaded, and an unreleased paid model never leaves your machine. The only exception is buying or restoring a subscription: that is handled by Apple’s or Google’s own purchase system, and the receipt it returns is checked by our subscription provider (RevenueCat) so the app knows whether Pro is active. That check carries an anonymous store purchase identifier and the receipt — never your name, your models, your files or your instructions.
  • SPRUE shows no ads. Store versions may offer purchases (via RevenueCat and the app store); those partners have their own privacy policies, linked below.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or photos. We have no user accounts and no server-side profile of you. The only time we see your email is if you choose to email our support address.

Data stored on your device

Your shelf — each model with its house defaults (bed size, units, naming convention), and each audit you have run on it with its label, the names and byte sizes of the files in that release, the summary counts, every finding with the working behind it, the mesh table and the coverage figures — is stored locally on your device, in the app’s local storage under the key sprue.save.v1. The contents of your mesh files are never stored: SPRUE parses them in memory and keeps only the measurements. You can export the whole shelf as JSON, and any report as plain text or CSV, on every tier including the free one, and import a JSON export back. None of it is uploaded or transmitted by the app, and deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising

SPRUE does not show ads and does not include any advertising SDK.

Purchases

If SPRUE offers in-app purchases, the transaction is handled by the app store (Apple App Store or Google Play) and processed for us by RevenueCat, which manages entitlements and receipts. We never see or store your payment card details. RevenueCat may process a purchase/receipt identifier to unlock what you bought and restore it across your devices. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy, the Apple Privacy Policy, and the Google Privacy Policy.

Analytics

SPRUE does not include our own third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. Any limited, aggregated diagnostics you receive come from the app stores' standard reporting (for example crash counts), which is governed by Apple's and Google's policies, not ours.

Children

SPRUE is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will address it.

Your choices and rights

Because we don't hold a personal profile of you, most control lives on your device: you can clear the app's local data. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA over data our purchase partners process; you can exercise those directly with those partners (linked above) or contact us and we'll help point you to the right place.

International users

BlackRaven AI LLC operates the app and support from Wyoming, United States. Our purchase partners may process data in other countries under their own safeguards.
