Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What SPRUE does, and what it does not decide. SPRUE reconciles a release against its own instructions: it tells you where the parts list, the step guide, the hardware list and the stated quantities disagree with the STL and 3MF files actually in the folder, and it reports each mesh’s topology against the published STL and 3MF definitions. It is not a slicer and it makes no claim about print success, strength, fitness or safety. There is no time estimate, no material estimate, no G-code and no support generation anywhere in the app. The overhang panel is a facet-angle summary in the orientation you supplied — a geometry statement, never a prediction.
  • Anything it could not read is counted on the face of the report. Every file and every instruction line SPRUE failed to parse is named and totalled as UNCHECKED on the report itself, never silently skipped. A pick with no mesh files, or with no instructions to reconcile against, is reported as unreconcilable rather than as clean.
  • Prove it. SPRUE’s claim is measured rather than asserted. Open the “Prove it” screen and the app re-runs ten bundled synthetic cases on your own handset, showing its findings beside the known answer. Off the phone, against a frozen synthetic corpus recorded before the engine existed — 360 kits, 607 injected defects across 16 types, 60 clean kits carrying deliberate traps — the engine finds 607 of 607, 100% on every one of the 16 types individually, and raises 0 false flags on the 60 clean kits. It agrees with the STL and 3MF specifications on 108 of 108 property verdicts, and parses 7,316 of 7,316 files and 3,906 of 3,920 instruction steps, the remaining 14 named as UNCHECKED. The null baseline recorded before any engine code existed — compare the file count to the part count and stop — finds 0 of 607 and raises 30 flags on those same 60 clean kits.
  • No third-party content, anywhere. Every case SPRUE ships was generated by this project’s own frozen generator. The app never bundles, reproduces or ships anyone else’s model, ZIP, README or instruction text.
  • What is free and what is Pro. The instrument is free forever: audit a release of any size, with all 16 finding classes and the full working behind each one, both mesh formats, all three document formats, folder, multi-file or ZIP intake, the sprue frame, the named UNCHECKED count, the “Prove it” screen, and export as text, CSV and full JSON plus JSON import, on every tier — your own records are never gated. Free keeps 1 model on the shelf with its last 3 audits listed and the diff between them. Pro adds unlimited models on the shelf, the whole audit history listed and diffable, comparison of any two non-consecutive versions, and Across the shelf: which finding classes keep coming back across every model and audit, and everything open right now. Pro never buys a better answer — the engine is the same engine on both tiers.
  • Subscription terms. SPRUE Pro is $39.99 a year opening with a 21-day free introductory offer (annual plan only, offered once per store account), or $7.99 a month with no trial, or $119.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted, hidden or truncated. Every model, every audit, every finding and every export stays on the device, stays readable and stays exportable, however many there are. What comes back is the free ceiling on the audit list: the record itself is never clipped, and no audit is ever deleted by a lapse.
  • Does it need a signal? No. Parsing, topology, reconciliation and export are all computed on your own device, so SPRUE opens, audits, draws the frame and exports with the radios off.
  • Check the release yourself before you publish it. SPRUE reports where your documents and your files disagree. It does not print your model, test your fit or approve your listing. The release is yours.
  • Restore purchases. Restore is on the Pro screen and in Settings; entitlements are re-checked with the store.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.

Sources & attribution

SPRUE’s engine was written against an answer key and a null baseline both recorded before any engine code existed. The generator that builds the corpus was frozen by SHA-256 before src/engine/ was written and may never be edited by the engine. Mesh validity is graded against two published definitions, cited with their terms in the app: the STL vertex-to-vertex and counter-clockwise rules, and the 3MF Core Specification v1.4.0 requirements that every triangle edge share common vertex endpoints with the edge of exactly one other triangle and that adjacent triangles carry the same outward face-normal orientation. Designers’ own public corrections were sought as a third evidence class; fewer than fifteen were obtainable because the marketplaces return HTTP 403 to an automated fetcher, so that class is reported and explicitly not used as a threshold. All 360 kits, 607 injected defects and 108 property fixtures are original synthetic output of this project.
