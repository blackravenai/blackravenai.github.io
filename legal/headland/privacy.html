Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-09-05

HEADLAND is a setting-out tool for people who lay out and drive trellised blocks — vineyards, hop yards and orchards — made by BlackRaven AI LLC. It runs entirely on your device. There is no account and no sign-in, and we designed it to need as little of your information as possible.

The short version

  • No account, no login, no name or email required.
  • Your data is saved on your device, not on our servers.
  • We don't run our own analytics or tracking.
  • HEADLAND has no server of its own. It takes the block outline, the row bearing and the spacings you enter, solves every row’s post run so that no line post lands on a vine, writes the chainage sheet, the plant schedule, the wire runs, the splices and the take-off, and exports your CSV and JSON — all of it on the device, and all of it with the network switched off. There is no artificial-intelligence service behind it, and nothing about a block of yours is sent anywhere to be read. The built app contains no network code at all: no fetch, no XMLHttpRequest, no beacon, no socket. The only exception is buying or restoring a subscription: that is handled by Apple’s or Google’s own purchase system, and the receipt it returns is checked by our subscription provider (RevenueCat) so the app knows whether Pro is active. That check carries an anonymous store purchase identifier and the receipt — never your name, your blocks, your client, your ground or anything you typed.
  • HEADLAND shows no ads. Store versions may offer purchases (via RevenueCat and the app store); those partners have their own privacy policies, linked below.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or photos. We have no user accounts and no server-side profile of you. The only time we see your email is if you choose to email our support address.

Data stored on your device

Your blocks — their names, the outline and row bearing you entered, the headlands and turn rows, every spacing and clearance in the spec, the solved plan cached against them, the as-driven records of what your crew actually drove against what was planned, whether the material has been ordered and your order note — together with your app settings and the last spec you used, are stored locally on your device, in the app’s local storage under the key headland.save.v2 (a headland.save.v1 file from an earlier version is read once, carried forward, and left in place as your own backup). You can export the setting-out sheet as CSV and the entire save as JSON at any time, on any tier, including after a Pro plan ends. None of it is uploaded or transmitted by the app, and deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising

HEADLAND does not show ads and does not include any advertising SDK.

Purchases

If HEADLAND offers in-app purchases, the transaction is handled by the app store (Apple App Store or Google Play) and processed for us by RevenueCat, which manages entitlements and receipts. We never see or store your payment card details. RevenueCat may process a purchase/receipt identifier to unlock what you bought and restore it across your devices. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy, the Apple Privacy Policy, and the Google Privacy Policy.

Analytics

HEADLAND does not include our own third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. Any limited, aggregated diagnostics you receive come from the app stores' standard reporting (for example crash counts), which is governed by Apple's and Google's policies, not ours.

Children

HEADLAND is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will address it.

Your choices and rights

Because we don't hold a personal profile of you, most control lives on your device: you can clear the app's local data. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA over data our purchase partners process; you can exercise those directly with those partners (linked above) or contact us and we'll help point you to the right place.

International users

BlackRaven AI LLC operates the app and support from Wyoming, United States. Our purchase partners may process data in other countries under their own safeguards.
