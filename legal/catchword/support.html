Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What is free and what is Pro. Every check runs at full strength on the free tier — one title, manuscripts up to 60 mapped pages, the whole ledger with the working printed under every finding, accept and un-accept, the candidate-omission worksheet, the UNCHECKED report, the correction sheet as .txt and .csv, shelf JSON export and import, the audit history already on your shelf, Prove It and every setting. Pro adds manuscripts of any length, unlimited titles, per-title synonym lists and the pass-to-pass repagination diff. Pro never buys a more accurate number: the engine is the same engine on both tiers.
  • Subscription terms. CATCHWORD Pro is $59.99 a year and opens with a 21-day free trial (annual plan only, offered once per store account), or $8.99 a month with no trial, or $179.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next charge and the period you have paid for runs to its end.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted or hidden. Every title, every recorded pass, every accepted finding and every synonym stays on the device, stays readable, stays exportable as text, CSV and shelf JSON, and stays deletable — including shelves longer than the free tier would let you build today. What stops is starting a new title beyond the first, auditing a manuscript longer than 60 mapped pages, editing synonyms and reading the pass-to-pass diff. A lapse changes what the app will do next; it never takes a record away.
  • What CATCHWORD checks. It reads two documents — a finished, paginated manuscript and a finished index as text — and compares them. Locator truth against the headword, its subentry terms, any synonym you declare and accepted inflected and inverted forms; dead entries; both ends of every range and holes inside it; every See and See also target, with circular and dead-end chains named; alphabetisation under the Chicago rule you declare, with the rule cited; duplicate and near-duplicate headwords; and locator format consistency.
  • The honest limit, stated in the app. An indexer indexes a discussion, and a discussion can continue onto a page that never repeats the term. CATCHWORD cannot know that. When it happens, declare a synonym for the title or accept the finding — both are remembered by fingerprint and both carry forward into every later pass. passim is accepted as written and its interior is not audited, though its endpoints are.
  • It never says an index is correct. The promise is exactly this: every locator the app could check, it checked, and here is the page. It does not write an index, it does not assert that a candidate omission is missing, and it makes no claim that any index is complete, correct, standards-compliant or fit to print. That judgement is yours.
  • It does not read page images. There is no OCR, on any tier. A page with no text layer is reported UNCHECKED with its folio rather than quietly skipped, and the unchecked count is printed beside the findings on the face of every report.
  • Which files it opens. A PDF with a text layer, a DOCX with real page breaks, or plain text with page marks. A DOCX with no hard page breaks cannot be paginated, and the app says so instead of inventing pages.
  • Prove it. The app carries ten complete manuscript-and-index pairs whose planted faults are known exactly. Open the Prove It screen and CATCHWORD re-runs them on your own handset and shows its answers against the known ones, so you can check the instrument rather than trust it.
  • Restore purchases. Tap Restore purchases on the plans sheet or in Settings; entitlements are re-checked with the store.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.

Sources & attribution

CATCHWORD is measured against a frozen answer key recorded before its engine existed: 44 synthetic manuscripts spanning 9,522 pages carrying 532 injected index errors across 14 kinds, plus 40 hand-authored correct indexes used as the false-flag corpus. It finds 532 of 532 injected errors (100.0%, and 100.0% on every one of the 14 kinds) and raises 0.397 false findings per 100 clean entries against a limit of 2. Its parser reads 28,711 of 28,711 index entries and maps 131,238 of 131,238 pages. Two deliberately weak baselines recorded before the engine — trusting the index outright, and a keyword-only ablation — reach 0.0% and 1.9% respectively, the latter at 24.46 false flags per 100 clean entries. Real published index errata are reported rather than scored, because only two were obtainable from the publisher errata pages read, which is below the project’s own 15-item floor for a threshold. Alphabetisation follows the letter-by-letter and word-by-word rules of the Chicago Manual of Style, cited rule by rule on screen. The generator is frozen by SHA-256 and re-hashed on every run, and every miss is reported rather than tuned away. No third-party book text is bundled in the app: every manuscript, index and specimen it ships is prose written for this project.
