Privacy Policy

Last updated September 22, 2026 — iOS release

Squeezy Fish is a single-player game/app made by BlackRaven AI LLC. It runs on your device. We designed it to need as little of your information as possible — there are no accounts and no sign-in.

The short version

  • No account, no login, no name or email required to play.
  • Your progress is saved on your device, not on our servers.
  • We don't run our own analytics or tracking.
  • The iOS release uses Google AdMob for ads and Apple's StoreKit for the optional monthly ad-removal subscription. Advertising and purchase services process data as described below.

Information we do not collect

No name, email, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or account registration is required to play. The game does not request access to those device resources. This is not a claim that our advertising or purchase services process no information. If you contact support, we receive the email address and information you choose to send. Please do not include a child's name, age, or other personal details in an ad report.

Data stored on your device

Scores, settings, earned bubbles, cosmetics, streaks, and a coarse player-age choice are stored locally in the app. We do not request a birth date or send that age choice to our own server. Use Change Player when someone else uses the device. Removing app data removes local game progress; it does not cancel an Apple subscription or delete records held by Apple or Google.

Advertising

The iOS release requests Google AdMob ads with child-directed and under-age treatment, a General content rating, and personalization disabled for all player-age choices. The app does not request Apple's tracking permission. Ad requests are blocked if Google's consent response does not permit them; children are not shown a form asking them to authorize data processing.

Child-directed and non-personalized settings do not mean that advertising processes no data. Google's SDK documentation describes potential processing of IP address (including approximate location), app/device identifiers, ad views and interactions, and diagnostic/performance information to serve, measure, secure, and troubleshoot ads. The fields and uses depend on Google's age/privacy treatment and applicable settings. We do not provide a name, email, birth date, or precise GPS location to the ad service.

Ad Privacy Options and Report an Ad are in the Reef Shop. External links and reporting controls are parent-gated for children and unknown ages. An active ad-removal subscription suppresses ad presentation; ad availability also depends on Google's service and approval.

See Google's Advertising / Privacy & Terms and how Google uses information from sites/apps that use its services.

Purchases

Apple processes the iOS Ad-Free Monthly subscription. The app uses Apple's verified subscription status to remove ads, restore purchases, and respond to changes such as refunds. We do not receive payment-card details. Purchases and restores are parent-gated for children and unknown ages; Apple's purchase authorization is separate from that gate.

The iOS release does not initialize the RevenueCat purchase SDK or create a RevenueCat customer identity on the device. Our existing Apple server-notification connection to RevenueCat remains configured for subscription events such as renewals, cancellations, and refunds; it is separate from the app's StoreKit purchase flow. See the Apple Privacy Policy and RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Analytics

The release does not start our optional growth analytics, attribution, or marketing SDKs. Apple provides store reporting, and Google processes advertising measurement and diagnostics described above. We do not describe an advertising-supported app as collecting no data.

Children

Squeezy Fish is intended for children and adults. A local age-range choice controls parent gates; it is not identity verification. The arithmetic gate is an access barrier, not verifiable parental consent to data collection. We do not offer child accounts, chat, or user uploads. Parents can contact support about data concerns, inappropriate ads, or deletion requests; please provide only what is necessary to identify the concern.

Your choices and rights

You can change the local player setting, remove local app data, manage subscriptions through your Apple account, and contact us about information processed in connection with the app. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing and to complain to a data-protection authority. Contact support@blackravenai.com to make a request; we will review the request and explain any information needed to process it. Apple's and Google's own privacy controls also apply to their services.

International users

BlackRaven AI LLC is a United States company. Our service providers may process information in other countries as described in their privacy policies. Local game data remains until removed from the device; service-provider records follow their applicable retention policies. Contact us about retention or deletion of support correspondence and subscription-related records.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, we will update this policy and its date. Publishing a policy update or continuing to use the app is not a substitute for consent where consent is required.
