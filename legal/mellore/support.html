Support

Last updated 2026-09-06

Need help with MELLORE? You're in the right place. BlackRaven AI LLC makes MELLORE, and we read every message.

Contact us

Email support@blackravenai.com and we'll help. We aim to reply within 2–3 business days. To speed things up, tell us your device and OS version and describe what happened.

Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What is free and what is Pro. All eleven checks, on every pattern, as often as you like, plus the full arithmetic behind every flag, THE LIGHTBOX, the Markdown audit report, the whole workroom as JSON, your house defaults and your own thread numbers, and 3 patterns on the workroom shelf with all of their versions — free forever. Pro adds an unlimited shelf, THE DIFF on a re-audit (cleared, still open, newly introduced) and THE RELEASE LEDGER.
  • Subscription terms. Pro is $6.99 a year with a 21-day free trial (annual only, once per account), or $1.49 a month with no trial, or $24.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically until you cancel; the lifetime purchase never renews. Cancel any time in your App Store or Google Play account under Subscriptions — cancelling stops the next charge and puts the free limits back.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted or locked. Every pattern, version, flag and report stays readable, exportable and deletable; the shelf simply stops taking new patterns past three. Adding a new version to a pattern already on the shelf is never blocked.
  • Flattened or scanned pages. MELLORE reads vector artwork and text. It never guesses at a raster image and it does not do OCR: if a page is a flattened bitmap it is reported UNCHECKED, with a count, on the face of the report. Silence is never mistaken for a pass.
  • Thread quantity. MELLORE does not estimate skeins or floss lengths. No citable per-stitch coverage figure exists for free-form hand embroidery, so the number is not computed rather than invented.
  • MELLORE audits the document, not the design. It reconciles a pattern against its own legend, stitch key, order of work and stated dimensions. It cannot tell you whether the design is good, and it is not a substitute for stitching your own proof when you want one.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.

Sources & attribution

MELLORE’s document reading is implemented against published specifications: the PDF 1.7 content-stream and filter grammar, the SVG 1.1 path grammar, and the DEFLATE format of RFC 1951 (implemented in pure JavaScript and round-tripped against zlib). Bezier extent solving and the millimetre arithmetic are standard closed-form geometry. No third-party pattern, template, legend or errata text is bundled or shipped with the app.
