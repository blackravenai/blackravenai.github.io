Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What WITNESS does, and does not do. It measures the width of the crack in your photograph, states the uncertainty on that width, and places it in a cited width category. It is a measurement aid and not a structural assessment. It does not diagnose a cause, judge whether a building is safe, recommend a repair, or tell you what any code requires. Those remain matters for you and a qualified engineer.
  • What is free and what is Pro. The whole measurement is free forever: unlimited readings, the width, the ± band, the resolving limit, the w/σ figure, the capture gate and its refusal reason, the cited category with its citation, the printed target card, the Prove-it run, one property with three cracks and every visit ever recorded on them — and all export, CSV, JSON and the annotated PNG. Pro adds the monitoring series (the dated ghost-rule ladder and the change read against that pair’s own measurement noise), every property you work, the re-measure queue, and the one-sheet ledger report. Pro never buys a better number — the engine is identical on both tiers.
  • Subscription terms. Witness Pro is $34.99 a year and opens with a 21-day free trial (yearly plan only, offered once per store account), or $5.99 a month with no trial, or $99.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next charge and the period you have paid for runs to its end.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted or hidden. Every property, every crack and every dated visit stays exactly where it is, stays readable, stays editable and stays exportable as CSV, JSON and annotated PNG. A lapse lowers a ceiling; it never takes a record away.
  • Why do I have to print a card? Because a photograph on its own has no scale. The printed target gives the app four marks of known separation in the same plane as the crack, which is what turns pixels into millimetres, keys the orientation, and — because the card’s own printed edges are known step edges — lets the app measure that photograph’s blur rather than assume it. Print it at 100 %, do not scale to fit, and check the printed ruler on it with a tape before the first job.
  • It refused to give me a number. That is the instrument declining to print a width it cannot defend. The capture gate names what to change — the target not found or not flat with the crack, too much motion, too little resolution, the crack narrower than the photograph can resolve. Re-shoot closer, squarer and steadier.
  • What the ± band means. Every width carries a band propagated from the profile fit, the scale field across the frame, the recovered focal length and the card’s stand-off from the wall. On the app’s frozen test set the band covered the true width 94.2 % of the time, against a target of 90 %.
  • Why will it not tell me a crack changed? Change is only called when the crack is at least three times the width of that photograph’s own blur — the w/σ figure printed on every capture. Below that line the measured drift is larger than the changes worth reporting, so WITNESS says so plainly instead of calling a change it cannot stand behind. A sharp close capture clears the line at 0.25 mm; a soft distant one fails it at 0.6 mm.
  • Where do the categories and intervals come from? The width categories and the tolerable widths are cited on screen to their published sources, and the suggested re-measure interval is attributed to the practitioner it came from and is yours to change. WITNESS does not invent an interval and does not reproduce anyone’s prose — it cites the values and writes its own wording.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.
