Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What HORNBACK does, and what it does not decide. You name the skin the way your invoice named it, say what form it is in, where it came from and what paperwork you hold, and where it is going. HORNBACK resolves that trade name against a transcribed taxon list, reads the transcribed federal rules and the destination state’s own row, and returns one outcome with the sentence that decides it quoted beside its citation and the date that copy was read, plus the wording your listing may carry and the names struck out of it. It does not give legal advice and it does not tell you what you may do. It does not decide your case, does not speak for any agency, evaluates no permit and reads no foreign country’s export statute. It identifies nothing from a photograph and has no camera feature at all: the species, the origin and the documentation are your own entries. The U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service and the destination jurisdiction govern; the app does not.
  • Where the rules come from, and how stale they can be. Twenty-three sections of 50 CFR taken from the eCFR at a single stated issue date, 16 U.S.C. 3372(a) and 16 U.S.C. 1538, and twelve state rows across nine states, each read from an official legislature site with its verbatim operative sentence and the date it was read. Two rows carry published federal-court injunctions, which the app attaches as dated, cautioned notes rather than folding into an outcome, because dockets move. Every screen and every export carries the citation and the read date, so you can always see how old the copy is. Rules change between our releases. Verify against the current rule before you publish a listing or tape a parcel.
  • HORNBACK holds no CITES data, deliberately. The Species+ database published by UNEP-WCMC and the CITES Secretariat is excellent at what it does, and it is free on both stores. Its Terms of Use forbid commercial use of the material or of any work derived from it, so a paid app may not carry it, and HORNBACK carries none of it — not bundled, not in the development harness, not as a private test set. Every cross-border case returns a first-class REFER citing 50 CFR 23.91(a), names Species+ on screen, and sends you to the live database to check the appendix yourself.
  • Why some answers are UNKNOWN, AMBIGUOUS or a population question. Many trade names cover more than one animal. When a typed name maps to more than one taxon, HORNBACK returns the candidates with the strictest candidate outcome rather than guessing, because guessing the taxon silently changes the answer. A name it does not recognise returns UNKNOWN. An untagged or unknown origin returns the strictest cited outcome, never an assumption in your favour. And twenty of the ninety-five taxa are reached only by a narrower listing that covers particular populations — ostrich, kangaroo, bison and deer among them — so those return a cited population question rather than a prohibition, because taking the strictest match flatly would be wrong.
  • What is free and what is Pro. The whole solver is free for ever, at full accuracy: every axis, every outcome, every citation and every quoted provision, the words for your listing and the names struck from it, the Rules screen with all its rows, the Prove-it screen, five skins in the Book, and CSV and JSON export of everything you entered. Pro adds an unlimited Skin Book, the Changed queue that re-solves your whole book when a row moves and lists the records whose outcome changed with the before and the after, one-tap re-solve that appends to a record’s history rather than overwriting it, and your own dated edits to a shipped rule row. Pro never buys a different outcome. The answer, the citations and the quoted text are identical on both tiers, and a refusal is never held back at any tier.
  • Subscription terms. HORNBACK Pro is $27.99 a year and opens with a 21-day free trial (annual plan only, offered once per store account), or $4.99 a month with no trial, or $74.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next charge and the period you have paid for runs to its end. We cannot cancel it for you, and the app will not ask you to stay.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted or hidden. Every skin already in the Book stays readable, editable, deletable and exportable in full by both CSV and JSON — including the records filed past the free five, and including the citations they were decided with. Dated row edits you already made stay in force. A lapse lowers a ceiling on new records and on making a new row edit; it never takes a record away.
  • The tables are yours to correct. Every rule row ships as an editable, dated, cited default with a line telling you to verify it against the current rule. When you turn a row off in your copy, your copy decides your outcomes from then on, and the date and note you wrote travel with it into every future solve and every export.
  • Prove it. Open the Prove-it screen and HORNBACK re-runs cases from the development answer key on your own handset against the same engine, showing the expected outcome beside the measured one, so you can check the instrument instead of trusting it. Nothing is fetched and nothing is cached.
  • Does it need a signal? No. Every resolution, membership test and citation lookup is arithmetic over bundled tables, so the app opens, decides, records and exports with the network switched off — which is also why nothing about your stock ever leaves the handset.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.

Sources & attribution

HORNBACK’s rule tables, its layered solver and its listing wording are implemented directly from the published texts they cite — twenty-three sections of 50 CFR (including 50 CFR 10.13, 14.91, 17.11, 17.42 and 23.91) taken from the eCFR at a single stated issue date, together with 16 U.S.C. 3372(a) and 16 U.S.C. 1538, and the statutes of nine US states — all reproduced under 17 U.S.C. § 105 and Georgia v. Public.Resource.Org, Inc., 590 U.S. 255 (2020). Two published federal-court injunctions are carried as dated rows citing the reported decisions. No licensed third-party dataset is used anywhere in the app: in particular, the Species+ / CITES database is named on screen and linked, and nothing is computed from it or bundled with it. The engine was measured against an answer key frozen before it existed: 39,520 enumerated cases decided twice, once by the shipped engine and once by an independent derivation sharing no code with it, agreeing on the outcome and the citation set in 39,520 of 39,520; 18 of 18 published worked examples at 50 CFR 14.91(c); 34 of 34 regulator-stated decisions; 1,164 of 1,164 membership and operative-text byte-checks; 176 of 176 trade names resolved or returned as ambiguous; and zero violations across ten asserted properties. A null baseline recorded before the engine existed — assume the skin is fine because the invoice named it — is wrong on 70.76% of those cases. Every table, threshold and read date is cited line by line in the app’s design record and answer key.
