Frequently asked

"Upload to my board" doesn't appear / won't flash

Flashing a real Arduino straight from Circuit Quest uses WebSerial, which only exists in desktop Chrome or Edge. On iOS, Android, Safari and Firefox, Circuit Quest shows the honest fallback instead: copy the sketch and upload it with the free Arduino IDE.

If flashing fails on desktop: check the USB cable is fully seated (some cables are charge-only), close the Arduino IDE or any serial monitor holding the port, then unplug the board and plug it back in.

Where is my data? Is there an account?

No account. Everything — circuits, progress, settings — lives on your device. That also means uninstalling the app (or clearing browser data on the web) erases it, so export builds you care about first: any project → Share → Build file.

How do I share a circuit with a friend?

Open the build → Share. Send the file, copy the link, or let them scan the QR code — they import it from the Builds page and get their own editable copy, checked by the same safety analyzer.

Why does Circuit Quest refuse to flash my circuit?

If the checker found something that could damage parts once real current flows, Circuit Quest blocks flashing and tells you exactly what to fix — usually one resistor away. You can override it, but the warning exists because the failure it describes is real.

The simulation and my real build behave differently

Step back through the build instructions (the overview lists every step) and run the "Check it before you power it" list again — nine times out of ten a wire is one row off or an LED is backwards. If you're sure the app is wrong, email us the build file; those reports make Circuit Quest better for everyone.

What is Circuit Quest Plus?

Circuit Quest Plus is Circuit Quest's one optional in-app purchase: an auto-renewing subscription (monthly or yearly, with a 7-day free trial where the store offers it) that unlocks the Missions builds (lasers, robots, radar and mad science), plus new "Plus drop" builds every month and the community Showcase builds. Everything else — the Starter Path, the designer and simulator, every other template, and your own builds — is free. Each week's featured mission (the weekly drop) is free for everyone. Payment is processed by Apple or Google; Circuit Quest checks whether Plus is active through RevenueCat using an anonymous app user ID — no account and no personal information.

The Monthly Showcase — how do I submit?

Open one of your own builds (or the Showcase screen) → Submit to the Showcase. Circuit Quest opens your email app with your build link and the details you entered; nothing is sent until you press send. We pick 1–3 designs to feature each month. Use a nickname, not your full name. To report a featured design or have yours removed, use "Report a problem with this design" or email us.

Restoring, cancelling and refunds

Plus belongs to your store account (Apple ID or Google account). On a new device, open Settings → Circuit Quest Plus → Restore purchase while signed in to the same store account. Cancel any time in your Apple ID subscription settings (or Google Play → Subscriptions) at least 24 hours before renewal. If Plus ends, the mission templates lock again — but nothing is deleted: copies you made of a mission stay in My Builds and keep working. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google through their own processes (Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com; Google Play: your order history).

Reporting a bug

The perfect bug report: the build file (Share → Download file), your device and browser/app version, and the steps to make it happen. If the app ever shows its "That address doesn't match anything" screen or a blank page, the URL you were on is the clue — send it along.
