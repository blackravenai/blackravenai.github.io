Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-11-01

Circuit Quest is an electronics design, simulation and learning app made by BlackRaven AI LLC. It runs on your device, and it was built to need as little of your information as possible: there are no accounts, no sign-in and no ads. Usage analytics is optional.

The short version

  • No account, no login — we never ask for your name, email, or anything else.
  • Your circuits, progress and settings are saved on your device, not on our servers. Purchase records and optional usage analytics are processed separately as described below.
  • No ads. No advertising SDKs, no advertising identifiers.
  • Optional usage analytics. PostHog is off until you opt in. No cross-app advertising tracking.
  • One optional subscription. Circuit Quest Plus is sold through Apple or Google; its status is checked via RevenueCat using an anonymous app user ID — no Circuit Quest account required.

Information we do not collect

The native app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts or browsing history. It has no user account. Anonymous purchase identifiers and, if enabled, usage events are processed as described below; these may qualify as personal data under applicable law. The only time we see your email address is if you choose to write to our support address.

Data stored on your device

Everything Circuit Quest saves lives locally: your circuit designs and wiring, learn-path progress, XP and trophies, your parts inventory, and app settings. On the web version this uses your browser's local storage; in the iOS and Android apps it uses the app's own storage. None of it is transmitted to us. You can erase all of it at any time with the reset option in Settings, by clearing the app's data, or by uninstalling the app.

Camera and photos

Circuit Quest only uses the camera or your photo library when you tap to attach a reference photo of an object you're building around. In a project's measured objects, a small, downscaled thumbnail is saved with that project on your device; in the Fabricate measuring tool the photo is shown on screen only and never saved. Photos are never uploaded or sent to us. You can remove a photo at any time.

Sharing circuits

When you share a build file, the circuit is included in that file. The recipient gets their own local copy. Web sharing links and QR codes are disabled in this iOS release. Any service you choose to send a file through processes it under its own privacy policy.

Purchases

Circuit Quest offers one optional in-app purchase: Circuit Quest Plus, an auto-renewing subscription (monthly or yearly, with a free trial when the store offers one) that unlocks the advanced Missions templates and the included bonus and Showcase builds. Everything else in Circuit Quest is free. Payment is processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) — we never see or store your card details, name, or email address.

To know whether Circuit Quest Plus is active on your device, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), a purchase-management service. RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous app user ID that the app generates (it is not linked to your name, email, or any account), the purchase and subscription records the store provides for that ID, and basic technical details needed to talk to the store (such as app version, OS version, and store country). It does not receive your circuits, progress, or anything else you make. RevenueCat's handling of this data is described in its privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy. If you never open the Plus screen or subscribe, Circuit Quest still checks the store once at launch on the iOS and Android apps so a subscription you already own is recognised; on the web there is no store connection at all.

We use these records to unlock Plus, restore it on devices signed in to the same store account, understand subscription performance and handle support questions. We do not use them for advertising or to profile you.

Included curated content

This iOS release reads the curated bonus and Showcase content bundled with the app. Remote content downloads are disabled while the content website is unavailable.

Showcase submissions

You can submit a design you made to the Monthly Showcase. Circuit Quest never sends it for you: it opens your own email app (or share sheet) with a message to support@blackravenai.com containing only what you typed — an optional display name, a title and a note — and the build itself as a link. We use submissions only to consider and, if chosen, feature the design with the display name you gave, after you ticked the consent box. Please use a nickname rather than your full name, especially if you are under 18. To have a submission or featured design removed, email us and we will remove it from the feed.

Analytics

Optional PostHog usage analytics is described below. We also receive aggregated statistics (such as crash counts or install numbers) from the app stores' standard developer reporting, which is governed by Apple's and Google's own policies.

Children

Circuit Quest is made to be safe for curious people of every age, including children. Optional analytics must not be enabled by children without appropriate parent or guardian involvement. There are no ads, no social features, no chat with other users, and nothing a child creates ever leaves the device unless they deliberately share a circuit file. If you believe a child has somehow sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

You can reset local progress in Settings, clear app data or uninstall, and turn optional analytics off in Settings. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete data under applicable privacy laws. Contact support@blackravenai.com for help with purchase or analytics records; we may need information to locate records associated with an anonymous identifier. Uninstalling does not itself cancel a subscription or delete records held by purchase or analytics providers.

International users

BlackRaven AI LLC operates the app and support from Wyoming, United States. Your circuit content stays on your device unless you deliberately share it. RevenueCat purchase processing and optional PostHog analytics may involve processing in the United States; see the provider policies linked on this page.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy we will change the date at the top and post the new version at this URL. Meaningful changes will be called out in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions or requests? Email support@blackravenai.com.

Optional usage analytics

If you choose Allow analytics, PostHog receives basic events such as first analytics-enabled launch, onboarding completion, feature completion, paywall views and successful purchases, together with a random analytics identifier, app version and platform. This helps us understand app usage and campaign results. We do not send names, builds, circuit content, photos or anything else you type or create. Automatic interaction capture and session recording are disabled. Analytics is off until you opt in; change your choice through Usage analytics in settings. Declining does not limit the app.

PostHog processes these events in our US-hosted project. We disable IP-based location enrichment and do not use analytics for cross-app advertising tracking. Turning analytics off stops future collection; previously collected events remain subject to retention and deletion policies. Contact support@blackravenai.com about data requests. More information: PostHog privacy policy.
