What Circuit Quest is

Circuit Quest is an educational tool for designing, simulating, and learning to build hobby electronics around the Arduino Uno. Its safety checks, simulations, numbers, and build instructions are teaching aids — carefully sourced and tested, but not a substitute for component datasheets, product manuals, or your own judgment.

Real electronics are real

Read this part twice

Circuit Quest helps you build physical circuits with real electrical current. Even at hobby voltages, mistakes can damage your Arduino, your components, other property — or, with batteries, hot parts, and soldering irons involved, hurt you.

  • You build real-world circuits at your own risk. Circuit Quest's checks reduce risk; they cannot eliminate it, and they only know about what you told the app.
  • Work only with low-voltage projects of the kind Circuit Quest shows. Never connect anything to mains/wall power.
  • Verify parts against their official datasheets before powering anything you care about.
  • Children should build with adult supervision.
  • Unplug power before rewiring. If something smells hot, disconnect first, debug second.
