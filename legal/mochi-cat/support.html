Support

Last updated 2026-09-27

Need help with Mochi Cat? You're in the right place. BlackRaven AI LLC makes Mochi Cat, and we read every message.

Contact us

Email support@blackravenai.com and we'll help. We aim to reply within 2–3 business days. To speed things up, tell us your device and OS version and describe what happened.

Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What is free and what is Premium. Free forever, on every cat in the house: the clicker and the full session player, Foundations plus Sit, Come and Happy Carrier, the Kitty Diary, the litter and appetite watch, the treat budget, “My cat ate…”, the Lily check and every safety tool, Lost Cat Mode, Speak Cat, and up to 10 records per cat. Premium adds the full Trick Tree, the whole Little Academy, unlimited Kitty Papers, and the weekly Purrdate report card. Safety tools are never behind Premium, and anything that ships free stays free.
  • Subscription terms. Premium is $79.99 a year or $14.99 a month (US prices; the store shows your local price). Any free trial is an introductory offer on the yearly plan, shown in the store before you confirm and offered once per account; the app reminds you two days before a trial ends. Subscriptions renew automatically at the same price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends. Cancel any time — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions.
  • My Premium lapsed. Nothing is deleted or hidden. Every cat, diary entry, record and bit of training progress stays on the device and stays yours; only the Premium lessons and extras lock again.
  • “My cat ate…” is not veterinary care. It is educational information that tells you how worried to be and who to call. No app can examine your cat. If your cat ate or touched something that might be harmful — any part of a lily, a dog flea treatment, a human painkiller, string — call your veterinarian, an emergency clinic, or a pet poison helpline right away. Mochi Cat never tells you to make a cat vomit at home.
  • Training methods. Every lesson is force-free and reward-based, with short sessions the cat can always walk away from. If your cat shows aggression, fear that does not ease, a change in litter habits or a sudden change in behaviour, see your vet first.
  • Lost Cat Mode. The flyer is made on your phone from the details you enter. Nothing is posted anywhere unless you share it.
  • Restore purchases. Open My Cat, then tap Restore purchase; your entitlement is re-checked with the store.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.

Sources & attribution

Mochi Cat’s training curriculum follows force-free, reward-based methods consistent with the American Veterinary Society of Animal Behavior’s 2021 position statement on humane training and the AAFP/ISFM Cat Friendly Handling guidelines. Its toxin guidance is educational and conservative by design — any uncertainty rounds up to “call now” — and every urgent result directs you to a veterinarian or a pet poison helpline. Treat-budget arithmetic uses the standard resting-energy formula with cat life-stage factors. None of this replaces your veterinarian.
