Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-07-08

RUMPUS! is a single-player game/app made by BlackRaven AI LLC. It runs on your device. We designed it to need as little of your information as possible — there are no accounts and no sign-in.

The short version

  • No account, no login, no name or email required to play.
  • Your progress is saved on your device, not on our servers.
  • We don't run our own analytics or tracking.
  • Store versions may show ads (Google AdMob) and offer purchases (via RevenueCat and the app store). Those partners have their own privacy policies, linked below.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or photos. We have no user accounts and no server-side profile of you. The only time we see your email is if you choose to email our support address.

Data stored on your device

Your game/app progress — things like best scores, settings, in-app currency, owned items, and streaks — is stored locally on your device (for example in the app's local storage or the operating system's app storage). This data stays on your device, is not transmitted to us, and can be removed at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Advertising

App-store versions of RUMPUS! may display ads served by Google AdMob. To show ads, AdMob and its partners may access device and usage information — including a mobile advertising identifier — to deliver and measure ads and, where permitted, to personalize them. On iOS, personalized ads are shown only if you allow tracking; otherwise you'll see non-personalized ads. You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings.

See Google's Advertising / Privacy & Terms and how Google uses information from sites/apps that use its services.

Purchases

If RUMPUS! offers in-app purchases, the transaction is handled by the app store (Apple App Store or Google Play) and processed for us by RevenueCat, which manages entitlements and receipts. We never see or store your payment card details. RevenueCat may process a purchase/receipt identifier to unlock what you bought and restore it across your devices. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy, the Apple Privacy Policy, and the Google Privacy Policy.

Analytics

RUMPUS! does not include our own third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. Any limited, aggregated diagnostics you receive come from the app stores' standard reporting (for example crash counts), which is governed by Apple's and Google's policies, not ours.

Children

RUMPUS! is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Where a store version shows ads to a general audience, they are requested in a manner intended to be appropriate for a general audience. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will address it.

Your choices and rights

Because we don't hold a personal profile of you, most control lives on your device: you can clear the app's local data, reset or limit your advertising identifier, and (on iOS) allow or deny tracking. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA over data our ad and purchase partners process; you can exercise those directly with those partners (linked above) or contact us and we'll help point you to the right place.

International users

BlackRaven AI LLC operates the app and support from Wyoming, United States. Our advertising and purchase partners may process data in other countries under their own safeguards.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy we'll change the date below and post the new version at this URL. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
