Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What TOUCHMARK does and does not say. TOUCHMARK renders label text in the format of 16 CFR 1500.19 and 1500.121 and of the tracking-label requirement at 15 U.S.C. § 2063(a)(5)(A), from the entries you make. It does not determine that a product is compliant, certified, approved, tested or safe, it drafts no Children’s Product Certificate, it makes no determination about whether your product needs testing or qualifies for an exemption, and it is not legal advice. You own your label. Check it against your own materials and records before you order a tag run.
  • The small-parts result is a stated model of your own measurement. TOUCHMARK computes a stated model of the size test at 16 CFR 1501.4(b)(1) from the three caliper dimensions you type, and prints that model on the screen that uses it. Parts that are not boxes are approximated by their bounding box, which errs toward flagging. The definitive test is the CPSC procedure of 16 CFR 1501.4(b) performed at a CPSC-accepted third-party laboratory.
  • What is free and what is Pro. The instrument is free for ever for two products: the whole solve with every statement in its prescribed wording and its citation, both ban forks, the complete cap-height arithmetic at your own panel area, THE GAUGE, the true-scale on-screen preview, the four-element tracking mark, the Prove-it screen, and the batch book as a record — commit runs and read every batch you have ever entered. Pro adds products beyond the second, the re-solve diff against your last struck batch, print-ready label artwork as an SVG at true physical size, and the full packaging mark. Pro never buys a different answer: the solver is the same solver on both tiers, and your export is never gated.
  • Subscription terms. TOUCHMARK Pro is $29.99 a year and opens with a 21-day free introductory offer (annual plan only, offered once per store account), or $4.99 a month with no trial, or $84.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next charge and the period you have paid for runs to its end.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted or hidden. Every product you built on Pro still opens, still solves, still shows its whole batch book, still exports as JSON, CSV and text and still deletes on the free tier, including books far longer than the free tier would let you build today. What stops is starting a new product beyond the second, the re-solve diff, the print-ready artwork and the packaging mark. A lapse lowers a ceiling on new work; it never takes a record away.
  • Textiles. Where your article is a textile, TOUCHMARK names the fibre-content and care-labelling requirement and cites 16 CFR 303 and 16 CFR 423. It computes none of it; that is a different instrument.
  • Prove it. Open the Prove-it screen and TOUCHMARK re-runs ten of the answer-key cases on your own handset and shows its answers beside the expected ones, so you can check the instrument rather than trust it.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.
