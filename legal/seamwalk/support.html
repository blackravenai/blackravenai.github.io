Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What is free and what is Pro. The instrument is free forever: unlimited audits of any pattern PDF, all eleven reconciliation families, every size in one pass, the working shown behind every number, THE WALK in full, the seam map proposed from your own pieces and editable with undo, the “Prove it” self-test, and a shelf of two patterns with two walks each and the diff between them. Pro adds the shelf itself — every pattern and every version you have ever walked — plus version diffs across the whole history, cross-release statistics, the re-audit-due queue and the publishable errata sheet. Pro never buys a different measurement: the eleven families are the same eleven families on both tiers, and CSV and shelf-JSON export are never gated.
  • Subscription terms. SEAMWALK Pro is $69.99 a year and opens with a 21-day free introductory offer (annual plan only, offered once per store account), or $12.99 a month with no trial, or $199.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next charge and the period you have paid for runs to its end.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted or hidden. Every pattern you saved on Pro still opens, still shows its walks and its findings, still exports as CSV and as shelf JSON and still deletes on the free tier, including shelves far longer than the free tier would let you build today. What stops is saving a new pattern beyond the second, a third walk on one pattern, the cross-release statistics, the re-audit-due queue and the errata sheet. A lapse lowers a ceiling on new work; it never takes a record away.
  • How the check works. SEAMWALK parses the PDF itself — objects, cross-references, Flate streams, optional-content groups — so each graded size is separated out, then assembles the printed tiles back into one coordinate space and segments the drawn paths into pieces, edges, notches and seam-allowance lines. It measures true arc length along every edge, pairs the mating edges of each seam across every size, and reconciles what the file draws against what your cover declares. The eleven families are: seam length mismatch, seam allowance, notch missing, piece missing in a size, grade flat, grade reversed, inventory piece absent, inventory missing from the layout, inventory name mismatch, tile scale and scale square.
  • The seam map is proposed, then confirmed by you. SEAMWALK only declares a pairing the geometry supports in essentially every size, so it is deliberately conservative: a badly broken mate is offered as a suspected mate that does not match rather than asserted. You can add, remove, re-ease and rename anything on the map, and every change re-runs the audit with undo.
  • Naming the edges. A PDF does not name its edges, so SEAMWALK numbers them positionally until you name them. Type side seam, armscye or waist once and the name flows into the seam map, the suspected-mate list, THE WALK’s tape labels, every finding’s working, the CSV and the errata sheet. Re-walking a pattern already on your shelf carries your edge names, ease settings and hand-added seams forward onto the new geometry.
  • Prove it. The app carries six synthetic sample patterns whose planted faults are known exactly. Open the “Prove it” screen and SEAMWALK re-audits them on your own handset and shows its answers against the known ones, so you can check the instrument rather than trust it.
  • A file came back unreadable. SEAMWALK measures vector geometry. A page that is a flattened raster image carries no measurable edges, and the app names it as unreadable rather than passing it silently. Export your pattern as vector artwork before tiling and it will read.
  • What it will not do. SEAMWALK reconciles a pattern against the specification the pattern itself declares. It does not draft, grade or fit, it ships no size charts and no block library, and it gives no fit or sizing advice. It does not promise a pattern is free of error — it reports the disagreements it can measure and shows you the arithmetic behind each one. Finding nothing is not a guarantee.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.

Sources & attribution

SEAMWALK is measured against a frozen answer key of 154 authored multi-size pattern PDFs, 33 of them deliberately clean, plus 19 reconstructions of real published errata from three named independent designers. On the authored key it finds 349 of 352 planted defects (99.15% against a 95% threshold) with 0 false findings per case against a limit of 2, and 0 false findings on the clean cases; it flags 19 of 19 of the real errata against an 80% threshold. Its extractor identifies 5,817 of 5,817 piece instances with label and size, and across 9,758 seam-length comparisons the mean error is 0.0002 mm and the worst 0.0014 mm against a 0.5 mm bound. The three missed defects are named in the app’s design record: in each case a second planted defect on the same piece erased the evidence for the first, and on observable defects the engine is 349 of 349. A deliberately weak comparison baseline recorded before the engine existed fails all four thresholds. Every case PDF in the corpus parses under Poppler, an independent implementation this build did not write. The method, every threshold and every constant are cited line by line in the app’s design record and its answer key.
