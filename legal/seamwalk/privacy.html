Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-09-04

SEAMWALK is a pattern-checking tool for people who draft and sell their own PDF sewing patterns, made by BlackRaven AI LLC. It runs entirely on your device. There is no account and no sign-in, and we designed it to need as little of your information as possible.

The short version

  • No account, no login, no name or email required.
  • Your data is saved on your device, not on our servers.
  • We don't run our own analytics or tracking.
  • SEAMWALK has no server of its own. It opens your pattern PDF, reads the objects and streams inside it, resolves the per-size layers, assembles the tiles into one sheet, measures every piece, edge, notch and seam allowance it draws, runs all eleven reconciliation families against the specification your own cover declares, and keeps your shelf on the device — and it works with the network switched off. There is no artificial-intelligence service behind it and no unreleased pattern of yours is sent anywhere to be read. The only exception is buying or restoring a subscription: that is handled by Apple’s or Google’s own purchase system, and the receipt it returns is checked by our subscription provider (RevenueCat) so the app knows whether Pro is active. That check carries an anonymous store purchase identifier and the receipt — never your pattern files, your seam maps, your findings or anything you typed.
  • SEAMWALK shows no ads. Store versions may offer purchases (via RevenueCat and the app store); those partners have their own privacy policies, linked below.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or photos. We have no user accounts and no server-side profile of you. The only time we see your email is if you choose to email our support address.

Data stored on your device

The pattern PDFs you open are read into memory on the device and are never written anywhere by the app; only the measurements and findings taken from them are kept. Your patterns — their names, every dated walk you have saved, the findings each walk produced by family, the seam map you corrected, the edge names you typed, the ease and gather figures you set, and the size range each walk covered — together with your app settings are stored locally on your device, in the app’s local storage under the key seamwalk.save.v1. You can export any of it — or all of it — as an audit CSV or as a complete shelf JSON backup at any time, on any tier, including after a Pro plan ends. None of it is uploaded or transmitted by the app, and deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising

SEAMWALK does not show ads and does not include any advertising SDK.

Purchases

If SEAMWALK offers in-app purchases, the transaction is handled by the app store (Apple App Store or Google Play) and processed for us by RevenueCat, which manages entitlements and receipts. We never see or store your payment card details. RevenueCat may process a purchase/receipt identifier to unlock what you bought and restore it across your devices. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy, the Apple Privacy Policy, and the Google Privacy Policy.

Analytics

SEAMWALK does not include our own third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. Any limited, aggregated diagnostics you receive come from the app stores' standard reporting (for example crash counts), which is governed by Apple's and Google's policies, not ours.

Children

SEAMWALK is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will address it.

Your choices and rights

Because we don't hold a personal profile of you, most control lives on your device: you can clear the app's local data. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA over data our purchase partners process; you can exercise those directly with those partners (linked above) or contact us and we'll help point you to the right place.

International users

BlackRaven AI LLC operates the app and support from Wyoming, United States. Our purchase partners may process data in other countries under their own safeguards.
