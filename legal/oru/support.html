Support

Last updated 2026-09-28

Need help with ORU? You're in the right place. BlackRaven AI LLC makes ORU, and we read every message.

Contact us

Email support@blackravenai.com and we'll help. We aim to reply within 2–3 business days. To speed things up, tell us your device and OS version and describe what happened.

Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What ads ORU shows. Family-safe, non-personalised ads from Google AdMob, requested with child-directed treatment and a G content rating for every player. An optional short video tapes your crane back once per flight. An occasional short ad may appear between flights — never in your first five flights, never in the first minute and a half of a session, never within three minutes of the last one, and never on the Daily Wind. Settings › Ad Privacy Choices reviews consent where the law provides it.
  • Who is playing, and the grown-up check. The first time ORU opens it asks whether a grown-up or a child is playing. Buying, restoring and ad-privacy choices sit behind a short grown-up check. Settings › Change Player asks again.
  • No Ads. A single $2.99 purchase (US price; the store shows yours) removes every ad, and the taped revive becomes free. It is not a subscription. Restore it in Settings › Restore Purchases.
  • Nothing paid is random, and stars are never for sale. Stars are earned in flight and spent on papers and trails that change how your crane looks, never how it flies.
  • Folds and wishes. Folds are three goals met inside a single flight; each pays stars and is replaced by a harder one. Every thousand cranes lets you write a wish, kept only on your device.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.
