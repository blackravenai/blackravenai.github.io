Email support@blackravenai.com and we'll help. We aim to reply within 2–3 business days. To speed things up, tell us your device and OS version and describe what happened.

Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What is free and what is Pro. The whole solver is free forever — all four species, the full cut taxonomy, the work order, every manifest, every customer quote, the yield book and uncapped export in JSON, CSV and plain text. Free keeps 3 animals on the rail with 2 buyers each. Pro removes both caps and re-fits the yield estimate from your own finished animals.
  • Subscription terms. Pro is $59.99 a year with a 28-day free trial (annual plan only, once per store account), or $8.99 a month with no trial, or $119.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price until you cancel; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your App Store or Google Play account under Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the period ends — cancelling stops the next charge and the plan runs to the end of the period you have already paid for.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted or locked. Every animal, buyer, break, artifact and yield record stays on the device, stays readable, stays exportable and stays deletable; the free caps simply apply again to what you add next.
  • What GAMBREL is not. It plans your animal on paper. It is not a storefront, it does not take payments, it does not route deliveries, and it does not keep herd or treatment records. It starts when the animal is booked for harvest.
  • No inspection, safety or legal claim. GAMBREL organises your own cutting plan and your own numbers. It makes no food-safety, inspection, grading or legal statement about any animal or any cut, and it is not a substitute for your processor’s rules or your local law.
  • The numbers are estimates until you weigh. Dressing percentage and cut yields are published reference figures until you record what actually came back from the locker. Every estimated figure says so in the app.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.

Sources & attribution

GAMBREL’s primal and sub-primal yield references are taken from published extension-service carcass-cutting data (including Penn State Extension’s beef carcass yield tables), which sum to about 97% of carcass weight; the app does not invent the remainder. The allocator is a deterministic seeded greedy solve refined by simulated annealing, constrained by package grain, and every constant and citation is recorded line by line in the app’s design record.
