Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-09-25

STOPBRAINROT is a habit game that helps you spend less time doomscrolling, made by BlackRaven AI LLC. Your brain is a pet: it heals when you put the phone down and rots when you scroll. It runs entirely on your device. There is no account and no sign-in, and we designed it to need as little of your information as possible.

The short version

  • No account, no login, no name or email required.
  • Your data is saved on your device, not on our servers.
  • We don't run our own analytics or tracking.
  • STOPBRAINROT has no server of its own and makes no network request for its core features: the Rot Check, lock-in sessions, urge surfing, check-ins, the grove, quests, streaks and every game system run inside the app and work with the network switched off. Reminders you turn on are scheduled locally by your phone. There are only two exceptions. Buying or restoring the optional GIGABRAIN membership is handled by Apple’s own purchase system, and the receipt it returns is checked by our subscription provider (RevenueCat) so the app knows whether the membership is active; that check carries an anonymous store purchase identifier and the receipt, never your answers, check-ins or progress. And if you choose to watch an optional rewarded ad (see below), Google AdMob loads for that ad only.
  • STOPBRAINROT may show ads; advertising partners' policies are linked below.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or photos. We have no user accounts and no server-side profile of you. The only time we see your email is if you choose to email our support address.

Data stored on your device

Your Rot Check score, your brain’s name, your streaks, neurons, grove, quests, check-in answers, lock-in history, chosen “danger zone” reminder time and app settings are stored locally on your device (in the app’s private storage under the key stopbrainrot-v1). They are included in your phone’s own device backup, if you use one, and are never uploaded or transmitted by the app. Share cards are drawn on your device and handed to your phone’s share sheet only when you tap share; the link on a card carries only the score and brain name the card already shows. If you choose Save Image, the card is saved to your Photos with your permission. Deleting the app, or using “factory-reset brain” in Settings, deletes all of it.

Advertising

STOPBRAINROT may display advertising provided by third-party ad networks; see their linked policies for how they handle data.

Purchases

If STOPBRAINROT offers in-app purchases, the transaction is handled by the app store (Apple App Store or Google Play) and processed for us by RevenueCat, which manages entitlements and receipts. We never see or store your payment card details. RevenueCat may process a purchase/receipt identifier to unlock what you bought and restore it across your devices. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy, the Apple Privacy Policy, and the Google Privacy Policy.

Analytics

STOPBRAINROT does not include our own third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. Any limited, aggregated diagnostics you receive come from the app stores' standard reporting (for example crash counts), which is governed by Apple's and Google's policies, not ours.

Children

STOPBRAINROT is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will address it.

Your choices and rights

Because we don't hold a personal profile of you, most control lives on your device: you can clear the app's local data. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA over data our purchase partners process; you can exercise those directly with those partners (linked above) or contact us and we'll help point you to the right place.

International users

BlackRaven AI LLC operates the app and support from Wyoming, United States. Our purchase partners may process data in other countries under their own safeguards.
