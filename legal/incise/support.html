Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What is free and what is Pro. The solve, the verdict, the limiting glyph and the computed fix are free forever on any die, in any face, with no limit — free computes the identical number Pro computes. THE WEEDED SHEET, the family proof sheet, JSON and CSV export and import, and every editable floor, margin and printer profile are free too, along with one 1:1 cut file so you can prove your own printer and plotter before you pay anything. Free keeps 3 dies, 1 companion fingerprint, clip art on 1 die and 1 saved house standard. Pro adds unlimited 1:1 cut files in EPS, DXF, SVG and PDF, the companion measurer, the unlimited archive, a saved standard per cemetery, and clip art on every die.
  • Subscription terms. Pro is $69.00 a year and opens with a 21-day free trial (annual plan only, once per account), or $9.99 a month with no trial, or $179.00 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted or hidden. Every die, every companion fingerprint and every saved standard already entered stays readable, editable, exportable and deletable. A lapse lowers the ceiling on new records; it never takes a record away.
  • Check the stencil before you blast it. INCISE computes a layout and writes a 1:1 file. It does not measure your granite, cut your rubber or run your plotter. Print the file, measure the 6.000 in rule bar on it, and verify the size on the sheet itself before the mask goes on the stone.
  • Where the blast floors come from. The type floor is calibrated from the trade’s own published minimum for a Times-metric roman; the 0.075 in figure is carried as the artwork line floor and is deliberately not applied to type. Every floor is labelled with where it came from and every one of them is editable, because the floor that matters is the one your rubber, your nozzle and your grit actually hold. Nothing INCISE has computed has yet been blasted on real granite by us.
  • Companion measurement. Measuring an inscription already in the ground needs a scale reference of known length laid flat on the face, in the same plane as the letters. Accuracy is proven against synthetic photographs through a perspective warp; real granite has lichen, glare and paint-filled letters, so read the numbers and judge them.
  • Imported clip art. The SVG reader ignores transform attributes on groups and takes arcs as chords. Flatten the art in your drawing program first. Raster art — a scanned rose — is refused, because there are no lines in it to measure.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.

Sources & attribution

INCISE’s feasibility model measures every shipped letter outline with a Euclidean distance transform and a morphological opening to find its narrowest sustained stroke, then bisects for the largest cap height that fits the die face and clears the floors; the companion measurer uses a planar homography by direct linear transform with Otsu thresholding and connected-component analysis. The method and every constant are cited line by line in the app’s design record. DXF output is written to the AutoCAD R12 specification, EPS to DSC 3.0 with a HiResBoundingBox, and PDF to a stated MediaBox.
