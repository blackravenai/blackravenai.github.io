Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-10

Kept is a returns and warranty tracker made by BlackRaven AI LLC: it turns a receipt into a return deadline and a warranty end date, and reminds you before they pass. It runs entirely on your device. There is no account and no sign-in, and we designed it to need as little of your information as possible.

The short version

  • No account, no login, no name or email required.
  • Your data is saved on your device, not on our servers.
  • We don't run our own analytics or tracking.
  • Kept has no server of its own. Reading a receipt uses Apple's on-device text recognition (Vision), so receipt images and their text never leave your phone. The return-policy table for 137 retailers ships inside the app. The only network activity is Apple's own: when you buy, restore or check Pro, the App Store and StoreKit on your device talk to Apple. No purchase data, and nothing you entered, is sent to us or to any third party. A store's returns page opens in your browser only if you tap “Start a return”.
  • Kept shows no ads. Purchases are made through Apple's App Store directly; there is no third-party purchase or subscription service.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or photos. We have no user accounts and no server-side profile of you. The only time we see your email is if you choose to email our support address.

Data stored on your device

Your purchases (store, item, price, purchase date, order number, category and notes), the receipt images you scan or choose, the return, warranty and price-protection dates worked out for each, whether you returned or kept each item, your reminder schedule, your App Lock setting and your app settings are stored locally on your device: records in the app's private database and receipt images as files in the app's private storage. A small summary of upcoming deadlines is shared with Kept's own widget through an App Group on the same device. The camera is used only while you scan a receipt, the photo picker only reads the photo you pick, and Face ID is used only to unlock the app if you turn App Lock on — Apple handles Face ID and Kept never receives your face data. Reminders are scheduled locally by your phone. You can export your records as CSV or PDF at any time. None of it is uploaded or transmitted by the app, and deleting the app deletes it. Like any app data, it is included in your device's own iCloud or computer backup if you have those turned on; that backup is handled by Apple under your account, not by us.

Advertising

Kept does not show ads and does not include any advertising SDK.

Purchases

Kept's in-app purchases and subscriptions are sold and processed entirely by Apple through the App Store, using Apple's StoreKit on your device. We never see or store your payment details, and no third-party purchase service is involved: the app asks Apple's StoreKit on your device whether you own Pro, and Apple signs that answer. We receive only the aggregated, anonymous sales reports Apple gives every developer. See the Apple Privacy Policy.

Analytics

Kept does not include our own third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. Any limited, aggregated diagnostics you receive come from the app stores' standard reporting (for example crash counts), which is governed by Apple's and Google's policies, not ours.

Children

Kept is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will address it.

Your choices and rights

Because we don't hold a personal profile of you, most control lives on your device: you can clear the app's local data. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA over data our purchase partners process; you can exercise those directly with those partners (linked above) or contact us and we'll help point you to the right place.

International users

BlackRaven AI LLC operates the app and support from Wyoming, United States. Our purchase partners may process data in other countries under their own safeguards.
