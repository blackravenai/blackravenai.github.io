Support

Last updated 2026-08-20

Need help with AVERTED? You're in the right place. BlackRaven AI LLC makes AVERTED, and we read every message.

Contact us

Email support@blackravenai.com and we'll help. We aim to reply within 2–3 business days. To speed things up, tell us your device and OS version and describe what happened.

Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the shop/store screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • Remove ads. If AVERTED offers a remove-ads or premium purchase, buy it once and restore it on your other devices with "Restore Purchases".
  • Reset or delete my data. Your progress is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.
