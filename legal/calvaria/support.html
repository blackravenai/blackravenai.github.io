Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What is free and what is Pro. Every verdict on every piece is free forever — the federal lists, all thirty transcribed state rows, every citation with its read-date, every REFUSED class, trade-name resolution, the UNKNOWN and AMBIGUOUS answers, the listing words, the Sources screen, and CSV and JSON export of everything, always. Free keeps ten pieces in the book. Pro adds an unlimited book, Re-solve with verdict history, and the state-row editor. Pro never buys a different answer: the engine runs identically whether you have paid or not.
  • Subscription terms. Pro is $29.99 a year and opens with a 21-day free trial (annual plan only, offered once per account), or $5.99 a month with no trial, or $79.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted or hidden. Every piece in your book stays readable, stays cited, and stays exportable as CSV and JSON, including pieces past the tenth. A lapse lowers a ceiling on new pieces; it never takes a record away, and reading and deleting your own pieces is never capped.
  • CALVARIA is not legal advice. It reads transcribed regulatory text and reports what that text says about the piece you described, with the citation and the date the text was read. It is not a lawyer, it does not know your facts, and it cannot authorise a sale. Where the answer is close, read the cited rule yourself and ask the agency.
  • Thirty states, not fifty. Thirty state rows have been transcribed and dated. A state that has not been transcribed returns STATE DECIDES — an explicit answer that the federal layer is all CALVARIA can speak to for that piece, not a guess that the sale is fine. Six transcribed rows are marked “verify first” because their primary text was not read end to end.
  • CITES is deliberately not transcribed. 50 CFR 23.91(a) states that the official appendices are the CITES Secretariat’s, not the ones printed in the CFR. Anything crossing a border returns a first-class REFER quoting that rule, and CALVARIA never grants an export.
  • Where the rules come from. Every row in the app is transcribed from primary text — 50 CFR 10.13 and 17.11, the Migratory Bird Treaty Act, the Endangered Species Act, the Bald and Golden Eagle Protection Act, the Marine Mammal Protection Act, NAGPRA and state statute and rule — and every row carries its citation and the date it was read. The Sources screen lists all of them, free.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.

Sources & attribution

CALVARIA’s verdict is a pure axis pipeline over transcribed primary text: species resolved against 50 CFR 10.13 and 17.11 membership, then part, origin and destination narrowed by federal statute and by the state row, where a state row may only narrow an outcome and never widen it. The engine is graded against an independent derivation over 29,760 enumerated cases and against 29 published regulator examples; the method and every constant are cited line by line in the app’s design record. No third-party regulatory database is bundled or queried.
