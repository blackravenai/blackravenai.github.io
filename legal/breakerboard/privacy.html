Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-09-04

BREAKERBOARD is a deck-board layout tool for the builder who lays the decking, made by BlackRaven AI LLC. It runs entirely on your device. There is no account and no sign-in, and we designed it to need as little of your information as possible.

The short version

  • No account, no login, no name or email required.
  • Your data is saved on your device, not on our servers.
  • We don't run our own analytics or tracking.
  • BREAKERBOARD has no server of its own. It searches the rip widths and the gap schedule, lays every butt joint onto a joist, staggers the rows, builds the cut list and the take-off, draws the board plan and keeps your Deck Book on the device, and it works with the network switched off. There is no artificial-intelligence service behind it and nothing you type about a job is sent anywhere to be read. The only exception is buying or restoring a subscription: that is handled by Apple’s or Google’s own purchase system, and the receipt it returns is checked by our subscription provider (RevenueCat) so the app knows whether Pro is active. That check carries an anonymous store purchase identifier and the receipt — never your decks, your dimensions, your costs or anything you typed.
  • BREAKERBOARD shows no ads. Store versions may offer purchases (via RevenueCat and the app store); those partners have their own privacy policies, linked below.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or photos. We have no user accounts and no server-side profile of you. The only time we see your email is if you choose to email our support address.

Data stored on your device

Your decks — their names, the outline you entered, the decking product and temperature band you selected, the gaps, the solved plan and its cut list, and the actuals you record afterwards (boards bought, boards left over, material cost, your notes) — together with the gap history taken from the decks you solve and your app settings are stored locally on your device, in the app’s local storage under the key breakerboard.save.v1. You can export any of it — or all of it — as a complete JSON backup at any time, on any tier, including after a Pro plan ends, and the cut list also exports as CSV. None of it is uploaded or transmitted by the app, and deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising

BREAKERBOARD does not show ads and does not include any advertising SDK.

Purchases

If BREAKERBOARD offers in-app purchases, the transaction is handled by the app store (Apple App Store or Google Play) and processed for us by RevenueCat, which manages entitlements and receipts. We never see or store your payment card details. RevenueCat may process a purchase/receipt identifier to unlock what you bought and restore it across your devices. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy, the Apple Privacy Policy, and the Google Privacy Policy.

Analytics

BREAKERBOARD does not include our own third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. Any limited, aggregated diagnostics you receive come from the app stores' standard reporting (for example crash counts), which is governed by Apple's and Google's policies, not ours.

Children

BREAKERBOARD is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will address it.

Your choices and rights

Because we don't hold a personal profile of you, most control lives on your device: you can clear the app's local data. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA over data our purchase partners process; you can exercise those directly with those partners (linked above) or contact us and we'll help point you to the right place.

International users

BlackRaven AI LLC operates the app and support from Wyoming, United States. Our purchase partners may process data in other countries under their own safeguards.
