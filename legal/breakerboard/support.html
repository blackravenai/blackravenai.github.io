Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What is free and what is Pro. The instrument is free forever for a rectangular deck: unlimited solves, the whole Board Plan — the rip at each edge, the gap schedule, every butt joint landed on its joist, rows, joints, boards, waste and clips with the reasons written out — the cut list and take-off on screen board by board, three decks of your own in the Deck Book, the “Prove it” self-test, and JSON export of everything you have entered, forever. Pro adds L-shaped and multi-part outlines, picture frames and breaker boards, cut-list export as CSV and as a saw sheet, the Deck Book past three decks, the actuals you record against each deck, and the gap history. Pro never buys a different plan: the solver is the same solver on both tiers, and your own data is never gated.
  • Subscription terms. BREAKERBOARD Pro is $59.99 a year and opens with a 21-day free introductory offer (annual plan only, offered once per store account), or $9.99 a month with no trial, or $179.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next charge and the period you have paid for runs to its end.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted or hidden. Every deck you saved on Pro still opens, still solves, still shows its cut list and still exports as JSON on the free tier, including Deck Books far longer than the free tier would let you build today. What stops is saving a new deck beyond the third, the L-shapes and picture frames, cut-list export, new actuals and the gap history. A lapse lowers a ceiling on new work; it never takes a record away.
  • How the plan is worked out. BREAKERBOARD solves the surface in two axes. Across the deck it searches the rip widths at both edges together with the gap schedule, so no edge board ends up a sliver and the spacers you actually carry will close the field. Along the deck it runs a dynamic program over the rows that lands every butt joint on a joist, holds your minimum offset between joints in neighbouring rows, keeps a joint pattern from repeating within the rows you set, and makes each board bear on the number of joists you require — while spending as little stock as the rules allow. Every figure on the plan opens to the reason behind it.
  • Prove it. The app carries ten test decks whose optimum this repository establishes by exhaustive search. Open “Prove it” and BREAKERBOARD re-solves all ten on your own handset, running 1,438 checks, and shows its answers against the known ones — so you can check the instrument rather than trust it.
  • Where the numbers come from. The on-centre spacings, gap ranges and temperature bands BREAKERBOARD offers are transcribed figures from named decking manufacturers’ own published installation instructions, cited in the app beside each table. They are defaults you select and edit to match the frame that is already built. The manufacturer’s instructions, the warranty terms and your local code govern, and the app says so on the plan itself.
  • What it will not do. BREAKERBOARD plans the deck surface and counts material. It gives no structural sizing of any kind — no joist span, no beam, no footing, no ledger, no guard or railing — and no stair geometry. It does not inspect your frame, measure your deck or check your product. Check every figure against the manufacturer’s instructions and your local code before you cut.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.
