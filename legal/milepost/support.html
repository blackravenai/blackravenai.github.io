Contact us

Email support@blackravenai.com and we'll help. We aim to reply within 2–3 business days. To speed things up, tell us your device and OS version and describe what happened.

Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What is free and what is Pro. Free: one vehicle with the full pace engine, the dashboard gauge, daily budget and reminders. Milepost Pro adds Home Screen, Lock Screen and interactive widgets, Siri and Shortcuts, more vehicles, the trend chart and projection, the buy-miles-now calculator, the turn-in checklist, export and dashboard photos.
  • Subscription terms. Milepost Pro is $9.99 per year or $19.99 once for lifetime access, in US dollars; local prices may differ. The yearly plan opens with a one-week free trial, offered once per Apple account; if you don't cancel before it ends, it becomes a paid yearly subscription. Subscriptions renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel any time in iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions (or from the app's Settings). Cancelling stops the next charge; the period you paid for runs to its end. The lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews and has nothing to cancel.
  • Is the overage figure exact? It is an estimate from your readings and the overage rate you entered. Your lease contract and the dealer's inspection decide what you actually owe.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.
