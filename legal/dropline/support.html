Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What is free and what is Pro. One wall per job is free forever, with every match type, the same proven solver, the same roll count and waste figures, the elevation, the reading and the cut list on screen, three saved jobs, and export of your own data. Pro adds the whole run — as many walls as the room has, with corner wraps — the focal rule, two-batch ordering, sharing and printing the per-roll cut list, and an unlimited job archive. Pro never buys a better number: the solver is the same solver on both sides.
  • Subscription terms. Pro is $32.99 a year and opens with a 21-day free trial (yearly plan only, offered once per account), or $5.99 a month with no trial, or $99.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next charge and the period you have paid for runs to its end.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted or hidden. Every job you entered on Pro still opens, still solves, still edits, still deletes and still exports on the free tier, including jobs with more walls than the free tier would let you create today. A lapse lowers a ceiling on new work; it never takes a record away.
  • Where the plan comes from. DROPLINE lays out every drop in hanging order, sets the cut length from the wall height plus the trim allowance and the pattern phase the match demands, then assigns the drops to rolls so that the fewest rolls are opened. It is an exact search, not the merchant’s area formula, and on the test corpus it opens fewer rolls than the formula on 38.9% of jobs.
  • Prove it. The app carries ten shipped test jobs whose optimum was established by exhaustive search off the device. Open Settings › Prove it and DROPLINE re-solves all ten on your handset and shows you its answer against the proven one, so you can check the instrument rather than trust it.
  • Measure the wall yourself. DROPLINE plans from the figures you type. It does not measure your room, inspect your paper or check your batch numbers, and a pattern repeat printed wrongly on the label will produce a wrong plan. Check the roll label and the wall before you cut.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.
