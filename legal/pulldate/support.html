Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What PULLDATE does, and does not do. PULLDATE assembles the statements a carton carries under the federal rule and under the rules transcribed for your state, in the order those rules put them, at the minimum type height each rule names, and shows you the source and the read date behind every line. It does not tell you that you are exempt, that you are licensed, or that you may sell eggs, and it never grades an egg. Grading is a determination made by a licensed grader against a candling basis; PULLDATE only reports what a carton must say once you have told it what basis you have. It is not legal advice.
  • Twenty-five states, and it says so. Twenty-five jurisdictions are transcribed, each with its own labelled, dated and URL-linked source. For any other state the app renders the federal set only and names on screen that your state is not transcribed. Do not read a federal-only answer as a complete state answer.
  • Cage-free, free-range and pasture-raised are deliberately out of scope. Several states define those terms by reference to a private industry standard rather than to public law, and reproducing a private standard is not something this app will do. If you make one of those claims on your carton, that wording is yours and PULLDATE does not check it.
  • What is free and what is Pro. The complete solve is free forever, for every one of the twenty-five states: every required element, every citation, every refusal, the whole weight-class and date arithmetic, THE LID drawn at true scale, the entire Prove-it screen and every export — carton sheet, true-scale SVG, lots CSV and full JSON — plus three kept lots in the batch book. Pro adds the book past three lots, the re-solve-and-diff that re-runs every kept lot when a rule or your operation changes, the age of each lot’s citations, and your own rule row on a state’s set. Pro never buys a different answer.
  • Subscription terms. PULLDATE Pro is $29.99 a year and opens with a 21-day free trial (annual plan only, offered once per store account), or $5.99 a month with no trial, or $79.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next charge and the period you have paid for runs to its end.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted, hidden or locked. Every lot in the book stays readable, exportable, editable and deletable however many there are, and the rule rows you wrote keep applying. Only the re-solve past the newest three goes quiet, and the book tells you so on its face. A lapse lowers a ceiling on new work; it never takes a record away.
  • The rules move. Every line on screen and in every export carries the date its source was read and the version of the tables that produced it, and the batch book shows you how old each lot’s citations are. Check them against the current rule and your own state department of agriculture before you order a carton run.
  • Prove it. Open the Prove-it screen and PULLDATE recomputes published USDA weight-class and net-quantity figures on your own handset and sets its answers beside the printed ones, including two places where USDA’s own printed table disagrees with the rule the rest of the table follows — reproduced as printed and named, not smoothed. Check the instrument rather than trust it.
  • Does it work with no signal? Yes. It solves, saves, draws and exports with the phone in aeroplane mode, because there is nothing to call.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.

Sources & attribution

PULLDATE’s federal statements are transcribed from 21 CFR 101.17(h) (the shell-egg safe handling instructions, including the wording, the hairline box and both exemptions) and its weight and net-quantity arithmetic from USDA AMS 7 CFR §56.218 Table I and AMS Exhibit II. Its twenty-five state rule sets are transcribed from each state’s own regulation, administrative code, department fact sheet or extension publication, and every row carries its title, its URL and the date it was read. Those texts are government edicts and are not subject to copyright (Georgia v. Public.Resource.Org, 590 U.S. 255 (2020)). No licensed consensus standard and no private industry standard is used anywhere in the app. The ground truth for the app’s own must-pass tests is parsed at test time from the regulator’s printed text rather than from the transcription, and every threshold, count and known source anomaly is recorded line by line in the app’s design record and answer key.
