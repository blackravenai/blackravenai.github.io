Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What is free and what is Pro. The engine is free forever, at the same accuracy Pro gets: all 14 checks with the full arithmetic printed beside every finding, unparsed lines reported by line number, the bag arithmetic for the batch size and spares policy you state, the “Prove it” self-test, one kit on the shelf with unlimited revisions and unlimited re-audits of it, and every export — the Markdown report, the marks CSV and the whole-shelf JSON. Pro adds the whole shelf rather than one kit, the revision ladder with the diff against your own last audit, and bag arithmetic across every batch size you run. Pro never buys a better audit — the 14 checks are the same 14 checks on both tiers, and export is never gated.
  • Subscription terms. SILKMARK Pro is $54.99 a year opening with a 21-day free introductory offer (annual plan only, offered once per store account), or $10.99 a month with no trial, or $164.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next charge and the period you have paid for runs to its end.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted or hidden. Every kit on the shelf, every revision filed under it and every audit kept against that revision stays readable, stays exportable and stays deletable — however many kits there are. What stops is adding a new kit beyond the first, opening the diff against a previous audit, and running the bagging sheet across several batch sizes. A lapse lowers a ceiling on new work; it never takes a record away.
  • What SILKMARK checks, and what it does not. SILKMARK reconciles four documents against one another: the bill of materials, the reference-designator list, the assembly guide and the bagging pick list. It resolves every designator across them, compares values and footprints per designator, checks that every part is introduced by a step, re-adds every quantity, and does the bag arithmetic under the spares policy and batch size you state. It checks documents, never circuits. It makes no claim about electrical safety, fitness for purpose, function, compliance or certification. An orientation finding means no step names an orientation for a part your own BOM says is polarised — it is a documentation-consistency item, never a statement about whether a circuit will work.
  • Lines it cannot read are reported, not skipped. A line SILKMARK cannot parse is raised as unchecked, by line number, with a count on the face of the report — never guessed and never silently passed over. A guide that names a part in a way no convention covers (a photograph caption, “the long screws”) is reported as unchecked rather than treated as clear. Finding nothing is not a guarantee that the kit is correct, and the app says so in those words on its own limits screen.
  • Prove it. The app carries ten kits whose faults were declared by hand rather than read back out of the engine. Open the “Prove it” screen and SILKMARK re-runs them on your own handset and shows its answers against the declared ones, so you can check the instrument rather than trust it.
  • Which files can it read? Plain and delimited text in the shapes you already export — comma, tab, semicolon and pipe-delimited bills of materials in four column conventions, plain designator lists, KiCad .net s-expressions and Eagle-style designator tables, assembly guides as numbered steps or as prose, and pick lists in three shapes. Text is also read out of a PDF on the device; a PDF that is only scanned images is reported as having no text layer rather than read as empty. SILKMARK never opens a binary EDA project file.
  • Whose documents are these? Yours. SILKMARK bundles no third-party bill of materials, netlist, manual or errata, and it reproduces none. Every kit in the shipped self-test was written for this app.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.
