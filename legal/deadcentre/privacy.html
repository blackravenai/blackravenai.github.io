Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-09-05

DEADCENTRE is a pedal-stroke measurement tool for professional bike fitters, made by BlackRaven AI LLC. It runs entirely on your device. There is no account and no sign-in, and we designed it to need as little of your information as possible.

The short version

  • No account, no login, no name or email required.
  • Your data is saved on your device, not on our servers.
  • We don't run our own analytics or tracking.
  • DEADCENTRE has no server of its own. The clip you film never leaves the handset: the pose model and the pedal-marker tracker are both inside the app — 11.8 MB of runtime and a 5.8 MB model ship in the bundle and are pinned by checksum — and the calibration, the angles, the knee-over-spindle offset, the band on every reading and the saddle-height arithmetic are all computed locally, with the network switched off. There is no artificial-intelligence service behind it, no video and no frame is uploaded, and the third-party pose runtime’s own usage telemetry is patched out at build time and blocked again by a content-security policy of connect-src ‘self’. Measured across every screen, the app makes zero off-origin requests. The only exception is buying or restoring a subscription: that is handled by Apple’s or Google’s own purchase system, and the receipt it returns is checked by our subscription provider (RevenueCat) so the app knows whether Pro is active. That check carries an anonymous store purchase identifier and the receipt — never your name, your riders, your clips or anything you typed.
  • DEADCENTRE shows no ads. Store versions may offer purchases (via RevenueCat and the app store); those partners have their own privacy policies, linked below.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or photos. We have no user accounts and no server-side profile of you. The only time we see your email is if you choose to email our support address.

Data stored on your device

Your rider files — each rider’s name or alias and your notes, and under each rider every session with its date, the bike label, the crank length, the convention you measured against, every reading with its band and the number of revolutions behind it, the capture grade, the adjustment you made and the stroke curves — together with the convention tables you edited, your app settings and your entitlement state, are stored locally on your device, in the app’s local storage under the key deadcentre.save.v2 (a save written by an earlier version under deadcentre.save.v1 is migrated forward and the old key is left in place). A video clip you pick is read frame by frame on the device and is never copied into the app or out of it; only the landmark tracks and the readings are kept. You can export every session as CSV and the whole file as JSON at any time, on any tier, including after a Pro plan ends, and import a JSON export back. None of it is uploaded or transmitted by the app, and deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising

DEADCENTRE does not show ads and does not include any advertising SDK.

Purchases

If DEADCENTRE offers in-app purchases, the transaction is handled by the app store (Apple App Store or Google Play) and processed for us by RevenueCat, which manages entitlements and receipts. We never see or store your payment card details. RevenueCat may process a purchase/receipt identifier to unlock what you bought and restore it across your devices. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy, the Apple Privacy Policy, and the Google Privacy Policy.

Analytics

DEADCENTRE does not include our own third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. Any limited, aggregated diagnostics you receive come from the app stores' standard reporting (for example crash counts), which is governed by Apple's and Google's policies, not ours.

Children

DEADCENTRE is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will address it.

Your choices and rights

Because we don't hold a personal profile of you, most control lives on your device: you can clear the app's local data. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA over data our purchase partners process; you can exercise those directly with those partners (linked above) or contact us and we'll help point you to the right place.

International users

BlackRaven AI LLC operates the app and support from Wyoming, United States. Our purchase partners may process data in other countries under their own safeguards.
