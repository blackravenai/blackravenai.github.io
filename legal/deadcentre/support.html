Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • Who this is for. DEADCENTRE is a measuring instrument for people who fit bicycles for a living — an independent fitter in a studio, or a two- or three-person shop. It is not for measuring yourself on your own bicycle, and it is not sold for that. It reports geometry: joint angles at dead centre, knee-over-spindle offset in millimetres, and the saddle height a convention you selected implies. It makes no statement about health, injury, pain, comfort or performance, and it never tells you what position a rider should be in. That judgement is the fitter’s, and the app says so on its own limits screen.
  • Every reading carries a band, and that is the point. DEADCENTRE does not claim a fixed accuracy figure, because it measured its own and would not round it in its favour. Instead it states a band with every number, and against a frozen answer key of 240 captures the truth falls inside the stated band on 95.9% of them. Typical bands, at the median: knee ±6.3°, hip ±5.5°, torso ±4.0°, elbow ±9.6°, knee-over-spindle ±39.7 mm. A band is not a disclaimer; it is the measurement.
  • Capture grade, and why a clip can be refused. How large the rider is in frame, and whether the camera is fixed, changes the result more than anything else, so the app grades it: Studio (camera fixed, rider 400 px or more of a 1080-px frame), Field (250 px or more), or Below. The grade is shown on the reading, on every session row, on the fit card, in the CSV and in the delta view. A clip whose pedal marker does not stand out from its background is refused with a reason rather than measured badly — in the marker test, three clips of eight were refused for exactly that, and the app was built to miss a coverage target rather than a correctness one.
  • What is free and what is Pro. The instrument is free forever, at the same accuracy Pro gets: one rider file with unlimited sessions, the Stroke Dial across all five metrics, every reading with its band, the capture grade, the saddle-height arithmetic, every cited convention table, the capture gate, undo, import, and CSV and JSON export of everything, always. Pro adds every rider rather than one, the Rider File across sessions with per-metric trends, the delta between any two sessions adjudicated against both delta bands, convention tables of your own, and the printable fit card. Pro never buys a more accurate number, and nothing you typed or measured is ever gated.
  • Subscription terms. DEADCENTRE Pro is $74.99 a year and opens with a 21-day free introductory offer (annual plan only, offered once per store account), or $11.99 a month with no trial, or $199.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next charge and the period you have paid for runs to its end. We cannot cancel it for you, and the app will not ask you to stay.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted, hidden or truncated. Every rider, every session, every reading and both exports come back exactly as they were and stay readable, editable, deletable and exportable, however many riders there are. What stops is measuring into a second rider file, the across-sessions and delta views, editing a convention into your own, and the printable fit card. A lapse lowers a ceiling on new work; it never takes a record away.
  • Prove it. Open the “Prove it” screen and DEADCENTRE re-runs shipped cases on your own handset and shows its answers beside the expected ones, so you can check the instrument rather than trust it.
  • Does it need a signal in the studio? No. The pose model and the whole engine are inside the app, so it films, measures, saves and exports with the phone in aeroplane mode.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.

Sources & attribution

DEADCENTRE is measured against frozen answer keys and null baselines recorded before the engine existed on disk. Across 240 frozen synthetic captures it accepts 221 and the band it states covers the truth on 95.9% of them; angle error is unbiased (slope 1.00, mean signed error under 0.8°), with mean absolute error of 2.65° at the knee, 2.23° at the hip, 1.57° at the torso, 3.75° at the elbow and 13.72 mm knee-over-spindle. Two null baselines recorded beforehand score 43.00° (single frame, no dead-centre detection) and 25.89 mm (no scale recovery, no plane model), and both state no band at all. A fourth key renders real pixels to test the pedal-marker tracker over 2,712 frames: centroid error 0.250 px mean, calibration-axis error 0.053% against a ±1.0% threshold, with a nearest-colour null failing at 0.0%.
