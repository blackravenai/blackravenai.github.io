Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-09-06

CLEANPLATE is a measuring instrument for people who light a chroma-key backing and shoot against it, made by BlackRaven AI LLC. It runs entirely on your device. There is no account and no sign-in, and we designed it to need as little of your information as possible.

The short version

  • No account, no login, no name or email required.
  • Your data is saved on your device, not on our servers.
  • We don't run our own analytics or tracking.
  • CLEANPLATE has no server of its own. It reads one frame you took of your own lit backing with the subject standing in it — the six-by-four evenness map in stops, the three spill indices at the crown, the face edge and the shoulder, the backing purity, the exposure and geometric separations, the cast-shadow fraction and the difficulty index — and it computes every one of those figures, the capture gate that decides whether the frame can be measured at all, and the corrections it prints beside them, on the device. It works with the network switched off. There is no artificial-intelligence service behind it, and a frame of your set, your client or the person standing in front of your backing is never sent anywhere to be looked at. Measured across every screen, the app makes zero off-origin requests. The only exception is buying or restoring a purchase: that is handled by Apple’s or Google’s own purchase system, and the receipt it returns is checked by our subscription provider (RevenueCat) so the app knows whether Pro is active. That check carries an anonymous store purchase identifier and the receipt — never your name, your photographs, your stands, your clients or anything you typed.
  • CLEANPLATE shows no ads. Store versions may offer purchases (via RevenueCat and the app store); those partners have their own privacy policies, linked below.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or photos. We have no user accounts and no server-side profile of you. The only time we see your email is if you choose to email our support address.

Data stored on your device

Your stands (the room, the backing’s size and material as you described them, and the convention you chose to judge them against) and every read on them — the evenness map and its per-cell figures, the three spill indices and whether each was measured from a reference frame or inferred from one frame, the purity, both separations, the cast-shadow fraction, the difficulty index, the capture-gate result and the corrections that were printed — together with your app settings and the small thumbnail each read keeps, are stored locally on your device, in the app’s local storage under the key cleanplate.save.v1. The frames you capture are read, measured and discarded; only the figures and that thumbnail are kept. You can export the whole book as JSON and every read as a CSV row at any time, on any tier, including after a Pro plan ends, and import a JSON export back. None of it is uploaded or transmitted by the app, and deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising

CLEANPLATE does not show ads and does not include any advertising SDK.

Purchases

If CLEANPLATE offers in-app purchases, the transaction is handled by the app store (Apple App Store or Google Play) and processed for us by RevenueCat, which manages entitlements and receipts. We never see or store your payment card details. RevenueCat may process a purchase/receipt identifier to unlock what you bought and restore it across your devices. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy, the Apple Privacy Policy, and the Google Privacy Policy.

Analytics

CLEANPLATE does not include our own third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. Any limited, aggregated diagnostics you receive come from the app stores' standard reporting (for example crash counts), which is governed by Apple's and Google's policies, not ours.

Children

CLEANPLATE is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will address it.

Your choices and rights

Because we don't hold a personal profile of you, most control lives on your device: you can clear the app's local data. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA over data our purchase partners process; you can exercise those directly with those partners (linked above) or contact us and we'll help point you to the right place.

International users

BlackRaven AI LLC operates the app and support from Wyoming, United States. Our purchase partners may process data in other countries under their own safeguards.
