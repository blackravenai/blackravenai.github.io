Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-09-06

GANSEY is a reconciliation instrument for independent knitwear designers who publish graded garment patterns, made by BlackRaven AI LLC. It runs entirely on your device. There is no account and no sign-in, and we designed it to need as little of your information as possible.

The short version

  • No account, no login, no name or email required.
  • Your data is saved on your device, not on our servers.
  • We don't run our own analytics or tracking.
  • GANSEY has no server of its own, and it makes no network request of any kind. It reads the pattern text you hand it, resolves the nested size brackets everywhere a number appears, runs a separate stitch-and-row count model down every size you graded, checks the gauge arithmetic, the size-table progression, the ease and the yardage, reconciles any chart against its written form and its key, and renders the size run — all of it arithmetic performed on your own handset, with the network switched off and the phone in aeroplane mode if you like. There is no artificial-intelligence service behind it, and an unpublished pattern of yours is never uploaded anywhere to be read. The shipped bundle was checked for network primitives and contains none: no fetch, no XMLHttpRequest, no WebSocket, no sendBeacon and no EventSource. The only exception is buying or restoring a subscription: that is handled by Apple’s or Google’s own purchase system, and the receipt it returns is checked by our subscription provider (RevenueCat) so the app knows whether Pro is active. That check carries an anonymous store purchase identifier and the receipt — never your name, your patterns, your customers or anything you typed.
  • GANSEY shows no ads. Store versions may offer purchases (via RevenueCat and the app store); those partners have their own privacy policies, linked below.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or photos. We have no user accounts and no server-side profile of you. The only time we see your email is if you choose to email our support address.

Data stored on your device

Your shelf (each pattern’s name, the instruction text and size table you audited, every dated audit with its per-size verdict, its checkpoints, its findings and the arithmetic behind each one, the coverage and UNCHECKED counts that audit ran with, and the findings you marked as intentional), your house defaults (gauge, ease, yards per stitch and the abbreviation dictionary that accrues in your own words) and your app settings are stored locally on your device, in the app’s local storage under the key gansey.save.v2. An older gansey.save.v1 record is read once, carried forward, and deliberately left where it is rather than deleted. You can export the ledger as text, any audit as CSV and the whole shelf as JSON at any time, on any tier, including after a Pro plan ends — your own records are never gated. None of it is uploaded or transmitted by the app, and deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising

GANSEY does not show ads and does not include any advertising SDK.

Purchases

If GANSEY offers in-app purchases, the transaction is handled by the app store (Apple App Store or Google Play) and processed for us by RevenueCat, which manages entitlements and receipts. We never see or store your payment card details. RevenueCat may process a purchase/receipt identifier to unlock what you bought and restore it across your devices. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy, the Apple Privacy Policy, and the Google Privacy Policy.

Analytics

GANSEY does not include our own third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. Any limited, aggregated diagnostics you receive come from the app stores' standard reporting (for example crash counts), which is governed by Apple's and Google's policies, not ours.

Children

GANSEY is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will address it.

Your choices and rights

Because we don't hold a personal profile of you, most control lives on your device: you can clear the app's local data. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA over data our purchase partners process; you can exercise those directly with those partners (linked above) or contact us and we'll help point you to the right place.

International users

BlackRaven AI LLC operates the app and support from Wyoming, United States. Our purchase partners may process data in other countries under their own safeguards.
