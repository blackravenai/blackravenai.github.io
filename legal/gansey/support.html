Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What GANSEY does, and what it does not decide. You paste the graded pattern you are about to publish, and its size table if you have one. GANSEY runs the counts down every size you graded, holds each checkpoint against the number the pattern states, and prints the arithmetic that produced the disagreement — the row, the size, the count it made and the count you wrote. It does not design patterns, it does not grade patterns, it will not generate a size run for you, and it never edits your file. It does not tell you a pattern is error-free, and the words do not appear in it: what it reports is that every line it could parse, it reconciled, with the UNCHECKED count printed on the face of the report. The decision to publish stays yours.
  • UNCHECKED is not a pass, and neither is silence. Where a line is in a shape the app does not model, GANSEY counts it and says so rather than assuming it was fine. Hand it a document with no knitting instructions in it — front matter, a diary entry, an image-only export — and it returns “No verdict — nothing to reconcile” and names the two likely causes, instead of a green verdict over nothing. Constructions it declines to guess at, such as AT THE SAME TIME, three charts across one row and alternating intervals, are reported as unchecked with a count.
  • Yardage is an estimate. The yardage check works from yards per stitch and flags a size run whose yarn requirement does not progress with the garment. It is not a substitute for a swatch and the app says so where it prints the figure.
  • What is free and what is Pro. The reconciliation itself is free for ever: every check, every size, every finding with its arithmetic shown, the accept-as-intentional decisions, the coverage and UNCHECKED counts, the worked example, the on-device self-test, both themes, a shelf holding three patterns with their last three audits each including the re-audit diff, and all three exports, never gated, now or ever. Pro adds an unlimited shelf, forty dated audits per pattern instead of three, chart reconciliation, the house dictionary answering abbreviations your key omits, and the printable ledger. Pro never buys a more accurate number — the engine is the same engine on both tiers, and a free report says on its face that the chart was not reconciled.
  • Subscription terms. GANSEY Pro is $27.99 a year and opens with a 21-day free introductory offer (annual plan only, offered once per store account), or $4.99 a month with no trial, or $79.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next charge and the period you have paid for runs to its end. We cannot cancel it for you, and the app will not ask you to stay.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted, hidden or truncated. Every pattern on the shelf stays readable and stays exportable, and an audit history built to forty stays at forty — the shelf never trims below what you already had. What stops is filing a fourth new pattern, reconciling a new chart, the house dictionary and the printable ledger. A fourth pattern still audits in full and still exports; it is simply not filed. A lapse lowers a ceiling on new work; it never takes a record away.
  • Prove it. Open the Prove-it screen and GANSEY re-runs ten cases whose answers are known on your own handset, showing its answers beside the expected ones, so you can check the instrument rather than trust it.
  • Does it need a signal? No. Every parse, count and reconciliation is arithmetic on your own document, so the app opens, reads, audits and exports with the network switched off — which is also why an unpublished pattern never leaves the machine.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.

Sources & attribution

GANSEY’s engine was measured against an answer key written before any application code existed, and the numbers are published rather than summarised. The key holds 73 real corrections published by knitwear designers and yarn houses — third-party material cited by URL only, with no text reproduced anywhere in the app or its harness — of which 35 are decidable from the document alone and carry authored reconstructions; the other 38 (52.1%) are not decidable without the yarn or the designer and are excluded by construction. Alongside them sit 48 generated corrected patterns, five hand-authored hard cases and 500 frozen mutations across twelve defect types. On the last measured run the engine met all four ranked thresholds: 100.0% recall on every one of the twelve defect types (500 of 500), 0.00 false flags per 100 corrected patterns (none over 53), 100.00% parser coverage of 21,271 instruction lines, and 97.4% recall on the decidable errata subset. A null baseline recorded first scores 28.8% overall and 0.0% on nine of the twelve types. Three limits are printed rather than hidden. The single errata miss is named on the record: a correction that rescales an entire yardage run by one factor leaves the grading intact, and deciding it needs the yarn rather than the document. The parser’s 100% coverage is measured over a corpus this build wrote, so a separate wild diagnostic runs 46 lines in shapes the generator never emits and scores 41 of 46 (89.1%), the five declined being the genuinely ambiguous constructions, all of which the app reports as UNCHECKED with a count rather than guessing. And two corrections to the frozen key are disclosed in the harness itself: one defect found by reading before any checker existed, and two hand-authored cases in which the engine was right and the cases were wrong — the cases were corrected, not the engine. No third-party pattern text, no erratum text, no yarn-brand logo and no yarn photography is reproduced or bundled anywhere in the app.
