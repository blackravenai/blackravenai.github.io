Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-09-06

HUNDREDWATT is a rule-resolution instrument for small e-commerce sellers who ship goods containing lithium batteries, made by BlackRaven AI LLC. It runs entirely on your device. There is no account and no sign-in, and we designed it to need as little of your information as possible.

The short version

  • No account, no login, no name or email required.
  • Your data is saved on your device, not on our servers.
  • We don't run our own analytics or tracking.
  • HUNDREDWATT has no server of its own, and it makes no network request of any kind. The published tables it reasons over — USPS Publication 52 and 49 CFR 173.185, both United States Government works — are transcribed into the app itself, about six kilobytes of thresholds carrying the section, the figure and the date each was read. Everything else is arithmetic on the parcel you described: the watt-hour working, the section that governs it, the marks and labels that section requires, and the to-scale drawing of your box face, all computed locally, with the network switched off and the handset in aeroplane mode if you like. There is no artificial-intelligence service behind it and nothing is uploaded — not the product, not the customer, not the parcel. The shipped bundle was checked for network primitives and contains none at all: no fetch, no XMLHttpRequest, no WebSocket, no sendBeacon and no EventSource. The only exception is buying or restoring a subscription: that is handled by Apple’s or Google’s own purchase system, and the receipt it returns is checked by our subscription provider (RevenueCat) so the app knows whether Pro is active. That check carries an anonymous store purchase identifier and the receipt — never your name, your products, your buyers or anything you typed.
  • HUNDREDWATT shows no ads. Store versions may offer purchases (via RevenueCat and the app store); those partners have their own privacy policies, linked below.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or photos. We have no user accounts and no server-side profile of you. The only time we see your email is if you choose to email our support address.

Data stored on your device

Your shipment book (each parcel’s name, the figures you entered for it — chemistry, configuration, watt-hours or grams of lithium, cell count, gross and net mass, carrier service, condition and box-face dimensions — together with the determination that resulted, the section it cites, the marks and labels it names, every citation with the date its source was read, and the threshold values in force at the time), your edited thresholds and source dates, and your app settings are stored locally on your device, in the app’s local storage under the key hundredwatt.save.v1. You can export the whole book as JSON and as CSV at any time, on any tier, including after a Pro plan ends — your own records are never gated. None of it is uploaded or transmitted by the app, and deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising

HUNDREDWATT does not show ads and does not include any advertising SDK.

Purchases

If HUNDREDWATT offers in-app purchases, the transaction is handled by the app store (Apple App Store or Google Play) and processed for us by RevenueCat, which manages entitlements and receipts. We never see or store your payment card details. RevenueCat may process a purchase/receipt identifier to unlock what you bought and restore it across your devices. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy, the Apple Privacy Policy, and the Google Privacy Policy.

Analytics

HUNDREDWATT does not include our own third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. Any limited, aggregated diagnostics you receive come from the app stores' standard reporting (for example crash counts), which is governed by Apple's and Google's policies, not ours.

Children

HUNDREDWATT is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will address it.

Your choices and rights

Because we don't hold a personal profile of you, most control lives on your device: you can clear the app's local data. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA over data our purchase partners process; you can exercise those directly with those partners (linked above) or contact us and we'll help point you to the right place.

International users

BlackRaven AI LLC operates the app and support from Wyoming, United States. Our purchase partners may process data in other countries under their own safeguards.
