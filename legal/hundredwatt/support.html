Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What HUNDREDWATT does, and what it does not decide. You describe the parcel you have packed. HUNDREDWATT names the published section that governs it — a USPS Publication 52 section or a paragraph of 49 CFR 173.185 — states the verdict that section produces, lists the marks and labels the section requires, and draws them on your box face at the size the rule states, each one tappable to the words that require it. It shows the watt-hour arithmetic on the numbers you entered rather than assigning a rating. It never says a shipment is compliant, certified, approved, permitted or safe to ship, and it never tells you that you may ship. The carrier, the regulator and your own responsibility as offeror govern, and every screen says so.
  • It will not write your shipper’s declaration. Where a document is required, HUNDREDWATT names it and cites the rule that requires it. It does not generate, pre-fill or sign a declaration for dangerous goods.
  • United States domestic movement only. The IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations and the Battery Shipping Regulations are licensed publications, and no figure in this product comes from any of them. Choose an international lane and the app returns NOT COVERED, names ICAO Technical Instructions, the IATA DGR and your carrier’s own international guide, and computes nothing. That answer is on the free tier, because charging for the words “not covered” would be a con.
  • Where the figures come from, and how to check them. Every threshold is transcribed from USPS Publication 52 or 49 CFR 173.185, carries its section and the date it was read, and is an editable default with a line asking you to verify it against the current publication. Two fee figures used only in the app’s own cost arithmetic and Sources screen — a carrier’s undeclared-dangerous-goods assessment and a postal hazmat noncompliance fee — have no government source, are marked as such, and never enter a determination.
  • Two publications disagree, on purpose. 49 CFR 173.185(c)(1)(iv) raises the ground ceiling where Publication 52 does not follow it, so the identical battery can be excepted by highway and not accepted at the post office counter. HUNDREDWATT keeps the two tables apart and tells you which one you are standing in rather than averaging them into one comfortable answer.
  • Excluded by policy and by design. Vape and e-cigarette hardware, and damaged, defective, recalled or swollen cells (49 CFR 173.185(f)), are named on the surface as outside what this app resolves.
  • What is free and what is Pro. Free forever: the whole USPS domestic determination with every citation and source date, the watt-hour working, the address panel and its marks, the international NOT COVERED answer, the Prove-it screen, ten saved parcels, and CSV and JSON export of everything you own. Pro adds US ground and US domestic air resolved under 49 CFR 173.185, the print-ready mark sheet at true measured scale, a shipment book with no ceiling, and “What changed” — a re-solve of your whole book against a figure that has moved. Pro never buys a more accurate answer; the engine is the same engine on both tiers.
  • Subscription terms. HUNDREDWATT Pro is $33.99 a year and opens with a 21-day free introductory offer (annual plan only, offered once per store account), or $6.99 a month with no trial, or $99.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next charge and the period you have paid for runs to its end. We cannot cancel it for you, and the app will not ask you to stay.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted, hidden or truncated. Every parcel in the book stays readable, editable, deletable and exportable, however many there are. What stops is saving an eleventh new record, the 49 CFR service lanes, the true-scale mark sheet and “What changed”. A lapse lowers a ceiling on new work; it never takes a record away. Erasing everything in Settings also leaves your subscription intact.
  • Prove it. Open the Prove-it screen and HUNDREDWATT re-runs ten published rows on your own handset, showing its answers beside the expected ones, and it re-runs them at your edited figures and says so when they differ from the cited defaults.
  • Does it need a signal at the packing bench? No. There is no network code in the app at all, so it opens, resolves, draws and exports with the network switched off.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.
