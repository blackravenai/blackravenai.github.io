Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What is free and what is Pro. The instrument is free forever: the whole layout solver at full accuracy, every bay angle, both bundled rule sets and any rule set you edit, obstacles, entrances, the whole accessible-stall placement, THE PLATE at true scale, the complete setting-out sheet on screen, the paint take-off, the “Prove it” self-test, every export (sheet text, mark-offset CSV, lot-book CSV and whole-save JSON, plus restore), and one lot kept in the book. Pro adds the book itself — an unlimited number of measured lots — plus the re-stripe schedule sorted by what is due, and the arrangement comparison that lays every angle side by side with its stall count, accessible count, linear feet and gallons. Pro never buys a more accurate number: the solver is the same solver and the tables are the same tables on both tiers, and export is never gated.
  • Subscription terms. HAIRPIN Pro is $34.99 a year and opens with a 21-day free introductory offer (annual plan only, offered once per store account), or $5.99 a month with no trial, or $99.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next charge and the period you have paid for runs to its end.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted or hidden. Every lot you measured on Pro still opens, still solves, still draws its plate, still prints its setting-out sheet and still exports as CSV and as JSON on the free tier, including books far longer than the free tier would let you build today. What stops is saving a new lot beyond the first, the re-stripe schedule and the arrangement comparison. A lapse lowers a ceiling on new work; it never takes a record away.
  • How the layout is solved. HAIRPIN searches the layout rather than filling stalls left to right: orientation, bay angle, first-band offset and module sequence, with interlock-correct band depths and obstacle-aware interval packing, then places the accessible stalls by shortest accessible route to the entrance you marked and hatches their access aisles. It gives you back a stall count, an accessible count, the drive-aisle widths and a setting-out sheet of chalk-and-string distances measured from two datum corners, so you can snap the first line from the sheet alone.
  • Where the rules come from. The dimension tables shipped with the app are cited defaults that you select and may edit, and every rule panel names its source and the date it was transcribed. HAIRPIN reports what it computes and which table cell it used. Local code and the engineer of record govern the lot — that line sits on the plate and on every export, and it is not decoration.
  • Prove it. The app carries a self-test whose right answers are known exactly. Open the “Prove it” screen and HAIRPIN re-runs the table lookups and re-solves the shipped cases on your own handset and shows its answers against the known ones, so you can check the instrument rather than trust it. Two published table figures disagree with the geometry derived from their own components, and the app names both on that screen and ships the wider published figure.
  • The book and the re-stripe cycle. Every lot you mark as painted is saved with the layout as painted, so the same sheet reprints in three years. Each lot carries its own re-stripe cycle, three years by default, and the schedule sorts your book by what is due next and says how many months it has been since the last coat.
  • What it will not do. HAIRPIN is a geometry and counting instrument. It does not inspect, certify or approve anything, it produces no traffic-engineering, drainage, slope or structural output, and it does not measure your lot for you — it lays out the dimensions you typed. Rectangles and L-shaped lots, rectangular obstacles, boundary entrances and 45, 60, 75 and 90 degree bays are what it covers; tracing from a photograph, curved bays and split-angle bays are outside it and are not claimed.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.
