Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-09-06

STUDCOUNT is a parts-list reconciliation tool for designers who publish brick-compatible building instructions, made by BlackRaven AI LLC. It runs entirely on your device. There is no account and no sign-in, and we designed it to need as little of your information as possible.

The short version

  • No account, no login, no name or email required.
  • Your data is saved on your device, not on our servers.
  • We don't run our own analytics or tracking.
  • STUDCOUNT has no server of its own. The parts catalogue it checks against ships inside the app, and the model file and the parts list you pick are parsed, expanded and reconciled on the device — the app makes no network request at all while you work, and it works with the network switched off. The only exception is buying or restoring a subscription: that is handled by Apple’s or Google’s own purchase system, and the receipt it returns is checked by our subscription provider (RevenueCat) so the app knows whether Pro is active. That check carries an anonymous store purchase identifier and the receipt — never your models, your parts lists, your releases or anything you typed.
  • STUDCOUNT shows no ads. Store versions may offer purchases (via RevenueCat and the app store); those partners have their own privacy policies, linked below.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or photos. We have no user accounts and no server-side profile of you. The only time we see your email is if you choose to email our support address.

Data stored on your device

The files you pick are read into memory, reconciled and then dropped: STUDCOUNT never stores a model file or a parts list and never uploads one. What is saved on your device is the audit — your releases and their versions (the expanded model totals, the parsed list totals, the findings, the unchecked counts, the catalogue snapshot date and the file names and sizes you audited), your house defaults (declared spares and substitutions) and your app settings, held in the app’s local storage. You can export any of it — or all of it — as a printable report, a findings CSV, a full reconcile CSV or a JSON of the whole shelf at any time, on any tier. None of it is uploaded or transmitted by the app, and deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising

STUDCOUNT does not show ads and does not include any advertising SDK.

Purchases

If STUDCOUNT offers in-app purchases, the transaction is handled by the app store (Apple App Store or Google Play) and processed for us by RevenueCat, which manages entitlements and receipts. We never see or store your payment card details. RevenueCat may process a purchase/receipt identifier to unlock what you bought and restore it across your devices. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy, the Apple Privacy Policy, and the Google Privacy Policy.

Analytics

STUDCOUNT does not include our own third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. Any limited, aggregated diagnostics you receive come from the app stores' standard reporting (for example crash counts), which is governed by Apple's and Google's policies, not ours.

Children

STUDCOUNT is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will address it.

Your choices and rights

Because we don't hold a personal profile of you, most control lives on your device: you can clear the app's local data. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA over data our purchase partners process; you can exercise those directly with those partners (linked above) or contact us and we'll help point you to the right place.

International users

BlackRaven AI LLC operates the app and support from Wyoming, United States. Our purchase partners may process data in other countries under their own safeguards.

Changes to this policy
