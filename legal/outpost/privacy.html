Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-07-08

OUTPOST is a space-mission planning tool made by BlackRaven AI LLC. It runs on your device. We designed it to need as little of your information as possible — there are no accounts and no sign-in.

The short version

  • No account, no login, no name or email required.
  • Your data is saved on your device, not on our servers.
  • We don't run our own analytics or tracking.
  • OUTPOST shows no ads. Store versions may offer purchases (via RevenueCat and the app store); those partners have their own privacy policies, linked below.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or photos. We have no user accounts and no server-side profile of you. The only time we see your email is if you choose to email our support address.

Data stored on your device

Your missions, concepts, simulation settings, advisor conversation, and preferences are stored locally on your device (in the app's local storage). This data stays on your device, is not transmitted to us, and can be removed at any time by deleting items in the app, clearing the app's data, or uninstalling the app. You can export a backup of your own data from the app's Settings whenever you like.

Optional AI features

OUTPOST includes an optional AI advisor. It is off until you configure an engine, and you choose where it runs:

  • Local model: if you point OUTPOST at a model server running on your own machine or network (such as Ollama), your advisor messages go only to that server. Nothing leaves your network and we never see it.
  • Cloud model (your own key): if you enter your own API key, your advisor messages (and the workspace context you ask about) are sent directly from your device to that provider — for example Anthropic, under the Anthropic Privacy Policy — to generate the response. There is no OUTPOST server in between, and we never see your messages or your key.

Your API key is stored only on your device, is used only to call the provider you chose, and can be removed at any time in Settings. If you never configure an engine, no advisor data leaves your device at all.

Advertising

OUTPOST does not show ads and does not include any advertising SDK.

Purchases

If OUTPOST offers in-app purchases, the transaction is handled by the app store (Apple App Store or Google Play) and processed for us by RevenueCat, which manages entitlements and receipts. We never see or store your payment card details. RevenueCat may process a purchase/receipt identifier to unlock what you bought and restore it across your devices. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy, the Apple Privacy Policy, and the Google Privacy Policy.

Analytics

OUTPOST does not include our own third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. Any limited, aggregated diagnostics you receive come from the app stores' standard reporting (for example crash counts), which is governed by Apple's and Google's policies, not ours.

Children

OUTPOST is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will address it.

Your choices and rights

Because we don't hold a personal profile of you, most control lives on your device: you can clear the app's local data. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA over data our purchase partners process; you can exercise those directly with those partners (linked above) or contact us and we'll help point you to the right place.

International users

BlackRaven AI LLC operates the app and support from Wyoming, United States. Our purchase partners may process data in other countries under their own safeguards.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy we'll change the date below and post the new version at this URL. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
