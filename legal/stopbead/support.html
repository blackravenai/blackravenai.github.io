Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What STOPBEAD does, and what it does not decide. STOPBEAD reads a charted pattern back out of the file you are about to sell and holds its three views — the bead grid, the legend and the word chart — against each other, in the stitch you charted in. It recounts every cell of every colour, checks the word chart row by row against the lattice, checks the materials list and the stated dimensions against the recount, and prints the arithmetic behind every flag. It does not design patterns, does not correct a bead code, and never edits your file. It makes no claim that a pattern is correct or fit for sale — it reports what it found and what it could not read, and the decision to publish stays yours.
  • UNCHECKED is not a pass. Where a cell, a page or a whole tile cannot be read, STOPBEAD counts it and prints the count on the face of the report rather than quietly assuming it was fine. Grid-read coverage is shown as a percentage on every audit. Text and CSV charts and vector PDFs are read; a pattern that exists only as a scanned picture cannot be read here, there is no character recognition inside the app, and none is promised.
  • Where the gram and size figures come from. Beads-per-gram and bead-pitch figures come from published bead-retailer tables, cited on the report and in the app’s design record — tables whose own publishers describe them as approximations. Every constant is a default you can edit, and every figure is printed with its band and with the table and defaults that produced it. Gram and size results are estimates, and STOPBEAD says so where it prints them.
  • Bead codes. Codes are validated for form and range only, and are never silently corrected. No bead-brand pattern, swatch, logo or photograph is bundled anywhere in the app, and the sample patterns behind the Prove-it screen are the harness’s own designs.
  • What is free and what is Pro. The instrument is free for ever: audit anything, unlimited times, with all nine checks, the full arithmetic, the coverage and UNCHECKED counts, THE STRIP and the split view, the working behind every flag, re-audit after a fix, every constant editable, both themes, three releases with their whole version chain, and all three exports, never gated. Pro adds an unlimited Tray, the re-audit diff (what the fix cleared, what is still open and what the fix introduced), batch audit across a back catalogue, and the recurrence count that shows which checks keep flagging across your Tray. Pro never buys a more accurate recount — the engine is the same engine on both tiers.
  • Subscription terms. STOPBEAD Pro is $29.99 a year and opens with a 21-day free introductory offer (annual plan only, offered once per store account), or $4.99 a month with no trial, or $79.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next charge and the period you have paid for runs to its end. We cannot cancel it for you, and the app will not ask you to stay.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted, hidden or truncated. Every release, every version and every flag stays readable and stays exportable, however many there are, and a diff computed while Pro was active stays yours to read. What stops is starting a fourth release, opening a new diff, batch auditing and the recurrence count. A lapse lowers a ceiling on new work; it never takes a record away.
  • Prove it. Open the Prove-it screen and STOPBEAD re-runs bundled cases whose answers are known on your own handset, showing its answers beside the expected ones, so you can check the instrument rather than trust it.
  • Does it need a signal at the bench? No. Every parse, recount and reconciliation is arithmetic on your own file, so the app opens, reads, audits and exports with the network switched off — which is also why an unreleased pattern never leaves the machine.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.
