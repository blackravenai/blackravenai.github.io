Support

Last updated 2026-09-29

Need help with Ventward? You're in the right place. BlackRaven AI LLC makes Ventward, and we read every message.

Contact us

Email support@blackravenai.com and we'll help. We aim to reply within 2–3 business days. To speed things up, tell us your device and OS version and describe what happened.

Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What ads Ventward shows. Family-safe, non-personalised ads from Google AdMob, requested with child-directed treatment and a G content rating for every player. An optional short video can double what the ridge earned while you were away, or re-arm the SURGE once per ridge — skipping it never costs you anything. An occasional short ad may appear after every fourth WHALEFALL, after the reward screen — never in the Daily Dive and never in your first two sessions. Tap AD PRIVACY CHOICES in Settings to review ad consent where the law provides it.
  • Who is playing, and the grown-up check. On its second launch Ventward asks who is playing. Buying, restoring and ad-privacy choices sit behind a short grown-up check. Settings › CHANGE PLAYER asks again.
  • No Ads, Ever and THE DEEP LANTERN. No Ads, Ever is a one-time $3.99 purchase that removes every ad; the boosts you could watch for become free. THE DEEP LANTERN is a one-time $5.99 purchase that doubles everything the ridge earns, forever, and includes No Ads, Ever (US prices; the store shows your local price). There are no subscriptions.
  • Nothing paid is random. There are no loot boxes and no currency packs. Nodules are earned by playing; bones, seamounts and species can only be earned.
  • Earnings while you are away. The ridge keeps earning for up to 8 hours from the start (up to 24 with DEEP ROOTS and a Deep Current). The welcome-back card shows how long you were away and what it paid.
  • How do I play? Tap a vent. The ? button shows the seven-picture Field Guide any time, and Settings › REPLAY THE FIRST DIVE brings the tapping hand back.
  • Restore purchases. Open Settings and tap RESTORE PURCHASES; purchases are re-checked with the store.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.
