Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What is free and what is Pro. The whole federal layer is free forever, quoted, cited and dated, and so is the verdict on every specimen — whichever layer decides it. A refusal is never held back at any tier. Free also includes the weight arithmetic, the full corpus browser with all its rules and jurisdictions, notes on any corpus row, the Prove-it screen, 5 saved records, and unlimited CSV and JSON export. Pro adds the 21-jurisdiction state layer’s own wording on findings that do not refuse, the destination and marketplace layer, the provenance-statement wording your listing can carry, unlimited records, the correlation panel’s cross-set filter and corpus-version breakdown, and dated corrections to a corpus row. Pro never buys a different verdict.
  • Subscription terms. COUNTRYROCK Pro is $29.99 a year and opens with a 21-day free trial (annual plan only, offered once per store account), or $5.99 a month with no trial, or $79.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next charge and the period you have paid for runs to its end.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted or hidden. Every record you saved stays readable, editable, deletable and exportable in full by both CSV and JSON — including records saved past the free five, and including the citations that were held in Pro at the moment you solved them. A lapse lowers a ceiling on new records; it never takes a record away.
  • COUNTRYROCK is not legal advice and does not tell you what you may do. It reports what the public rules it quotes say about the class, origin, documentation and destination you entered, and it shows you the rule’s own words, its citation and the date that copy was read. It does not decide your case, does not speak for any agency, evaluates no permit, and computes no foreign country’s export law. The land-managing agency and the destination jurisdiction govern; the app does not.
  • Where the rules come from, and how stale they can be. The Paleontological Resources Preservation Act (16 U.S.C. ch. 1C); 43 CFR Part 49; 36 CFR Part 291; 43 CFR Subpart 3622; 43 CFR § 8365.1-5; 36 CFR Parts 261 and 2, all from the eCFR at a stated issue date; BLM Instruction Memorandum 2012-182; the Forest Service’s and Park Service’s own published worked examples; 21 state jurisdictions each with its citation and read date; eBay’s artifacts policy; and two public-record precedent rows. Every verdict on screen carries the citation and the date the text was read, so you can always see how old it is. Rules change between our releases. Verify against the current rule before you publish a listing.
  • Why some answers are UNKNOWN. 21 of 51 US jurisdictions are transcribed. The other 30 return UNKNOWN, which is a deliberate first-class answer rather than a silent gap — the app will not guess a state rule it has not read. The corpus browser tells you on its face which jurisdictions are present.
  • Prove it. The app carries a self-test you can run on your own handset: ten cases from the answer key, re-run on your device against the same engine the development harness tests, showing the expected and the measured verdict side by side. Nothing is fetched and nothing is cached.
  • The corpus is yours to correct. Every rule row is an editable, dated default. Notes are free at every tier; a dated correction to the quoted text or the citation is a Pro feature, and once made it is carried into every future solve and into your exports.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.
