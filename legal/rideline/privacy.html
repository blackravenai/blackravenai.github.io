Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-09-28

RideLine is a motorcycle route planner made by BlackRaven AI LLC: tell it the ride you want and it plans two or three paved, scored routes you can send to your nav. There is no account and no sign-in, and we designed it to need as little of your information as possible.

The short version

  • No account, no login, no name or email required.
  • Your data is saved on your device, not on our servers.
  • We don't run our own analytics or tracking.
  • Planning a ride needs our route engine. When you tap RIDE, the app sends the map point you are planning from, the style and length you picked (or the words you typed) to RideLine’s server, which returns routes and does not keep the request. Typed requests are counted against the free monthly allowance using a random ID the app makes on your phone — not your name, device identifier or advertising ID. If typed-request understanding is switched on, the words you typed (never your location history) are processed by our AI provider, Anthropic, to read the request; Anthropic does not use API data to train its models. Map tiles are loaded from OpenFreeMap. Saved rides, recorded rides and your progress never leave the phone unless you share them.
  • RideLine shows no ads. Store versions may offer purchases (via RevenueCat and the app store); those partners have their own privacy policies, linked below.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or photos. We have no user accounts and no server-side profile of you. The only time we see your email is if you choose to email our support address.

Data stored on your device

Your saved routes, the rides you record (the GPS track, time and distance), GPX files you import, your ride verdicts, your rider level, badges and stats, your nav-device choice and your settings are stored locally on your device in the app’s database. Your phone’s own iCloud or Android backup may carry that database to a new phone; we never see it. Location is used only while the app is open — to start a plan where you are and to record a ride you start — and a recording is kept only on the phone. When you tap Share, a public link is created holding only that route’s line, name and stats (no name, no device, no speeds, no times). A thumbs up or down on a ride sends that verdict with the route’s stats and a coarse sample of its line, with no identifier, so RideLine learns which roads are great. Deleting a ride deletes it from the phone; deleting the app deletes everything.

Advertising

RideLine does not show ads and does not include any advertising SDK.

Purchases

If RideLine offers in-app purchases, the transaction is handled by the app store (Apple App Store or Google Play) and processed for us by RevenueCat, which manages entitlements and receipts. We never see or store your payment card details. RevenueCat may process a purchase/receipt identifier to unlock what you bought and restore it across your devices. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy, the Apple Privacy Policy, and the Google Privacy Policy.

Analytics

RideLine does not include our own third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. Any limited, aggregated diagnostics you receive come from the app stores' standard reporting (for example crash counts), which is governed by Apple's and Google's policies, not ours.

Children

RideLine is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will address it.

Your choices and rights

Because we don't hold a personal profile of you, most control lives on your device: you can clear the app's local data. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA over data our purchase partners process; you can exercise those directly with those partners (linked above) or contact us and we'll help point you to the right place.

International users

BlackRaven AI LLC operates the app and support from Wyoming, United States. Our purchase partners may process data in other countries under their own safeguards.
