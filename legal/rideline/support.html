Support

Last updated 2026-09-28

Need help with RideLine? You're in the right place. BlackRaven AI LLC makes RideLine, and we read every message.

Contact us

Email support@blackravenai.com and we'll help. We aim to reply within 2–3 business days. To speed things up, tell us your device and OS version and describe what happened.

Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What is free. Unlimited rides from the style and time chips, sending routes to Garmin, phone apps and Google Maps, share links, saved rides, ride recording and GPX import are free, with no ads and no trial. Typed plain-English requests are free up to 10 a month and reset on the 1st.
  • Founder Lifetime. A single one-time purchase ($99 in the US; the store shows your local price) that unlocks unlimited typed requests and every Pro feature RideLine ships later, for the life of the app. It is not a subscription: nothing renews and there is nothing to cancel. Restore it from the menu (the three lines at the top) › Restore purchase. Founder spots are limited to 1,000.
  • My Garmin changed the route. Send the ride with the Garmin option: the file holds the exact line as a TRACK (which the device never re-plans) and a ROUTE with dense shaping points. Ride the track, or set your Zumo’s recalculation to Off or Prompted, and check the device shows about the same distance as RideLine. Copying the file to the Zumo’s SD card keeps every point.
  • Google Maps looks different. Google Maps cannot open GPX files, so RideLine sends 9 stops along the route and Google chooses the roads between them. Use a GPX app for the exact line.
  • “RideLine doesn’t have roads loaded where you are.” Routing covers the regions listed in that message and grows as more are added. Pan the map into a covered area to plan there.
  • Ride safely. RideLine is a planning aid. Roads, surfaces and closures in map data can be wrong or out of date: ride to the conditions, obey posted signs, and never handle your phone while riding.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.
