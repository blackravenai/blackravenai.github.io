Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What STITCHTALLY does, and what it does not decide. You open the pattern PDF you are about to upload. STITCHTALLY reads the sheet, then runs eleven reconciliations over what it recovered: the hole count on every mated edge against its stated iron pitch, the pitch itself, evenness of spacing, the print test square against the sheet it is printed on, declared sizes, mirrored pieces, tiled-page registration, overlap and duplication, the hardware list against the numbered steps, hardware sizes, and steps that name a piece the pattern does not contain. Each break is reported with the arithmetic behind it. It does not draw patterns, it does not edit your file, and it never renumbers anything. It does not tell you a pattern is error-free, and it makes no claim that a piece will fit, assemble or hold. The decision to publish stays yours.
  • UNCHECKED is not a pass. Where a page is a flattened raster, or where no reading of the sheet decides which edges a step joins, STITCHTALLY counts the check it declined and prints that count on the face of the report rather than assuming the page was fine. Of five real published patterns sampled during development, one shipped as raster with nothing to count and one shipped as vector with no hole marks at all; both were reported unchecked, with the count shown, and no character recognition was attempted or claimed.
  • Which edges are sewn together comes from your own words. The app solves the pairing from the numbered steps under one constraint — a step joins one seam, an edge is sewn once. Where that cannot settle it, it asks you once, with its own proposal pre-selected and every candidate edge listed with its hole count and its length. It never guesses. Every answer you give is kept in the seam book and applied automatically to any later audit that raises the same two pieces, on a re-audit or on the next release that reuses them. The seam book is free forever, listed and clearable in house defaults.
  • What is free and what is Pro. The audit is free forever: any pattern, as often as you like, all eleven reconciliations, the full working behind every finding, the UNCHECKED count, editable house defaults, the seam question and the seam book, a slab of 2 patterns with 2 audits each and the diff between them, the Prove-it screen, and both exports — CSV and JSON — never gated, now or ever. Pro adds a slab with no limit, every audit of a pattern rather than the last two, cross-release statistics, the re-audit queue and the printable findings sheet. Pro never buys a more accurate number — every check and every piece of working is identical on both tiers.
  • Subscription terms. STITCHTALLY Pro is $32.99 a year and opens with a 28-day free introductory offer (annual plan only, offered once per store account), or $5.99 a month with no trial, or $89.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next charge and the period you have paid for runs to its end.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted, hidden or truncated. Every pattern on the slab stays readable and stays exportable, however many there are — a slab built to twenty stays at twenty. What stops is filing a new third record, the cross-release statistics, the re-audit queue and the printed findings sheet. A third pattern still audits in full and still exports; it is simply not filed. A lapse lowers a ceiling on new work; it never takes a record away.
  • Prove it. Open the Prove-it screen and STITCHTALLY re-runs seven patterns whose answers are known, on your own handset, showing its answers beside the expected ones, so you can check the instrument rather than trust it.
  • Does it need a signal? No. Every parse, measurement and reconciliation is arithmetic on your own document, so the app opens, reads, audits and exports with the network switched off — which is also why an unlisted pattern never leaves the machine.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.

Sources & attribution

STITCHTALLY’s engine was measured against an answer key frozen before any engine code existed, and the numbers are published rather than summarised. The key holds 320 authored pattern PDFs carrying 600 planted defects across fifteen known types, with 49 clean patterns held back as a false-flag control. On the last measured run the engine found 595 of 600 (99.17%) with the worst single type at 97.2%, raised no false flag at all over the 49 clean patterns, read 316,386 of 317,091 stitch marks (99.78%) with 0 of 4,868 pages unread, and reproduced all nine cited iron-pitch conventions exactly. A null baseline recorded before the engine existed — counting closed outlines as pieces — scores 0.0% recall on every type, 0.0% coverage, no pitch conversion, and 85.71 false flags per 100 clean patterns. The corpus reproduces byte-identically from its seed and every case file opens under Poppler, an independent PDF implementation this build did not write. Limits are published rather than hidden: the five misses are named individually with their causes; the corpus is synthetic by construction, deliberately randomising compression, fonts, placement operators and label formats so the reader cannot be tuned to one dialect; and no claim of recall against real published leathercraft corrections is made anywhere, because fewer than fifteen such corrections were obtainable. Iron-pitch conversions follow the stitches-per-inch and millimetre conventions published by the irons’ own makers, cited case by case in the app’s design record. No third-party pattern file is bundled or redistributed anywhere in the app or its harness.
