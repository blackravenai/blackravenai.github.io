Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-09-06

STITCHTALLY is a pattern-audit instrument for independent leathercraft designers who sell printable PDF patterns, made by BlackRaven AI LLC. It runs entirely on your device. There is no account and no sign-in, and we designed it to need as little of your information as possible.

The short version

  • No account, no login, no name or email required.
  • Your data is saved on your device, not on our servers.
  • We don't run our own analytics or tracking.
  • STITCHTALLY has no server of its own, and it makes no network request of any kind, on any screen, in any tier. It decompresses and parses the pattern PDF you hand it — the object graph, the compressed object streams, the page tree, the content operators, the font encodings — recovers the punched hole marks, the piece outlines, the spec block, the numbered steps and the hardware list from the page itself, runs eleven reconciliations over them and draws the tally, all of it geometry and arithmetic performed on your own handset, with the network switched off and the phone in aeroplane mode if you like. There is no artificial-intelligence service behind it, and an unreleased pattern of yours is never uploaded anywhere to be read. The shipped bundle carries no advertising SDK. The only exception is buying or restoring a subscription: that is handled by Apple’s or Google’s own purchase system, and the receipt it returns is checked by our subscription provider (RevenueCat) so the app knows whether Pro is active. That check carries an anonymous store purchase identifier and the receipt — never your name, your patterns, your customers or anything you typed.
  • STITCHTALLY shows no ads. Store versions may offer purchases (via RevenueCat and the app store); those partners have their own privacy policies, linked below.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or photos. We have no user accounts and no server-side profile of you. The only time we see your email is if you choose to email our support address.

Data stored on your device

Your slab (each pattern’s name, and every dated audit of it with its findings, the working behind each finding, the recovered seams and hole counts, the coverage and UNCHECKED counts that audit ran with, and the scale and pitch figures it read), your seam book of confirmed edge pairings, your house defaults such as the iron list, and your app settings are stored locally on your device, in the app’s local storage under the key stitchtally.save.v1. You can export the whole slab as JSON and any audit as CSV at any time, on any tier, including after a Pro plan ends — your own records are never gated. None of it is uploaded or transmitted by the app, and deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising

STITCHTALLY does not show ads and does not include any advertising SDK.

Purchases

If STITCHTALLY offers in-app purchases, the transaction is handled by the app store (Apple App Store or Google Play) and processed for us by RevenueCat, which manages entitlements and receipts. We never see or store your payment card details. RevenueCat may process a purchase/receipt identifier to unlock what you bought and restore it across your devices. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy, the Apple Privacy Policy, and the Google Privacy Policy.

Analytics

STITCHTALLY does not include our own third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. Any limited, aggregated diagnostics you receive come from the app stores' standard reporting (for example crash counts), which is governed by Apple's and Google's policies, not ours.

Children

STITCHTALLY is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will address it.

Your choices and rights

Because we don't hold a personal profile of you, most control lives on your device: you can clear the app's local data. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA over data our purchase partners process; you can exercise those directly with those partners (linked above) or contact us and we'll help point you to the right place.

International users

BlackRaven AI LLC operates the app and support from Wyoming, United States. Our purchase partners may process data in other countries under their own safeguards.
