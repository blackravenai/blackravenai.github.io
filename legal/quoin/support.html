Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What is free and what is Pro. The whole solve is free forever, at the same accuracy Pro gets: stretcher bond, one opening, any wall size, the full setting-out elevation, the gauge rod, the cut plan, the take-off, “where the bond breaks”, the comparison against a naive layout, the “Prove it” self-test, both themes, both unit systems, a wall book holding 3 walls, and export of the sheet and of the whole book. Pro adds Flemish, English, English garden wall, header and stack bond; a second and third opening; reverse bond; choosing where the broken bond falls; and an unlimited wall book with the season totalled. Pro never buys a different answer — the solver is the same solver on both tiers, and export is never gated.
  • Subscription terms. QUOIN Pro is $49.99 a year and opens with a 21-day free introductory offer (annual plan only, offered once per store account), or $9.99 a month with no trial, or $139.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next charge and the period you have paid for runs to its end.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted or hidden. Every wall you set out on Pro still opens, still shows its elevation, its rod, its cut plan and its take-off, still exports as a sheet and as JSON, and still deletes on the free tier — including books far longer than the free tier would let you build today, and walls laid in bonds the free tier cannot start. What stops is starting new work beyond the free ceiling. A lapse lowers a ceiling on new work; it never takes a record away.
  • How the setting out works. You enter the wall as it will actually be built — length, courses, brick format, and every opening exactly where the drawing puts it. QUOIN searches the declared decision space (end cuts, where the broken bond falls, which unit starts each course, and how the residual is absorbed) for the layout that reads right: the fewest short laps, then the fewest off-grid cuts, then the fewest cuts, then the smallest joint deviation. Every perp is opened or closed in proportion to the brick it follows, within the tolerance you set and never past it, so the perpends stay plumb up the wall instead of creeping. The gauge rod is found by an exhaustive bed-joint search at 0.25 mm so the courses land on your sills and heads.
  • Where the bond breaks, it says so. An opening whose reveal does not sit on the coordinating grid cannot carry the bond across it. QUOIN tells you how far off the grid each reveal is, puts the cuts where your placement rule says, and rings every lap that comes out under a quarter brick rather than hiding it.
  • Prove it. The app carries ten walls from its own answer key, each with the score an independent brute-force enumerator found for it. Open the “Prove it” screen and the solver on your own handset re-runs all ten and shows its answers against the enumerator’s, so you can check the instrument rather than trust it.
  • What it will not do. QUOIN sets out bond geometry and counts material. It produces no structural output of any kind — no wall thickness, no pier or lintel sizing, no movement joints, no wind loading, no ties — and it does not replace the drawing. The specification, local code and the designer govern. Every brick format, tolerance and rule it ships is a default you select and may edit, not a rule the app asserts.
  • Why a hard wall takes a moment. A garden wall in stretcher bond solves in about 50 milliseconds. A long wall with three openings in Flemish or English bond takes two to five seconds, because the engine deliberately spends far more search when the plan still shows one to three short laps — that band usually means a fault-free plan exists and has not been found yet. The engine carries no clock: every budget is derived from the wall itself, so the same wall solves identically on any device.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.
