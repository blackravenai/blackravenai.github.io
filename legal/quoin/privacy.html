Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-09-04

QUOIN is a setting-out tool for bricklayers, made by BlackRaven AI LLC. It runs entirely on your device. There is no account and no sign-in, and we designed it to need as little of your information as possible.

The short version

  • No account, no login, no name or email required.
  • Your data is saved on your device, not on our servers.
  • We don't run our own analytics or tracking.
  • QUOIN has no server of its own. It solves the perpend layout of your wall, rules the gauge rod, works out the cut plan, takes the material off the drawn wall and writes the setting-out sheet on the device, and it works with the network switched off. There is no artificial-intelligence service behind it and nothing you measure is sent anywhere. The only exception is buying or restoring a subscription: that is handled by Apple’s or Google’s own purchase system, and the receipt it returns is checked by our subscription provider (RevenueCat) so the app knows whether Pro is active. That check carries an anonymous store purchase identifier and the receipt — never your walls, your dimensions, your openings, your take-off or anything you typed.
  • QUOIN shows no ads. Store versions may offer purchases (via RevenueCat and the app store); those partners have their own privacy policies, linked below.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or photos. We have no user accounts and no server-side profile of you. The only time we see your email is if you choose to email our support address.

Data stored on your device

Your walls — their names, the length, the courses and the brick format you chose, every opening with its width, position, sill and head, the bond and the placement rule you set, your joint tolerances, and for each saved wall the cuts, off-grid slivers and short laps QUOIN produced alongside what a naive layout would have produced — together with your app settings are stored locally on your device, in the app’s local storage under the key quoin.save.v2. You can export any of it — or all of it — as a plain-text setting-out sheet or as a complete JSON copy of the wall book at any time, on any tier, including after a Pro plan ends. None of it is uploaded or transmitted by the app, and deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising

QUOIN does not show ads and does not include any advertising SDK.

Purchases

If QUOIN offers in-app purchases, the transaction is handled by the app store (Apple App Store or Google Play) and processed for us by RevenueCat, which manages entitlements and receipts. We never see or store your payment card details. RevenueCat may process a purchase/receipt identifier to unlock what you bought and restore it across your devices. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy, the Apple Privacy Policy, and the Google Privacy Policy.

Analytics

QUOIN does not include our own third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. Any limited, aggregated diagnostics you receive come from the app stores' standard reporting (for example crash counts), which is governed by Apple's and Google's policies, not ours.

Children

QUOIN is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will address it.

Your choices and rights

Because we don't hold a personal profile of you, most control lives on your device: you can clear the app's local data. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA over data our purchase partners process; you can exercise those directly with those partners (linked above) or contact us and we'll help point you to the right place.

International users

BlackRaven AI LLC operates the app and support from Wyoming, United States. Our purchase partners may process data in other countries under their own safeguards.

Changes to this policy
