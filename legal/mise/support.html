Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What is free and what is Pro. The instrument is free forever: one draft kept on the Shelf with its full version history, all twelve checks with the arithmetic shown, THE PASS with its tie-lines, the “Prove it” self-test, single-recipe import, and export as JSON and as a Markdown report. Pro adds the Shelf itself — every draft you write, kept and versioned — plus the cross-recipe pass over a whole book, the house style held across every recipe in it, rulings that carry from one draft to the next, and batch import. Pro never buys a different answer: the twelve checks are the same twelve checks on both sides.
  • Subscription terms. MISE Pro is $59.99 a year and opens with a 30-day free introductory offer (annual plan only, offered once per store account), or $9.99 a month with no trial, or $179.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next charge and the period you have paid for runs to its end.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted or hidden. Every draft you saved on Pro still opens, still checks, still shows its versions, still exports and still deletes on the free tier, including shelves far longer than the free tier would let you build today. What stops is keeping a new draft beyond the first, the cross-recipe pass, the held house style and batch import. A lapse lowers a ceiling on new work; it never takes a record away.
  • How the check works. MISE reads the two sides of a recipe — the ingredient list and the method — as two separate documents, then binds every step back to the listed line it acts on. Twelve check families run over that binding: ingredients used but never listed, listed but never used, quantities that disagree between the two sides, component sums that do not add up, name drift, scaling that was applied to some lines and not others, times that do not sum to the stated total, temperature pairs where the Fahrenheit and the Celsius are not the same temperature, weight-and-volume pairs that contradict a cited density, missing preheats, equipment used but never listed, and yields that do not fit the pan.
  • Prove it. The app carries ten synthetic test drafts whose faults are known exactly. Open Settings › Prove it and MISE re-checks all ten on your handset and shows its answers against the known ones, so you can check the instrument rather than trust it.
  • What it will not do. MISE checks a recipe against itself. It does not cook the dish, it does not know whether the dish is good, and it does not give nutrition, health or food-safety advice. It does not promise a recipe is free of error — it reports the disagreements it can find in the text and shows you the arithmetic behind each one. A servings count that nothing in the text constrains is one of the shapes it will miss, and the app says so.
  • Where the numbers come from. Every conversion, density band and pan volume MISE uses is a cited constant, and each finding opens to the working: the two figures, the operation between them and the line numbers they came from. You are meant to overrule it. A flag you accept as written is recorded, and on Pro it stops being raised on your next draft.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.
