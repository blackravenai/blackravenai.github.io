Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What is free and what is Pro. The instrument is free forever: unlimited captures at full accuracy, the complete element breakdown, the section drawn at true 1:1, the printable 1:1 sheet with its check bar, the R12 DXF, the “Prove it” screen, and three sections kept on the shelf. Pro adds the shelf itself — an unlimited number of kept sections, geometry search, the ghost overlay that lays one section over another, and filing by house and room. Pro never buys a more accurate number, and export is never gated on any tier.
  • Subscription terms. Pro is $39.99 a year and opens with a 21-day free trial (annual plan only, offered once per account), or $6.99 a month with no trial, or $119.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next charge and the period you have paid for runs to its end.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted or hidden. Every section you kept on Pro still opens, still draws at 1:1, still prints, still exports to DXF and still deletes on the free tier, including shelves far longer than the free tier would let you build today. What stops is keeping a fourth new section, the geometry search and the ghost overlay. A lapse lowers a ceiling on new work; it never takes a record away.
  • How the measurement is made. CYMA calibrates off the gauge’s own geometry — the 1.00 mm pitch of the pins and their constant length — and rectifies two planes rather than one, because the pin tips stand several millimetres out of the plane of the gauge body. It then segments the pin staircase by curvature and fits real lines and arcs to it, so a cove comes back as an arc with a radius rather than a traced bitmap outline.
  • Prove it. The app carries shipped test profiles whose true geometry is known. Open the “Prove it” screen and CYMA re-solves them on your handset and shows its answer against the known one, so you can check the instrument rather than trust it.
  • What it will not do. CYMA reports the spans its pins could not reach instead of drawing through them — undercuts and deep quirks narrower than the pin pitch are broken open in red and labelled, not guessed. Check the section against the moulding before a knife is ground from it.
  • Printing at 1:1. The sheet carries a 100 mm / 4 in check bar. Print at 100% scale with page fitting off and measure the bar before you use the sheet as a template.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.

Sources & attribution

CYMA’s recovered section is checked against an authored answer key of 32 profiles rendered from 6 viewpoints each: over 192 cases the pooled root-mean-square profile error is 0.0303 mm against a 0.30 mm threshold, every case’s worst point is inside 0.4427 mm against a 0.60 mm bound, overall width is within 0.0086 mm, and every DXF round-trips to better than 3e-8 mm with its arcs preserved. Spans the pins cannot resolve are excluded from the comparison and counted on screen. The method, its thresholds, its ablations and every constant are cited line by line in the app’s design record and its answer key.
