Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-09-04

CYMA is a moulding-profile tool for finish and restoration carpenters, made by BlackRaven AI LLC. It runs entirely on your device. There is no account and no sign-in, and we designed it to need as little of your information as possible.

The short version

  • No account, no login, no name or email required.
  • Your data is saved on your device, not on our servers.
  • We don't run our own analytics or tracking.
  • CYMA has no server of its own. It reads the contour gauge in your photograph, rectifies the two planes, fits the lines and arcs, draws the section at 1:1, writes the DXF and the printable sheet and searches your shelf on the device, and it works with the network switched off. The only exception is buying or restoring a subscription: that is handled by Apple’s or Google’s own purchase system, and the receipt it returns is checked by our subscription provider (RevenueCat) so the app knows whether Pro is active. That check carries an anonymous store purchase identifier and the receipt — never your photographs, your sections, your houses or anything you typed.
  • CYMA shows no ads. Store versions may offer purchases (via RevenueCat and the app store); those partners have their own privacy policies, linked below.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or photos. We have no user accounts and no server-side profile of you. The only time we see your email is if you choose to email our support address.

Data stored on your device

The photographs you pick are read into memory on the device and are never written anywhere by the app; only the measurements taken from them are kept. Your sections — their fitted geometry, their pin readings, their width and thickness, the spans the pins could not reach, the capture time and the measured pixels-per-millimetre — together with the house, room and element you file each one under, your notes and your app settings, are stored locally on your device, in the app’s local storage. You can export any of it — or all of it — as an R12 DXF, a 1:1 PDF sheet or a complete JSON backup at any time, on any tier. None of it is uploaded or transmitted by the app, and deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising

CYMA does not show ads and does not include any advertising SDK.

Purchases

If CYMA offers in-app purchases, the transaction is handled by the app store (Apple App Store or Google Play) and processed for us by RevenueCat, which manages entitlements and receipts. We never see or store your payment card details. RevenueCat may process a purchase/receipt identifier to unlock what you bought and restore it across your devices. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy, the Apple Privacy Policy, and the Google Privacy Policy.

Analytics

CYMA does not include our own third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. Any limited, aggregated diagnostics you receive come from the app stores' standard reporting (for example crash counts), which is governed by Apple's and Google's policies, not ours.

Children

CYMA is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will address it.

Your choices and rights

Because we don't hold a personal profile of you, most control lives on your device: you can clear the app's local data. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA over data our purchase partners process; you can exercise those directly with those partners (linked above) or contact us and we'll help point you to the right place.

International users

BlackRaven AI LLC operates the app and support from Wyoming, United States. Our purchase partners may process data in other countries under their own safeguards.

Changes to this policy
