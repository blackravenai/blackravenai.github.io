Need help with HOOKSIDE? You're in the right place. BlackRaven AI LLC makes HOOKSIDE, and we read every message.

Contact us

Email support@blackravenai.com and we'll help. We aim to reply within 2–3 business days. To speed things up, tell us your device and OS version and describe what happened.

Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What is free and what is Pro. The whole solve is free forever — every vehicle class, every lens, unlimited walkarounds and captures, sealing with the SHA-256 manifest and verification, both themes, and every export including sheets past the free window. Free keeps the last 10 sealed sheets open in THE LEDGER. Pro adds the whole ledger with no window, THE RECORD accrual, the retention horizon and rig profiles.
  • Subscription terms. Pro is $49.99 a year with a 21-day free trial (annual only, once per account), or $6.99 a month with no trial, or $99.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price until you cancel; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your App Store or Google Play account under Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the period ends — cancelling stops the next charge and puts the free window back at the end of the period you have paid for.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted or locked. Every sealed sheet stays on the device, stays listed, stays exportable as JSON, CSV or plain text and stays deletable. Pro lowers a window; it never takes a record away.
  • What a sheet is, and is not. A sheet is your own declaration of what you photographed, like a paper condition report. HOOKSIDE does not sense where you are standing and cannot verify that you stood where it sent you.
  • The manifest is not a timestamp authority. The SHA-256 chain is computed on your device. It shows a sealed sheet has not been altered since sealing; it is not a trusted third-party timestamp and we make no legal claim for it.
  • Safety. HOOKSIDE states geometry — where a station falls relative to the traffic side you tell it about. It gives no safety instruction and no legal advice. Your scene assessment and your local law govern; the app does not.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.

Sources & attribution

HOOKSIDE’s stand-off geometry is computed from the standard thin-lens field-of-view relation d = (s/2) / tan(fov/2), with panel visibility scored by surface-normal incidence and occlusion; the method and every constant are cited line by line in the app’s design record. Sheet integrity uses SHA-256 (FIPS 180-4) over a canonical JSON serialisation.
