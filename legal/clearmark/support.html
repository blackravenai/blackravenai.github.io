Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What CLEARMARK does, and does not do. CLEARMARK renders warning text in the format of 27 CCR sections 25601 to 25607 from the entries you make and confirm, and draws that text at the size of the label you tell it about. It does not determine that any product requires a warning, does not require one, or is exempt. It makes no exposure assessment and no measurement of any chemical. It is not legal advice, and it is not a substitute for your own counsel or your own testing.
  • Where the material-to-chemical suggestions come from. The app proposes chemicals commonly associated with the materials you list, each one cited, dated and marked strong or weak, and each one has to be confirmed by you before it is named in any text. A suggestion is a prompt for your judgement, never a finding about your product.
  • What is free and what is Pro. The complete solve is free forever for 2 products — every governing section, every content fork, every citation, all five methods of transmission, all three warning forms, the type floors, the true-scale proof sheet on screen, the Prove It screen, and every export of your own data. Pro adds unlimited products, the SKU book with dated committed snapshots and the re-solve diff, the 2028 sunset audit, and print-ready true-scale artwork. Pro never changes a single character of the answer.
  • Subscription terms. Pro is $29.99 a year and opens with a 21-day free trial (annual plan only, offered once per account), or $4.99 a month with no trial, or $79.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted or locked. Every product, every confirmed association, every measurement and every committed solve stays readable, editable, exportable and deletable, however many there are. The free tier only refuses to add a new product past 2; it never takes a record away.
  • The regulation moves. Every screen and every export carries the date the regulation was read and the date of the chemical list it used. Check them against the current 27 CCR and the current OEHHA list before you order a print run.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.

Sources & attribution

CLEARMARK’s rules are transcribed from 27 CCR sections 25600 to 25607.53 (California Code of Regulations, Title 27, Article 6, Subarticle 2) and the OEHHA Proposition 65 chemical list published under 27 CCR 27001. Those texts are government edicts and are not subject to copyright (Georgia v. Public.Resource.Org, 590 U.S. 255 (2020)). No licensed consensus standard is used anywhere in the app. Each emitted line names the subsection it comes from and the date that subsection was read.
