Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What COURSELINE does, and what it does not decide. COURSELINE searches the exposure band your shingle manufacturer publishes for the product you selected, at or below the nominal exposure and never above it, and returns the exposure that lands the last course under the ridge cap closest to a full course on every plane at once — with the height of every course measured up from the eave and cross-checked back down from the ridge, the horizontal offset for every course, the cut list and the take-off. It plans a layout. It does not approve one, and it makes no structural, load, span or ventilation statement of any kind. The manufacturer’s installation instructions and your local building department govern the roof; check the layout against them before you strike the first line.
  • Where the shipped shingle numbers come from. The default product table carries published manufacturer exposure, headlap, offset and coverage figures, each one editable, and the app reproduces 45 of 45 transcribed figures from the source document it cites. If your bundle wrapper says something different from the table, the wrapper wins — edit the product and re-solve.
  • Prove it. Open the “Prove it” screen and COURSELINE re-runs a set of answer-key roofs on your own handset and shows its answers beside the expected ones, so you can check the instrument rather than trust it. The engine was written against an answer key recorded before any solver code existed: on 300 roofs graded against an independent exhaustive enumeration it equals the optimum 300 times out of 300, and across 1,500 roofs and 5,555 planes it commits 0 violations of 10 layout invariants. Nailing at the nominal exposure instead — the way the tape does it — equals the optimum 0 times out of 300 and commits 38,128 violations on the same set.
  • What is free and what is Pro. The instrument is free forever on one plane: the full exposure solve, every course height from the eave and back from the ridge, every offset, THE DECK drawing with the ridge detail, the squares, waste and bundle count, the first 5 cut pieces with the total stated, 3 roofs in the book, the text and CSV sheet export, the Prove it screen, and Sunlight mode. Pro adds the multi-plane solve on one shared exposure (up to 8 planes), the full cut list, the full take-off and an unlimited roof book. Pro never buys a better answer — it is the same solver on both tiers.
  • Subscription terms. COURSELINE Pro is $54.99 a year and opens with a 21-day free introductory offer (annual plan only, offered once per store account), or $8.99 a month with no trial, or $164.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next charge and the period you have paid for runs to its end.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted, hidden or truncated. Every roof in the book — including the multi-plane roofs you built during a trial — stays readable, editable, re-solvable, deletable and exportable, however many there are, and the complete JSON export of the whole book is never gated at any tier. What stops is adding a new plane beyond the first, the full cut list, the full take-off and adding a fourth roof. A lapse lowers a ceiling on new work; it never takes a record away.
  • Does it need a signal on the deck? No. The product table is bundled with the app and the solve is arithmetic on the handset, so it opens, solves, draws and exports with the phone in aeroplane mode.
  • Measure it yourself. COURSELINE lays out the roof you told it about. It does not measure your roof. Check the eave and the eave-to-ridge on the deck with your own tape before you commit the starter course.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.

Sources & attribution

COURSELINE’s exposure search and per-plane offset assignment are exact integer arithmetic in sixteenths of an inch — a one-sided scan of the manufacturer’s published exposure band combined with a dynamic program over the butt-joint offset sequence — and are documented line by line in the app’s design record. The engine was graded before release against an answer key and a null baseline both recorded before any solver code existed: an independent exhaustive depth-first enumeration of 11,996,426,127 states over 300 roofs, which the shipped engine equals on 300 of 300; 10 layout invariants checked across 1,500 roofs and 5,555 planes, on which it commits 0 violations against the null baseline’s 38,128; and 45 of 45 published manufacturer figures reproduced by the shipped defaults. The manufacturer figures are used as numbers only and no manufacturer document is bundled with the app.
