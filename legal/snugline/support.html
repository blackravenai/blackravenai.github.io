Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What SNUGLINE does, and what it does not do. It compares measurements you take yourself against the dimensional criteria printed in 16 CFR §1615.1(o) and §1616.2(m), quotes those criteria with their section and the date the text was read, and lays out the label wording the regulation prints, at its printed physical size. It does not perform, predict, simulate or replace any laboratory test, it does not tell you whether a garment may be sold, and it does not decide whether a garment is sleepwear in law. Every measurement is your measurement, and the regulator governs.
  • Where the tables came from. Every printed cell was transcribed from the eCFR and the govinfo XML and checked against a third independent source. Each cell ships with its section citation and the date the source was read, and each one is editable: if the regulation moves, you can change the cell yourself, and SNUGLINE records that the value is yours and shows it on every record it touches. Verify against the current CFR — the regulator governs.
  • Why does a size sometimes come back UNRESOLVED? Because SNUGLINE will not guess. The regulation prints every maximum twice, once in centimetres and once in inches, and 162 of the 175 printed pairs are not exact conversions of one another. Where your measurement falls between the two printed figures, or where your size label cannot be mapped to a printed row, SNUGLINE says so rather than picking one.
  • What is free and what is Pro. One style, graded end to end, is free forever — every size, all seven printed dimensions in both printed units, the design criteria, every citation and read date, that style’s own record and diff, the label wording on screen at true scale, and every export (CSV, plain-text record, JSON backup). Pro adds unlimited styles, the style book with re-solve and diff across runs, the three label layouts as print-ready SVG at true physical size, and editable defaults with per-cell provenance.
  • Subscription terms. Pro is $29.99 a year and opens with a 21-day introductory free trial (annual only, once per account), or $5.99 a month with no trial, or $79.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically until you cancel; the lifetime purchase never renews. Cancel any time in your App Store or Google Play account under Subscriptions — cancelling stops the next charge and puts the free limit back.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted or locked. Every style, size, measurement and edited cell stays readable, solvable, editable, deletable and exportable; only the actions above go back behind the gate.
  • Colour. The label colour drawn on screen is a placeholder and is captioned as one. Screens are not colour-managed print devices. Match any printed colour requirement against the specification itself with your printer, not against this screen.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.

Sources & attribution

The printed tables, the design criteria and the label wording SNUGLINE reproduces are quoted from Title 16 of the Code of Federal Regulations, Parts 1615 and 1616, which are works of the United States Government and are not subject to copyright.
