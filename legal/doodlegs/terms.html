The game is free to download and includes ads. Doodads are earned gameplay currency used for in-game unlocks. They have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn or exchanged for money. Availability and game balance may change with updates.

Ad-Free Monthly

The optional auto-renewing subscription removes in-app advertising while your entitlement is active. The US launch price is $4.99 per month. Apple displays the applicable local price, taxes and billing period before you confirm. No subscription is needed for ordinary play.

Manage or cancel in your Apple account’s Subscriptions settings. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription. Restore Purchases checks an existing entitlement; it does not restart an expired subscription.
