Email support@blackravenai.com. Tell us your device, iOS version and what happened. Screenshots help; never send passwords or payment details.

Subscription and restore

Open Settings → Restore Purchases using the Apple account that subscribed. An active entitlement shows “Ads removed — active subscription.” Restore does not renew an expired subscription. Manage billing and cancellation in Apple account settings.

Ad stuck or inappropriate?

Send the advertiser’s name, approximate time and a screenshot including the top corners. If an ad cannot be closed, close and reopen the app. Do not click an advertiser’s link to try to dismiss it. An incomplete reward may not be granted.

Privacy options

Google may report that no additional choices are currently required. A network or status-check error is different; try again when connected or contact us. Our Privacy Policy explains the family-ad settings.

Saves and controls

Change Player updates the age-range choice for the person playing. Erase Save resets local gameplay progress, but does not cancel a subscription. Shapes last six active seconds, or seven with Waxed Ink; the timer and redraw warning help you plan your next shape.
