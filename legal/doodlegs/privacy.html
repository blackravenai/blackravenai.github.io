Privacy Policy

Updated September 24, 2026 · iOS launch version

DoodleLegs (shown in the game as DOODLEGS!) is a drawing-and-running game from BlackRaven AI LLC. This policy explains the iOS version’s local saves, advertising and optional subscription.

Playing without an account

No BlackRaven account is required. Scores, drawings used during play, earned Doodads, unlocks, settings and the selected age range are handled on your device. We do not ask for an exact birth date, contacts or precise location. Game progress is stored locally, including an operating-system preferences copy; device backups may retain it.

Children and age choices

The game supports children and adults. An age-range choice is remembered on the device; use Change Player when someone else plays. Child and unknown-age purchase/restore actions and relevant external controls are parent-gated. A parent gate is not a substitute for legally required parental consent.

Ad requests use conservative child-directed and under-age-of-consent settings for every player, a general-audience content rating, and non-personalized treatment. The app does not request Apple tracking permission or enable personalized advertising. These settings do not mean that ad delivery involves no data processing.

Advertising and diagnostics

Google AdMob and its supporting SDKs may process IP addresses (from which approximate location can be inferred), device/app information, ad interactions and diagnostic or performance information to deliver, measure, secure and troubleshoot ads. We do not enable cross-app tracking or our own gameplay analytics SDK in this release. Apple may provide app-store and crash reporting under its settings and policies.

See Google’s privacy policy and how Google processes information from partner apps. Ads are third-party content; report an inappropriate or uncloseable ad to our support address.

Apple purchases

The optional Ad-Free Monthly subscription uses Apple’s native in-app billing. Apple processes payment and verifies subscription transactions; we do not receive your card details. The app checks verified entitlement information to remove ads and restore access. The RevenueCat client SDK is not started by the native Apple billing path. Where connected in store administration, RevenueCat may receive server-side purchase, renewal, cancellation and refund notifications for subscription administration. See Apple’s privacy policy and RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Your controls

Settings provides Privacy Policy, Terms, Restore Purchases, and Ad Privacy Options. Google determines whether additional privacy choices are required or available for the current request; if none are required, the app explains that instead of showing a form. A failed privacy-status check does not mean consent was granted.

Erase Save removes local gameplay progress; it does not cancel an Apple subscription or erase Apple/Google transaction or advertising records. Manage subscriptions in your Apple account. To request help with data access, correction or deletion, email us. Rights vary by location and we may need enough information to identify the relevant support record.

Contact, retention and updates

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to answer your request. We retain support correspondence as needed to resolve the issue and meet applicable obligations. Please do not send passwords, payment details or a child’s unnecessary personal information. Service providers retain data under their policies. We update this page when our practices change.
