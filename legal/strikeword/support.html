Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What STRIKEWORD does, and what it does not decide. STRIKEWORD resolves the descriptors a construction earns under the rule text it carries, and prints the arithmetic and the citation that decided each one. It never decides whether an article must be hallmarked or assayed, whether a mark is registrable, whether you may sell an article, or whether any description you finally publish is right for your circumstances. It is not advice, and every solve prints in plain words what it could not decide. You own the words you strike into metal and the words you publish — check them against the current source text and your own records before the punch lands.
  • Where the rules come from, and how current they are. Two bodies of rule text are transcribed: the United States Federal Trade Commission’s Guides for the Jewelry, Precious Metals, and Pewter Industries at 16 CFR Part 23, taken from the eCFR issue of 3 September 2026 and fetched on 4 September 2026, and the United Kingdom Hallmarking Act 1973, Schedules 1 and 2 and section 22, taken from legislation.gov.uk and fetched on the same date. Every rung shows its section, the quoted sentence and that fetch date, and every rung carries a line telling you to verify it against the current text. Rules change; a date on the screen is the honest way to say so.
  • Which places does it compute? The United States and the United Kingdom. There is no single European regime to transcribe, so choosing anything else says the app holds no citable rule for that place and computes nothing rather than guessing.
  • What is free and what is Pro. The instrument is free forever: the whole descriptor ladder in either place, one place at a time, with every earned and refused descriptor, the number that decided each one in the gutter, every citation with its quoted text and its fetch date, the copyable mark text, tag text and listing text, the SKU book with no limit on how many pieces it holds, CSV and JSON export and JSON import, re-solving one piece, two stock records, and the self-test screen. Pro adds three things: both places solved side by side with every rung the two regimes disagree on named, the stock library without a limit, and re-solving the whole book in one tap when a supplier changes a spec. Pro never buys a different answer — the solver is the same solver on both tiers, and your export is never gated.
  • Subscription terms. STRIKEWORD Pro is $29.99 a year and opens with a 21-day free introductory offer (annual plan only, offered once per store account), or $4.99 a month with no trial, or $79.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next charge and the period you have paid for runs to its end. We cannot cancel it for you, and the app will not ask you to stay.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted, hidden or truncated. Every piece in the book, every stock record and both exports come back byte-identical to what they were while you were subscribed, and stay readable, editable, deletable and exportable, however many there are. What stops is solving both places at once, holding more than two stock records, and the one-tap re-solve of the whole book. A lapse lowers a ceiling on new work; it never takes a record away.
  • Prove it. Open the “Prove it” screen and STRIKEWORD re-runs a set of the answer-key cases on your own handset and shows its answers beside the expected ones, so you can check the instrument rather than trust it.
  • Does it need a signal at the bench? No. The whole rule set is about sixty kilobytes of transcribed tables and the solve is arithmetic, so the app opens, solves and exports with the network switched off.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.

STRIKEWORD’s rule tables are transcribed from primary sources and cited line by line in the app. The United States tables come from 16 CFR Part 23, §§ 23.0–23.28 and the Appendix, eCFR issue 2026-09-03, a work of the United States Government in the public domain under 17 U.S.C. § 105. The United Kingdom tables come from the Hallmarking Act 1973, Schedules 1 and 2 and section 22, from legislation.gov.uk, reproduced under the Open Government Licence v3.0; the licence attribution ships inside the app. The engine was written against an answer key and a null baseline both recorded before any solver code existed: it reproduces 74 of 74 published worked examples, agrees with an independent second transcription on 3,200 of 3,200 enumerated constructions, passes 10 of 10 boundary property tests, decides 1,524 of 1,524 exemption-weight cases at the boundary, and traces 2,313 of 2,313 emitted lines to a cited, dated source. The null baseline — repeating the supplier’s own description — is wrong on 2,545 of those 3,200 constructions.
