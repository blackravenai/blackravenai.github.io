Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What is free and what is Pro. The whole proved solve for the room you are standing in is free forever: every piece with both of its end conditions, the coped end, the scarf placed on a real stud, the stick each piece comes out of, THE UNROLLED ELEVATION, the mitre and bevel for any corner you measured, the take-off, the saw log, every profile and rule in THE SHOP, both exports and the print sheet, and the Prove-it screen. Pro adds the job book beyond one room, THE RACK carried forward so kept offcuts serve later rooms as free stock, and the book totals with what a shop change costs across every room. Pro never buys a better answer, and it never gates anything you typed or measured.
  • Subscription terms. LONGPOINT Pro is $39.99 a year and opens with a 21-day free trial (yearly plan only, offered once per store account), or $6.99 a month with no trial, or $119.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next charge and the period you have paid for runs to its end.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted, hidden or locked. Forty rooms stay readable, editable, deletable and exportable, and so does every drop on the rack; only a forty-first room is stopped. A lapse lowers a ceiling on new records; it never takes a record away.
  • The walls as built are not the walls on the drawing. LONGPOINT does arithmetic on the numbers you entered. It does not know your room. Corners are not square, a room does not have to close on paper, and every plan it prints is a starting point you check with a bevel gauge and a tape before the blade goes through the stick. Verify every cut against your own material.
  • Where the numbers come from. Every rule is yours: stock lengths, profiles, cope or mitre, cope hand, corner clearance, framing spacing, scarf angle, kerf, end trim, keep line, reveal, head style, horn and returns. The published tables ship as cited, dated defaults you select and may edit, each carrying its citation and the line “verify against your own material.”
  • What LONGPOINT does not do. It states no structural, fire, sound or code result of any kind — no span, no load, no fastener schedule — and it is not an estimating, invoicing or job-management app. It derives the pieces and their end conditions from the room you measured and allocates them to sticks. What your local code requires of you remains a matter for you and your inspector.
  • Prove it. Open the Prove-it screen and LONGPOINT recomputes published compound-mitre, flat-mitre and casing-reveal figures on your own handset and shows its answers beside the printed ones, so you can check the instrument rather than trust it.
  • Does it work with no signal? Yes. It solves, saves, exports and prints with the phone in aeroplane mode, because there is nothing to call.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.

Sources & attribution

LONGPOINT’s compound-mitre arithmetic is the standard closed form for a crown sprung at angle S meeting a corner of C degrees — mitre = atan(sin S / tan(C/2)), bevel = asin(cos S · cos(C/2)) — and is checked on the device against freely published crown-angle tables; 60 published figures are reproduced to the decimals their sources print. The stick allocation is a branch-and-bound search over scarf placement and stick assignment, carrying a footage lower bound that proves the plan minimal for the stated problem: on 600 generated rooms it is optimal on 524 of the 524 that fall inside that bound, with the remaining 76 counted and reported rather than dropped, and 13 invariants hold across 1,200 rooms and 28,269 pieces. Against a null solver that simply cuts the sticks as they come, the engine buys 36.7% less stock over the same 600 rooms and never places a joint inside the corner clearance or off the framing spacing you declared. Every threshold, every skip and every constant is recorded line by line in the app’s design record and answer key.
