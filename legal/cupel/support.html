Contact us

Email support@blackravenai.com and we'll help. We aim to reply within 2–3 business days. To speed things up, tell us your device and OS version and describe what happened.

Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What is free and what is Pro. The full solver is free forever — every legal cut priced, the winning cut and its delta shown, with one open pile, 12 parcels and 2 refiner profiles. Pro adds unlimited parcels, piles and refiners, shipment history, reconciliation, the per-refiner realized-yield ledger and the printable manifest.
  • Subscription terms. Pro is US$59.99 a year with a 28-day free trial (annual only, once per account), or US$7.99 a month with no trial, or US$149.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price until you cancel; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your App Store or Google Play account under Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the period ends — cancelling stops the next charge and puts the free limits back at the end of the period you have paid for.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted or locked. Every pile, shipment, settlement and ledger row stays on the device, stays readable, stays exportable as CSV, JSON or manifest text and stays deletable. Only the free limits return.
  • What CUPEL does and does not do. It organises your own arithmetic on figures you type — your weights, your spot price, your refiner’s published schedule. It gives no legal, tax, accounting or financial advice, asserts no named refiner’s rates and never says what any party is owed. Weigh, assay and settle against your own scales and your own contract.
  • Restore purchases. Open Settings and tap Restore purchases; entitlements are re-checked with the store.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.
