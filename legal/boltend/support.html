Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What is free and what is Pro. The instrument is free forever for two articles: the whole United States solve with every section cited, the ordering, rounding, tolerance and exemption arithmetic shown line by line, the care check against all five elements of 16 CFR 423, the copy block your printer can set from, the complete version stack for every article, and CSV and JSON export of everything you entered. Pro adds articles beyond the second, the EU and UK statement under Regulation (EU) No 1007/2011, the re-solve diff that shows what changes on the label before you reprint, and the print-ready true-size label file. Pro never buys a different answer: the solver is the same solver on both tiers, and your export is never gated.
  • Subscription terms. BOLTEND Pro is $29.99 a year and opens with a 21-day free introductory offer (annual plan only, offered once per store account), or $4.99 a month with no trial, or $79.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next charge and the period you have paid for runs to its end.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted or hidden. Every article you built on Pro still opens, still solves, still shows its whole version stack, still exports as CSV and as JSON and still deletes on the free tier, including books far longer than the free tier would let you build today. What stops is starting a new article beyond the second, the EU and UK statement, the re-solve diff and the print-ready file. A lapse lowers a ceiling on new work; it never takes a record away.
  • How the statement is computed. BOLTEND parses the composition string as it is printed on the bolt end, resolves each trade name to the generic fibre name the rule requires, weights the components by the fibre weight you set, sorts by predominance, rounds to whole percentages so the statement totals exactly 100, applies the small-percentage and ornamentation exemptions, splits sectional statements where the article needs them, and adds the disclosures the rule requires. Every line on the label is tappable and opens the rule, the section number and the arithmetic behind it.
  • Care instructions are checked in words, not symbols. 16 CFR 423 expressly permits a care label written in words, and words are what BOLTEND renders. It ships no care symbol artwork in any jurisdiction — ISO 3758 / GINETEX symbols are registered marks requiring a licence, and the ASTM artwork sits inside a copyrighted standard. The validator checks your wording for the five elements the rule names: washing, bleaching, drying, ironing and, where required, a drycleaning instruction in the form the rule expects.
  • It refuses rather than guesses. A bolt-end string BOLTEND cannot parse — “linen blend”, an unlabelled remnant — is marked as unresolved in red and no label is cut from it. That is by design: a statement invented from a vague string is worse than no statement. Type the composition the mill gave you and it will solve.
  • Prove it. The app carries the regulator’s own published worked examples. Open the “Prove it” screen and BOLTEND re-solves them on your handset and shows its answers beside the printed ones, so you can check the instrument rather than trust it.
  • What it will not do. BOLTEND renders a label in the format of the regulation you select. It does not determine your country of origin, it does not supply the reasonable basis behind your care instructions, and it is not legal advice or a legal opinion. You own the final label. Check it against your own materials and your own records before you order a label run.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.

Sources & attribution

BOLTEND is measured against a frozen answer key before it ships. It reproduces 93 of 93 official worked examples exactly; it satisfies six stated properties across 201,399 enumerated cases with no sampling — every integer composition of 100 into two, three and four parts over six fibres, plus the two- and three-part subsets across eight exemption configurations; it returns the published verdict on 32 of 32 care-wording accept and reject cases and names the fault in all 13 rejects; and its generic-name table is set-equal to the regulator’s with all 112 rows cited to their source. A deliberately weak baseline that simply copies the bolt-end string fails every threshold, and that string is itself non-compliant in 90.3% of the enumerated cases.
