Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What is free and what is Pro. The instrument is free forever: unlimited checks of any worksheet and key, the whole report with the working printed under every finding, the margin rule, the count of questions it could not check, THE CORRECTED KEY rewritten line by line from its own working, text export, JSON shelf export and import, and three products on the shelf with their full check history. Pro adds the shelf itself — every product you publish — plus re-check-on-revision with a printed delta, house conventions applied across your whole catalogue, and A/B form pairing. Pro never buys a different answer: the engine is the same engine on both tiers, and your own data is never gated.
  • Subscription terms. STET Pro is $34.99 a year and opens with a 21-day free introductory offer (annual plan only, offered once per store account), or $5.99 a month with no trial, or $99.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next charge and the period you have paid for runs to its end.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted or hidden. Every product you kept on Pro still opens, still shows its check history, still re-checks, still exports as text and as shelf JSON and still deletes on the free tier, including shelves far longer than the free tier would let you build today. House conventions you already set become read-only rather than reverting, because reverting them would silently change results you have already acted on. What stops is keeping a new product beyond the third, the revision delta, editing conventions and form pairing. A lapse lowers a ceiling on new work; it never takes a record away.
  • How the check works. STET reads the worksheet and the answer key as two separate documents and reconciles them against each other. It recomputes every question it can solve — nine question families, in exact rational arithmetic on whole numerators and denominators rather than floating point, so 3/4 + 1/6 is compared as a fraction and not as a decimal that nearly matches. Where the key disagrees it names a cause from eight hypotheses, including the one that matters most: a key that has slipped by a line, which shows up as a run of consecutive corrections rather than as one. It also runs structural checks — a key entry with no question, a question with no key entry, a duplicated entry, a multiple-choice letter that does not exist on the sheet, a points total that does not add up.
  • The unchecked count is the point. Every question STET cannot recompute is counted and shown as unchecked in the same breath as the findings, and it is drawn in the margin in the second pencil. The app never reports a clean result by quietly skipping what it could not do. Nothing here promises a key is correct; STET reports the disagreements it can measure and prints the arithmetic behind each one.
  • THE CORRECTED KEY. When a finding resolves to exactly one value, STET can rewrite that line of your key — in your own numbering, line for line, nothing added and nothing moved — and show you every change as from → to before you apply it. Anything it cannot resolve is named and left exactly as you wrote it, and applying the rewrite leaves an undo. This is free on every tier.
  • It does not read images. STET checks pasted text. A worksheet that is a scan or a flattened image carries no readable text, and the app says so plainly rather than pretending to have checked it. There is no OCR, on any tier.
  • Prove it. The app carries eleven synthetic sample cases whose planted faults are known exactly. Open the “Prove it” screen and STET re-checks them on your own handset and shows its answers against the known ones, so you can check the instrument rather than trust it.
  • What it will not do. STET checks a worksheet against its own answer key. It does not write questions, it does not grade students, it does not teach, and it makes no claim about standards, curriculum or learning outcomes. It will never tell you a key is correct — only what it found, what it recomputed, and what it could not check.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.

Sources & attribution

STET is measured against a frozen answer key of 1,040 cases carrying 15,623 questions: 840 with a single injected defect across 14 defect kinds, 200 generated clean sets, 40 authored correct sets, and 21 reconstructions of real published answer-key errata. It finds 840 of 840 injected defects (100.00%, and 100% on every one of the 14 kinds) and names the cause on 97.50% of them, measured separately from the catch. It raises 0 false findings on the 200 generated clean sets and 0 on the 40 authored ones. Its parser classifies 15,623 of 15,623 questions as checked or unchecked against a 98% coverage threshold, and it catches 21 of 21 of the real published corrections, reported and never gated. A deliberately weak numbering-only baseline recorded before the engine existed reaches 21.43% and 0.0% on every arithmetic defect kind, and fails the first ranked threshold. The 12-case A/B form-pairing corpus was authored after the engine and is reported outside the ranked thresholds, labelled as weaker evidence. The generator is frozen by SHA-256 and re-hashed on every run. The method, every threshold, every constant and every miss are cited line by line in the app’s design record and its answer key.
