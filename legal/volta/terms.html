Real electronics are real

Read this part twice

Volta helps you build physical circuits with real electrical current. Even at hobby voltages, mistakes can damage your Arduino, your components, other property — or, with batteries, hot parts, and soldering irons involved, hurt you.

  • You build real-world circuits at your own risk. Volta's checks reduce risk; they cannot eliminate it, and they only know about what you told the app.
  • Work only with low-voltage projects of the kind Volta shows. Never connect anything to mains/wall power.
  • Verify parts against their official datasheets before powering anything you care about.
  • Children should build with adult supervision.
  • Unplug power before rewiring. If something smells hot, disconnect first, debug second.
