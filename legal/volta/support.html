Frequently asked

"Upload to my board" doesn't appear / won't flash

Flashing a real Arduino straight from Volta uses WebSerial, which only exists in desktop Chrome or Edge. On iOS, Android, Safari and Firefox, Volta shows the honest fallback instead: copy the sketch and upload it with the free Arduino IDE.

If flashing fails on desktop: check the USB cable is fully seated (some cables are charge-only), close the Arduino IDE or any serial monitor holding the port, then unplug the board and plug it back in.

Where is my data? Is there an account?

No account. Everything — circuits, progress, settings — lives on your device. That also means uninstalling the app (or clearing browser data on the web) erases it, so export builds you care about first: any project → Share → Build file.

How do I share a circuit with a friend?

Open the build → Share. Send the file, copy the link, or let them scan the QR code — they import it from the Builds page and get their own editable copy, checked by the same safety analyzer.

Why does Volta refuse to flash my circuit?

If the checker found something that could damage parts once real current flows, Volta blocks flashing and tells you exactly what to fix — usually one resistor away. You can override it, but the warning exists because the failure it describes is real.

The simulation and my real build behave differently

Step back through the build instructions (the overview lists every step) and run the "Check it before you power it" list again — nine times out of ten a wire is one row off or an LED is backwards. If you're sure the app is wrong, email us the build file; those reports make Volta better for everyone.

Refunds and purchases

Volta currently has no purchases. If that changes, purchases made through the App Store or Google Play are refunded through Apple/Google's own processes, and we'll document it here.

Reporting a bug

The perfect bug report: the build file (Share → Download file), your device and browser/app version, and the steps to make it happen. If the app ever shows its "That address doesn't match anything" screen or a blank page, the URL you were on is the clue — send it along.
