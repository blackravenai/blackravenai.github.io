Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-05

Volta is an electronics design, simulation and learning app made by BlackRaven AI LLC. It runs on your device, and it was built to need as little of your information as possible: there are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, and no tracking.

The short version

  • No account, no login — we never ask for your name, email, or anything else.
  • Your circuits, progress and settings are saved on your device, not on our servers. We don't have servers that hold user data.
  • No ads. No advertising SDKs, no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs inside the app.
  • No purchases. Volta doesn't sell anything inside the app today.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store personal information: no name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or browsing history. There are no user accounts and no server-side profile of you. The only time we see your email address is if you choose to write to our support address.

Data stored on your device

Everything Volta saves lives locally: your circuit designs and wiring, learn-path progress, XP and trophies, your parts inventory, and app settings. On the web version this uses your browser's local storage; in the iOS and Android apps it uses the app's own storage. None of it is transmitted to us. You can erase all of it at any time with the reset option in Settings, by clearing the app's data, or by uninstalling the app.

Camera and photos

Volta only uses the camera or your photo library when you tap to attach a reference photo of an object you're building around. In a project's measured objects, a small, downscaled thumbnail is saved with that project on your device; in the Fabricate measuring tool the photo is shown on screen only and never saved. Photos are never uploaded or sent to us. You can remove a photo at any time.

Sharing circuits

When you share a build, the entire circuit is encoded into the file, link, or QR code itself — nothing is uploaded to or stored on a server of ours. Whoever you give it to gets their own local copy. Like any website, the server that delivers the web app may keep standard, short-lived technical logs of requests (such as IP address and browser type) for security and operations; these are not used to identify or profile you.

Purchases

Volta currently offers no purchases. If a paid tier is introduced in the future, payment will be handled by the platform (Apple App Store, Google Play, or a payment processor on the web) — we will never see or store your card details — and this policy will be updated first.

Analytics

The app contains no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. Any aggregated statistics we receive (such as crash counts or install numbers) come from the app stores' standard developer reporting, which is governed by Apple's and Google's own policies.

Children

Volta is made to be safe for curious people of every age, including children. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children. There are no ads, no social features, no chat with other users, and nothing a child creates ever leaves the device unless they deliberately share a circuit file. If you believe a child has somehow sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, nearly all control lives on your device: reset everything in Settings, clear the app's data, or uninstall. Depending on where you live you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA; since we process no personal data, there is typically nothing for us to access, correct, or delete — but if you believe otherwise, contact us and we will help.

International users

BlackRaven AI LLC operates the app and support from Wyoming, United States. Because Volta keeps your data on your device, nothing you create is transferred to us in any country.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy we will change the date at the top and post the new version at this URL. Meaningful changes (for example, if purchases arrive) will be called out in the app's release notes.
