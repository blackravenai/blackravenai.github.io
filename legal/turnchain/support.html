Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What is free and what is Pro. One draft, the first 40 rows of it, the full arithmetic, the closure rail, the per-row workings, apply-fix and “I meant that”, the check history, the symbol chart and three of your own abbreviations are free forever — and so is every export, in all four formats, on every tier. Pro adds unlimited drafts kept on the shelf with their histories, patterns of any length, every nested size and a stitch dictionary with no ceiling. Pro never buys a more accurate number: your dictionary is read by the count model whether or not Pro is active, because correctness is not a tier.
  • Subscription terms. Pro is $34.99 a year and opens with a 21-day free trial (yearly plan only, offered once per store account), or $5.99 a month with no trial, or $89.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next charge and the period you have paid for runs to its end.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted or hidden. Every draft, every check history and every abbreviation stays exactly where it is, stays readable and stays exportable in all four formats. A lapse puts the free limits back on what you add next; it never takes a draft away.
  • What TURNCHAIN does, and does not do. TURNCHAIN reconciles the stitch counts you wrote against the stitch counts your own instructions produce, and tells you which row does not close and by how much. It does not design patterns for you, it does not test-crochet anything, and it cannot judge fit, gauge, tension, yarn quantity or whether the finished piece is the shape you intended. Those remain yours.
  • What it can miss. A stitch-count model can only see errors that show up in a stitch count. An error in a chain length, in a chain-space count, or in a row that publishes no stitch count at all is invisible to it, and the app says so rather than implying a clean pass means a clean pattern. Coverage of the pattern language is reported on the surface, and any row the parser cannot model is listed as unmodelled rather than silently passed.
  • Knitting. TURNCHAIN is crochet only, deliberately. Knitting has a different grammar — the UK/US term swap and right-side/wrong-side chart reversal — and supporting it badly would be worse than not supporting it.
  • US and UK terms. Craft Yarn Council US abbreviations are the primary vocabulary and UK terms are recognised; you set which terms a draft is written in, per draft, and whether the turning chain counts as a stitch.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.

Sources & attribution

TURNCHAIN’s vocabulary and chart symbols follow the Craft Yarn Council’s published standard crochet abbreviations and symbol set, used as the craft’s own public standard. The parser and the two-channel count model are the app’s own work, measured against a set of real published errata and recorded case by case in the app’s design record; the arithmetic is exact integer arithmetic over the rows you wrote.
