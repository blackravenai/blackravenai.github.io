Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What is free and what is Pro. The whole solve is free forever at full precision — unlimited frames, bows and openings, every broadseam offset on screen, the fit verdict, why-this-number, THE LADDER, the nest and its linear yards, THE BOOK with every recorded fit, and JSON export of everything you entered. A loft can chalk a baseline off the screen and cut the job without paying. Pro adds the four cut files (DXF, offsets sheet, offsets CSV, cut list) and applies the per-cloth take-up your own recorded fits have taught.
  • Subscription terms. Pro is $59.99 a year and opens with a 21-day free introductory offer (annual plan only, once per store account), or $9.99 a month with no trial, or $149.00 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next charge and the period you have paid for runs to its end.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted or hidden. Every frame, bow, opening, note and recorded fit stays exactly where it is, stays readable, editable, deletable and exportable as JSON. A lapse stops the cut files and the applied take-up; it never takes a record away and it never lowers the precision of the reading on screen.
  • What BROADSEAM does, and does not do. BROADSEAM develops flat panels from the frame dimensions you type in and states the seam curvature those dimensions imply. It does not measure your boat, it does not know your cloth, and it does not cut anything. Check every figure against the frame itself before you put a knife in the cloth.
  • The pattern is not a promise. A cover over a curved frame cannot be developed onto flat cloth without residual strain — that is geometry, not a defect. BROADSEAM states its own residual on every reading and refuses a frame it cannot pattern within your tolerance rather than guessing at one.
  • Where the take-up comes from. You supply it, by recording what a seam actually measured after you cut and sewed it. BROADSEAM ships no cloth constants; the correction is refitted per cloth from your own fits and clamped to plus or minus three per cent.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.

Sources & attribution

BROADSEAM’s development mathematics — superellipse bow fitting by bisection, equal-arc bow sampling with a centripetal Catmull-Rom loft, triangulated surface flattening by Gauss-Seidel edge-length projection, and the Gauss-Bonnet bound on residual strain — are implemented from published differential-geometry and mesh-parameterisation literature and are cited line by line in the app’s design record. DXF output is written to the AutoCAD R12 specification and read back by the app’s own parser as a proof gate.
