Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What STUBLINE does, and what it does not decide. STUBLINE turns a run you have already walked into the marks on each stick, measured from one reference end, so the last box lands where it must and no coupling ends up inside a bend. It does not size your conduit, count your conductors, calculate fill, decide a support method or make any electrical determination whatsoever. There is no electrical output in the app. Every number it prints is geometry and arithmetic against the figures you entered and the bender numbers on your bench screen, and it says so on the face of the sheet. Measure the pipe before you bend it and check the marks against the job.
  • Where the bender numbers come from. The take-up, radius, minimum radius and outside-diameter defaults are transcribed from two published sources and cited on the bench screen row by row: Klein Tools MKT200815 Rev. 09/20 and IDEAL ND 1534-2. Those sources publish 1/2″, 3/4″ and 1″ only; the 1-1/4″, 1-1/2″ and 2″ take-up values are the widely published field values rather than transcriptions, and each such row says so in the app. Every default is editable — if your bender’s shoe measures differently, type what you measured and the whole book re-solves against it.
  • The degree total is a sum, not a ruling. STUBLINE adds the bend angles in your run and shows the total against a limit you set yourself, with the default stated and editable. It is arithmetic on your own entries so you can see a run approaching a pull that will fight you. It is not a code determination and it is not an inspection.
  • What is free and what is Pro. The instrument is free forever: solving a run with any number of features and any number of sticks, no cap; the full marking sheet on screen with every mark from one reference end, the shrink carried and the couplings placed; every bender and both published charts, all editable; feet and inches to a sixteenth and millimetres; the degree total; the “Prove it” screen; export and import of the whole run book as JSON; and three runs in the book. Pro adds the run book without a limit, taking the marking sheet off the phone (share, print or download), and the itemised take-off with its CSV for the quote. Pro never buys a different answer — the solver is the same solver on both tiers, and your export is never gated.
  • Subscription terms. STUBLINE Pro is .99 a year and opens with a 21-day free introductory offer (annual plan only, offered once per store account), or .99 a month with no trial, or .99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next charge and the period you have paid for runs to its end. We cannot cancel it for you, and the app will not ask you to stay.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted, hidden or truncated. Every run in the book stays readable, stays solvable, stays exportable and stays deletable, however many there are — a run you entered on Pro does not close when Pro ends. What stops is starting a new run beyond the third, taking the sheet off the phone, and the itemised take-off. A lapse lowers a ceiling on new work; it never takes a record away.
  • Prove it. Open the “Prove it” screen and STUBLINE rebuilds ten runs in three dimensions from the marking sheet alone, on your own handset, and shows the worst position error it found. You can check the instrument rather than trust it.
  • Does it need a signal in the ceiling? No. The charts are a few kilobytes of transcribed numbers and the solve is arithmetic, so the app opens, solves, re-solves and exports with the phone in aeroplane mode, in a basement, on a deck.
  • Whose charts are these? The numbers are published manufacturer figures, transcribed as numbers with their source and date printed beside them, and they are your editable defaults rather than a reproduction of anyone’s document. STUBLINE bundles no manufacturer artwork, manual or table image.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.

Sources & attribution

STUBLINE’s solver is measured against an answer key and a null baseline both recorded before any solver code existed. Four hundred generated runs are reconstructed in three dimensions from the marking sheet alone by an independent reconstructor that shares no code with the solver — the harness fails the whole run if a single import crosses between them. Maximum position error is within a quarter inch on 400 of 400 runs, mean 0.0544 inches and worst 0.1461 inches, against a null baseline that passes 0 of 400 at a mean worst-case error of 22.08 inches. Across 1,200 further runs the solver records 0 invariant violations against the null’s 1,039, and refuses 4 runs as physically unbuildable rather than placing a coupling inside a bend, each refusal independently confirmed. It reproduces 156 of 156 published chart values and decomposes 74 of 74 chart-covered offsets exactly. Take-up, radius, minimum radius and outside diameter defaults are transcribed as numbers, with their source and date shown per row, from Klein Tools MKT200815 Rev. 09/20 and IDEAL ND 1534-2; values those sources do not publish are marked as field values on the row that carries them.
