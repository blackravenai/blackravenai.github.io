Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What is free and what is Pro. The engine is free forever, at the same accuracy Pro gets: all 27 checks, both severity tiers, the complete arithmetic printed beside every finding, unparsed lines reported by line number, the “Prove it” self-test, one draft on the shelf with unlimited versions and its ladder, and export of both the Markdown report and the whole-shelf JSON. Pro adds unlimited drafts on the shelf, house rules carried forward to every later version of a draft, the version-to-version comparison, and manuscripts over 40,000 words audited whole. Pro never buys a more accurate answer — the 27 checks are the same 27 checks on both tiers, and export is never gated.
  • Subscription terms. KEYWARD Pro is $99.99 once for lifetime, or $34.99 a year opening with a 21-day free introductory offer (annual plan only, offered once per store account), or $5.99 a month with no trial. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next charge and the period you have paid for runs to its end.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted or hidden. Every draft you filed on Pro still opens, still shows its versions, its findings and their arithmetic, still exports as Markdown and JSON, and still deletes on the free tier — including shelves far longer than the free tier would let you build today. What stops is starting a new draft beyond the first, carrying a house rule forward to a new version, and opening the version comparison. A lapse lowers a ceiling on new work; it never takes a record away.
  • How the audit works. You paste the manuscript in as you already wrote it — Markdown, Homebrewery, GM Binder page markers or plain text — and KEYWARD parses the stat blocks and the map key out of it. It then reconciles twice. Against the reference: every hit-point total re-derived from its hit dice and Constitution, every attack bonus from ability and proficiency, every save DC, every skill and saving-throw bonus, every experience award against the challenge rating, and every named condition, skill and damage type checked against the vocabulary the rules actually define. Against itself: every keyed area resolved, every cross-reference followed, every random-table range checked for gaps and overlaps, and every stated total re-added. Each finding opens to the two figures, the operation between them and the line number they came from.
  • Prove it. The app carries synthetic manuscripts whose faults are declared by hand, not read back out of the engine. Open the “Prove it” screen and KEYWARD re-runs them on your own handset and shows its answers against the declared ones, so you can check the instrument rather than trust it.
  • What it will not do, and this matters. KEYWARD checks arithmetic and reference, not craft. It does not judge encounter balance, pacing, prose, layout or art, and it cannot find a mistake that is not derivable from the text — a monster whose damage is simply too high for the party is not something arithmetic can see. Finding nothing is not a guarantee that the adventure is correct, and the app says so in those words on its own reference screen.
  • Lines it cannot read are reported, not skipped. A stat-block field line KEYWARD cannot parse is raised by line number as unreadable rather than passed over in silence, so the coverage figure means what it says and a section it never read can never be mistaken for a section that came back clean.
  • Which rules does it check against? Version 1 checks against a bundled table of names and numbers derived from an openly licensed system reference document under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License; the required attribution is printed on the app’s reference screen. KEYWARD reproduces no adventure, supplement or rulebook text, is not affiliated with or endorsed by any publisher, and makes no claim about any trademark.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.
