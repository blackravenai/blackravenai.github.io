SOAKAWAY

Privacy policy · version 1.0.0

THE SHORT VERSION

SOAKAWAY collects nothing about you, sends nothing anywhere, and asks for no permissions. It has no network code in it at all.

WHAT IS STORED

Your round book — the state of Brambling House, your progress, your settings and your records — is written to your device's local storage by the app itself. It never leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes it. "Start again" in Settings deletes it immediately.

WHAT IS NOT COLLECTED

No account, no email address, no name, no device identifier, no advertising identifier, no location, no contacts, no photos, no camera, no microphone, no files, no analytics, no crash reporting, no cookies, no fingerprinting.

THIRD PARTIES

There are none. SOAKAWAY contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, no social SDK and no third-party service of any kind.

PURCHASES

THE WHOLE HOUSE is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details and receive no personal information from the transaction.

CHILDREN

SOAKAWAY is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

SHARING A ROUND SHEET

If you tap "share the sheet", the app draws a picture on your device and hands it to your operating system's share menu. Where it goes after that is entirely your choice, and the app is not told.

CHANGES

If this policy ever changes, the new version will ship with an app update and be dated at the top of this page.
