HOW TO PLAY

How do I water something?
Press and hold anywhere on the pot screen. The can tips and the water runs. Let go when the soil looks right.
How am I supposed to know when it looks right?
Watch the leaves. They lift when the soil reaches what that plant wants — but there is a delay, and it is different for every kind, so the better you know a pot the earlier you can let go. The soil colour and the sound of the pour both tell you something too.
What do the grades mean?
TRUE is the narrow band the plant actually wants. GENEROUS and SHY are near misses and still fine. DRY is not enough. OVER means water is standing in the saucer.
I keep running out of calls.
You will. The tap on the landing costs a call, so every refill is a flat you do not get into. Water the pots that gain the most from a litre, and let the succulents wait — they can.
A plant died.
The aloe in flat 11 was already dead when you got the keys; the tenant's card says to leave it. Anything else takes several rounds of real neglect. Nothing can die in a single round.
Can I turn the pots?
Yes, and you should — everything in that building leans. TURN evens it up and the plant does slightly better for it. PINCH takes off the brown leaves.
