Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What is free and what is Pro. The instrument is free forever: the full engine at full accuracy — all nine checks, every finding opened to its arithmetic and the line it was read from, the coverage figure, the column, the “Prove it” self-test, the built-in sample, unlimited checks, and every export (the report, the findings CSV and the complete JSON backup). Pro adds every draft filed on the shelf rather than one, the manifest of record and the diff against it, and the house glossary you curate. Pro never buys a more accurate number: the nine checks are the same nine checks on both tiers, and export is never gated.
  • Subscription terms. BOXBACK Pro is $34.99 a year and opens with a 21-day free introductory offer (annual plan only, offered once per store account), or $6.99 a month with no trial, or $99.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next charge and the period you have paid for runs to its end.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted or hidden. Every draft you filed on Pro still opens, still shows its findings and their working, still exports as report, CSV and JSON, and still deletes on the free tier, including shelves far longer than the free tier would let you build today. What stops is filing a new draft beyond the first, the diff against the manifest of record and the house glossary. A lapse lowers a ceiling on new work; it never takes a record away.
  • How the check works. BOXBACK reads three documents as three separate sources — the rulebook draft, the component manifest that will be printed on the box back, and the card list you keep in a spreadsheet — and binds each setup demand and each named component back to the line that declares it. Nine check families run over that binding: a setup step demanding more of a component than the manifest holds, a component in the manifest that no rule ever uses, a card the rules name that the card list does not contain, quantities that disagree between the manifest and the card list, identifier ranges that do not match the deck, identifiers written to inconsistent widths, a cross-reference to a section number that does not exist, a keyword used but never defined, and a defined term used nowhere. Every finding opens to the two figures, the operation between them and the line numbers they came from.
  • Prove it. The app carries ten complete synthetic drafts whose faults are declared by hand, not read back out of the engine. Open the “Prove it” screen and BOXBACK re-runs all ten on your own handset and shows its answers against the declared ones, so you can check the instrument rather than trust it.
  • What it will not do. BOXBACK checks a rulebook against its own manifest and its own card list. It does not play the game, it does not read diagrams or card art, and it does not judge whether a rule is good. Of a set of 41 real published rulebook corrections gathered for the design record, 26 — 63% — are detectable from text at all; the other 15 need play, a diagram or a translation reading. The app says so in “How it works” and never claims a rulebook is error-free. Finding nothing is not a guarantee.
  • The documents come from where you already keep them. The rulebook is pasted from your document editor and the card list from your spreadsheet as CSV or TSV. Lines BOXBACK cannot read are reported by line number rather than skipped silently, so the coverage figure means what it says.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.
