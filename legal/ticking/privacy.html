Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-09-07

TICKING is a law-tag composer for the person who stuffs the article — the pillow, cushion, pet bed, mattress or upholstered piece — and has to get the tag right before the label run is ordered. It is made by BlackRaven AI LLC and runs entirely on your device. There is no account and no sign-in, and we designed it to need as little of your information as possible.

The short version

  • No account, no login, no name or email required.
  • Your data is saved on your device, not on our servers.
  • We don't run our own analytics or tracking.
  • Everything stays on your device. Ticking makes no network calls, has no account, and sends nothing anywhere. It has no server of its own. The jurisdiction table it reasons over — 51 US jurisdictions, 25 of them transcribed from that jurisdiction’s own published text with the citation and the date it was read, the other 26 recorded only as a dated requirement-level fact from an industry reference — is bundled inside the app as plain text tables. Everything else is arithmetic on the weights you typed: the filling-name resolution, the percentages by weight, each state’s own ordering and tolerance, the strictest-state intersection across your destinations and the tag geometry, all computed locally, with the handset in aeroplane mode if you like. There is no artificial-intelligence service behind it, no model and no inference; nothing you type is uploaded. The only exception is buying or restoring a subscription: that is handled by Apple’s or Google’s own purchase system, and the receipt it returns is checked by our subscription provider (RevenueCat) so the app knows whether Pro is active. That check carries an anonymous store purchase identifier and the receipt — never the articles you tag, the weights you enter, your maker name, your address or your registry number.
  • TICKING shows no ads. Store versions may offer purchases (via RevenueCat and the app store); those partners have their own privacy policies, linked below.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or photos. We have no user accounts and no server-side profile of you. The only time we see your email is if you choose to email our support address.

Data stored on your device

Your tag book (each article’s name, the article type, the new/secondhand/owner’s-material status, every filling you typed with the weight and the generic term it resolved to, the destination states, your maker or vendor identity block and registry number, the solved tag with every citation behind it, the date it was solved, the table version it was solved under and every dated re-solve in its history), your identity presets, your own dated edits to shipped jurisdiction rows and your app settings are stored locally on your device, in the app’s local storage under the key ticking.save.v2. Older ticking.save.v1 records are read once and carried forward, and fields we do not recognise are carried through untouched rather than dropped. You can export any of it — one tag as printer-ready plain text, or the whole book as JSON — at any time, on any tier, including after a Pro plan ends; your own data is never gated. None of it is uploaded or transmitted by the app, and deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising

TICKING does not show ads and does not include any advertising SDK.

Purchases

If TICKING offers in-app purchases, the transaction is handled by the app store (Apple App Store or Google Play) and processed for us by RevenueCat, which manages entitlements and receipts. We never see or store your payment card details. RevenueCat may process a purchase/receipt identifier to unlock what you bought and restore it across your devices. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy, the Apple Privacy Policy, and the Google Privacy Policy.

Analytics

TICKING does not include our own third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. Any limited, aggregated diagnostics you receive come from the app stores' standard reporting (for example crash counts), which is governed by Apple's and Google's policies, not ours.

Children

TICKING is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will address it.

Your choices and rights

Because we don't hold a personal profile of you, most control lives on your device: you can clear the app's local data. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA over data our purchase partners process; you can exercise those directly with those partners (linked above) or contact us and we'll help point you to the right place.

International users

BlackRaven AI LLC operates the app and support from Wyoming, United States. Our purchase partners may process data in other countries under their own safeguards.
