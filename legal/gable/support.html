Support

Last updated 2026-09-28

Need help with GABLE? You're in the right place. BlackRaven AI LLC makes GABLE, and we read every message.

Contact us

Email support@blackravenai.com and we'll help. We aim to reply within 2–3 business days. To speed things up, tell us your device and OS version and describe what happened.

Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What ads GABLE shows, and when. Family-safe, non-personalised ads from Google AdMob, requested with child-directed treatment and a G content rating for every player. Never while you are crossing: an optional short video can stand you back up once per run or double a run’s wicks, and an occasional short ad may appear after every fifth run at most — never on Tonight’s Roofs, never in your first two sessions, and never on a run where you earned a ladder or set a new best. Settings › Ad privacy reviews consent where the law provides it.
  • Who is playing, and the grown-up check. The first time GABLE opens it asks who is playing. Buying, restoring, sharing a ladder and ad-privacy choices sit behind a short grown-up check for younger players. Change player on the home screen asks again.
  • No Ads, Ever and the Lamplighters’ Guild. No Ads, Ever is a one-time $3.99 purchase that removes every ad; the rewards you could watch for become free. The Lamplighters’ Guild is $2.99 a month (US prices; the store shows your local price): no ads, free rewards, double wicks on every run, and the Guild greatcoat while you are a member. It renews automatically at the same price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends. Cancel any time — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions. If it ends, everything you bought with wicks stays yours.
  • Ladders are never for sale. There are no loot boxes and no currency packs. Ladders are only earned on the roofs and only given away; wicks are earned by crossing and buy looks only — nothing sold changes a gap, the mark or a ladder.
  • “I was right on the edge and still fell.” A ladder tip that lands just past either edge of a roof still catches it (“by a toe”), and after a fall GABLE tells you whether you were short or long and by how much.
  • A ladder code will not claim. Codes are 26 characters; O reads as 0 and I or L as 1. Tap Paste on the claim screen, or paste the whole message — the code is found inside it. A code can be claimed once per device, and never by the person who laid it — that is by design.
  • I gave a ladder to a friend. Where does it go? It waits on the roof you chose inside their run, with your name tag on it, until they reach that roof — across as many runs as it takes.
  • Restore purchases. Open the shop or Settings and tap Restore purchases; entitlements are re-checked with the store.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.
