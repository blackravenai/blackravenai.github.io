Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-09-28

GABLE is a one-thumb rooftop-crossing game made by BlackRaven AI LLC: hold to grow a ladder, let go to lay it across. The ladders you earn can only be given — to a stranded traveller in the town, or to a friend by code. It runs on your device. There is no account and no sign-in, and we designed it to need as little of your information as possible.

The short version

  • No account, no login, no name or email required.
  • Your data is saved on your device, not on our servers.
  • We don't run our own analytics or tracking.
  • GABLE has no server of its own; the town, the music and every sound are generated on the device and play offline. Ladders you lay for a friend travel as a 26-character code inside a message you choose to send; nothing is uploaded by the game. The only network activity comes from two partners: Google AdMob, which serves ads, and — only if you buy something — the app store’s purchase system with our subscription provider RevenueCat, which checks the receipt so the game knows what you own. The first time GABLE opens it asks who is playing; that answer stays on the device. Every ad request is sent as child-directed / under-age treatment, rated G, and non-personalised, whoever is playing — no ad in GABLE is ever personalised or used to track you across apps.
  • GABLE may show ads; advertising partners' policies are linked below.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or photos. We have no user accounts and no server-side profile of you. The only time we see your email is if you choose to email our support address.

Data stored on your device

Your runs, best scores and night streak, the wicks you have earned and the lamps, ladders and coats you own and wear, your errands and their progress, the ladders you have earned, claimed and given, the travellers you carried and their letters, which how-to tips you have already seen, your purchase state as last confirmed by the store, your answer to “Who is playing?”, and your sound, music and haptics settings are stored locally on your device, in the app’s local storage. The name tag you hang on a ladder is a nickname you choose; it travels only inside a code you decide to share. None of this is uploaded by the game, and deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising

GABLE may display advertising provided by third-party ad networks; see their linked policies for how they handle data.

Purchases

If GABLE offers in-app purchases, the transaction is handled by the app store (Apple App Store or Google Play) and processed for us by RevenueCat, which manages entitlements and receipts. We never see or store your payment card details. RevenueCat may process a purchase/receipt identifier to unlock what you bought and restore it across your devices. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy, the Apple Privacy Policy, and the Google Privacy Policy.

Analytics

GABLE does not include our own third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. Any limited, aggregated diagnostics you receive come from the app stores' standard reporting (for example crash counts), which is governed by Apple's and Google's policies, not ours.

Children

GABLE is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will address it.

Your choices and rights

Because we don't hold a personal profile of you, most control lives on your device: you can clear the app's local data. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA over data our purchase partners process; you can exercise those directly with those partners (linked above) or contact us and we'll help point you to the right place.

International users

BlackRaven AI LLC operates the app and support from Wyoming, United States. Our purchase partners may process data in other countries under their own safeguards.

Changes to this policy
