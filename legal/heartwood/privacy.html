Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-07-08

Heartwood is a private family-tree studio made by BlackRaven AI LLC. It runs on your device — every record you keep and every relationship it computes stays local. Family history is personal, so we designed Heartwood to need none of your information: there are no accounts, no sign-in, and no cloud.

The short version

  • No account, no login, no name or email required.
  • Your data is saved on your device, not on our servers.
  • We don't run our own analytics or tracking.
  • Heartwood shows no ads. Store versions may offer purchases (via RevenueCat and the app store); those partners have their own privacy policies, linked below.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or photos. We have no user accounts and no server-side profile of you. The only time we see your email is if you choose to email our support address.

Data stored on your device

Your family trees — people, relationships, life events, dates, places, notes, and source citations — plus your settings are stored locally on your device (in the app's local storage). None of it is transmitted to us or anyone else. Records about your relatives, including living people, exist only on your device; the app's "Protect the living" setting additionally hides living people's dates on anything you choose to share. You can remove data at any time by deleting records in the app, using Erase all data in Settings, or uninstalling the app. You can export your own archive whenever you like — GEDCOM and full JSON backups are always available, free — and sharing a Kinship Card, poster, or export happens only when you explicitly choose to share it.

Advertising

Heartwood does not show ads and does not include any advertising SDK.

Purchases

If Heartwood offers in-app purchases, the transaction is handled by the app store (Apple App Store or Google Play) and processed for us by RevenueCat, which manages entitlements and receipts. We never see or store your payment card details. RevenueCat may process a purchase/receipt identifier to unlock what you bought and restore it across your devices. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy, the Apple Privacy Policy, and the Google Privacy Policy.

Analytics

Heartwood does not include our own third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. Any limited, aggregated diagnostics you receive come from the app stores' standard reporting (for example crash counts), which is governed by Apple's and Google's policies, not ours.

Children

Heartwood is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will address it.

Your choices and rights

Because we don't hold a personal profile of you, most control lives on your device: you can clear the app's local data. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA over data our purchase partners process; you can exercise those directly with those partners (linked above) or contact us and we'll help point you to the right place.

International users

BlackRaven AI LLC operates the app and support from Wyoming, United States. Our purchase partners may process data in other countries under their own safeguards.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy we'll change the date below and post the new version at this URL. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
