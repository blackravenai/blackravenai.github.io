Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What is free and what is Pro. The instrument is free forever: the full solver at full accuracy on every log, all three log rules (Doyle, Scribner and International ¼), the reason panel behind every number, the “Prove it” screen, one board menu, the cut sheet on screen to check at the deck, every log you enter listed, tallied, corrected and deleted, and CSV and JSON export of everything. Pro adds a board menu for every customer, recovery by species over your whole book instead of the last 10 logs, totals and value by customer over the whole book, and the cut sheet shared or printed off the phone. Pro never buys a more accurate number.
  • Subscription terms. Pro is $39.99 a year and opens with a 21-day free trial (annual plan only, offered once per store account), or $6.99 a month with no trial, or $149.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iPhone, Settings › your name › Subscriptions › HEADRIG; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions › HEADRIG. Cancelling stops the next charge and the period you have paid for runs to its end.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted, locked or hidden. Every log stays on the device, stays listed, stays editable and stays exportable in full, including all of it in the CSV. One thing changes: the accruing tables go back to reading the last 10 logs instead of the whole book. A lapse narrows the analysis window; it never takes a record away.
  • HEADRIG does not grade lumber and does not size structural members. It computes where to open a log for yield under the inputs you give it. It states no grade, no span, no load and no safety instruction, and it never says “maximum” without saying under which inputs.
  • Sweep is our approximation, not the published one. The original research programme’s sweep model was never published in a form anyone can reproduce, so HEADRIG reduces the working radius by half the mid-span deviation and says so on the dial, in the reason panel, on the ledger card and on the cut sheet. It is not claimed as verified.
  • The wane allowance is our model too. It is off by default, and every figure in the app’s published answer key is measured with it off. Turn it on and the app prints the allowance in force on the deck, in the reason panel and on the cut sheet.
  • Check the log before you cut it. HEADRIG plans a breakdown from four measurements you type in; it does not measure your log and it does not run your mill. Verify every dimension against the log on the bunks before the blade goes in.
  • Restore purchases. Open MILL › settings and tap Restore purchases; entitlements are re-checked with the store.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.

Sources & attribution

HEADRIG’s opening-face mathematics are implemented from works of the United States Government, which carry no copyright protection under 17 U.S.C. § 105: Hallock and Lewis (1971), “Increasing Softwood Dimension Yield from Small Logs — Best Opening Face”, USDA Forest Service Forest Products Laboratory Research Paper FPL-166, with General Technical Reports FPL-48 and FPL-49 (Lewis, 1985). The app reproduces 133 of the 139 cells transcribed from those papers and names every miss in its published results rather than tuning them away. Two rules the papers never wrote down — the pre-1970 American Lumber Standard dry widths and the centred-cant thickness rule — were reverse-derived and are flagged in the app as derived, not quoted. The Doyle, Scribner and International ¼ log rules are the standard published formulae.
