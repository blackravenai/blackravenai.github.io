Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What is free and what is Pro. A whole working instrument is free forever: one vehicle with unlimited readings on it, every index with its measured band, every refusal guard, the full prescription with its cause and stop rule, the clear-coat budget, the before-and-after PROVEN test, bench mode, both device calibrations, and both exports — the job sheet and the complete JSON backup. Pro adds every vehicle on the board at once, the baseline library pooled across all your jobs, and the customer correction certificate. Pro never buys a better reading.
  • Subscription terms. Pro is $29.99 a year and opens with a 21-day free trial (annual plan only, offered once per account), or $4.99 a month with no trial, or $89.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next charge and the period you have paid for runs to its end.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted or hidden. Every vehicle you ever entered, every reading and every micron booked against a panel stays on the device, stays readable, stays editable and stays exportable. What stops is the pooled baseline analysis and adding another car to the board. A lapse lowers a ceiling; it never takes a record away.
  • Why did it refuse my reading? On purpose, and it tells you which of the nine conditions it found — direct sun or bay light on the panel, the phone tilted off parallel, the standoff too far or too close, the screen clipping, the hand moving during the burst, or a surface that is not clear coat. A refusal is the instrument declining to put a number it cannot defend on a customer’s job sheet. Re-read it in shade, flat, at the standoff the screen asks for.
  • It will not name a rotary hologram. Deliberately, and the app says so in the prescription. Holograms and wash marring are both aligned scratch families, and separating them needs about 65 independent strikes inside the reticle where the patch of panel in frame holds about 14. HALOCUT reports ALIGNED MARRING with a bearing and states that a mitt and a rotary both produce it. It will not guess.
  • What the microns mean. HALOCUT prescribes against a clear-coat budget and caps a single visit at part of it, so it will often tell you to do less than the panel could take and to finish on the next visit. It estimates removal from the stages it prescribes; it does not measure film thickness. If you own a gauge, enter the reading and the budget uses it.
  • Calibrate before the first job. Two short calibrations in Setup fix the screen’s pixels-per-millimetre and the camera’s angular scale for your handset. Without them the indices are comparable to each other but not to another operator’s phone.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.

The method, every constant and the measured accuracy of each figure are published in the app’s caveat harness and cited line by line in its design record.
