Support

Last updated 2026-08-28

Need help with PLINTH? You're in the right place. BlackRaven AI LLC makes PLINTH, and we read every message.

Contact us

Email support@blackravenai.com and we'll help. We aim to reply within 2–3 business days. To speed things up, tell us your device and OS version and describe what happened.

Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What is free and what is Pro. The full solver, the carry score, the face and the joint, and the text and CSV cut sheet are free forever, with 3 slabs on the rack and 1 open job. Pro adds an unlimited rack with offcuts written back and matched against new jobs, unlimited jobs, DXF export, the sign-off sheet and the full ledger with its yield trend.
  • Subscription terms. Pro is $129.99 a year with a 21-day free trial (annual only, once per account), or $22.99 a month with no trial, or $399.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically until you cancel; the lifetime purchase never renews. Cancel any time in your App Store or Google Play account under Subscriptions — cancelling stops the next charge and puts the free limits back.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted or locked. Every slab, job, photograph and ledger row stays readable, exportable and deletable; the free limits simply come back.
  • Colour. PLINTH reads vein geometry and relative tone from one photograph. It is not a colour-calibrated slab-inventory system and must never be used as one — check colour against the physical slab before you cut.
  • Check the cut before you make it. PLINTH plans a layout; it does not measure your stone or run your saw. Verify every dimension on the slab itself.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.

Sources & attribution

PLINTH’s rectification, vein-field and seam-carry mathematics are implemented from published computer-vision literature (planar homography by direct linear transform; structure-tensor orientation and coherence) and are cited line by line in the app’s design record. DXF output is written to the AutoCAD R12 specification.
