Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What is free and what is Pro. The first 24 puzzles of an interior are audited free, for ever, with every finding and its full working, UNCHECKED puzzles reported by number, one title on the shelf with its latest run, per-title conventions, the Prove-it screen, complete offline operation, and export always — the errata sheet and the shelf JSON are never gated. Pro adds every puzzle in the interior, unlimited titles on the shelf, the full run history and the run-to-run delta that shows a revision fixed what the last run found. Pro never buys a more accurate answer: the solvers are the same solvers on both tiers.
  • Subscription terms. GRIDPROOF Pro is $39.99 a year and opens with a 21-day free introductory offer (annual plan only, offered once per store account), or $6.99 a month with no trial, or $119.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next charge and the period you have paid for runs to its end.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted or hidden. Every title, every run and every finding stays on the device, stays readable and stays exportable as an errata sheet and as JSON, including shelves far longer than the free tier would let you build today. What stops is auditing past the 24th puzzle of a new run, starting a second title, and reading the history and the delta. A lapse lowers a ceiling on new work; it never takes a record away.
  • UNCHECKED is not a pass. Where a puzzle cannot be read, or where an exhaustive search would exceed the app’s node budget, GRIDPROOF reports that puzzle as UNCHECKED by number rather than guessing at it. Input is pasted text, never images: a puzzle that exists only as a picture in your layout cannot be solved here and is counted as UNCHECKED so the total always reconciles.
  • GRIDPROOF does not approve your book. It solves the puzzles it can and reports what it found, with the cells, entries or clue numbers that produced each flag. It makes no determination that a book is correct, publishable or fit for sale, and it is not a substitute for your own reading of the proof. The upload decision is yours.
  • A flag I disagree with. Every finding names the rule that fired and the exact location, and an accidental-word flag can be dismissed for that title only — your accidental-word threshold and your symmetry convention are per-title settings you control.
  • Prove it. Open the Prove-it screen and GRIDPROOF re-runs shipped cases whose answers are known on your own handset, showing its answers beside the expected ones, so you can check the instrument rather than trust it.
  • Restore purchases. Restore is on the Pro screen and in Settings; entitlements are re-checked with the store.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.

Sources & attribution

GRIDPROOF is measured against a frozen answer key before it ships. The key is generated by a program that shares no code with the app’s engine: 320 synthetic books, 2,796 puzzles, 120 of them clean and 200 carrying 600 planted defects across 15 kinds. On the last measured run the app found 600 of 600 defects (100.0%, including 40 of 40 sudoku grids with more than one solution), raised 0 false flags on the 120 clean books, classified 2,796 of 2,796 puzzles through the same paste-and-parse path the app itself uses, and overran its search budget 0 times. A deliberately weak baseline — checking the numbering and stopping — finds 40 of 600 on the same run, an ablation delta of 93.3 recall points. Sudoku solution counting is an exhaustive bitmask search short-circuiting at the second solution under a 2,000,000-node budget; logic grids are enumerated exhaustively to at most four categories of five items. The one licensed asset is the word list: /usr/share/dict/web2 (Webster’s Second International), public domain — 175,479 words after filtering. No third-party puzzle, clue, grid or answer is stored, bundled or reproduced anywhere in the app. Every threshold, every deviation and every constant is cited line by line in the app’s design record and its answer key.
