Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What STRETCHOUT does, and does not do. It develops the flat pattern for the fitting you specify and applies the allowances you set. It does not size duct, calculate airflow, velocity or pressure, choose a gauge, specify reinforcement, or tell you what any code or specification requires. You enter the gauge and the seam; the app tells you what the metal does. Every printed sheet carries the line “The fabricator is responsible for the part.”
  • What is free and what is Pro. The whole instrument is free forever: all nine fittings across three development methods, every seam, edge and bend allowance, the pattern drawn on screen, the half pattern for symmetric fittings, the method panel and ordinate table, the “Prove it” run, the tiled 1:1 PDF print, three fittings kept in the book, and JSON and CSV export of everything you typed. Pro adds the fitting book without a limit, R12 DXF export, and the shop’s own saved seam, edge and gauge presets. Pro never buys a better pattern — the solver is identical on both tiers.
  • Subscription terms. STRETCHOUT Pro is $99.00 a year and opens with a 21-day free trial (yearly plan only, offered once per store account), or $16.99 a month with no trial, or $299.00 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next charge and the period you have paid for runs to its end.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted or hidden. Every fitting in the book stays readable, re-opens as a live pattern you can edit and re-cut, still prints at 1:1 and still exports as JSON and CSV — including books far longer than the free tier would let you build today. What stops is keeping a fourth new fitting, DXF export and saving a new shop preset. A lapse lowers a ceiling on new work; it never takes a record away.
  • An allowance was cut back and the app said so. On a pattern that wraps most of the way round, the two seam edges can meet, and the seam allowance you asked for has nowhere to go. Rather than draw a shape that will not cut, STRETCHOUT reduces the allowance to the width that still cuts, marks it on the main screen and names the cause. Change the seam type or the seam position and it will draw the width you asked for.
  • Printing at 1:1. The pattern prints tiled across as many sheets as it takes, with registration marks and a printed check rule. Print at 100% scale with page fitting off and measure the check rule with a tape before you scribe from the sheets.
  • Where the seam runs changes the pattern. A round profile can take the seam at the side, the front or the back; an elbow gore at the side, the throat or the heel; a rectangular profile at a mid-side or a corner. Set it to the seam you are actually running — the development is not the same for all of them.
  • Seam and edge figures are your shop’s. They are shown, editable and saved as defaults, never presented as a standard. A seam figure is applied as sheet the machine eats; an edge figure as a finished width with the setback and bend allowance worked in.
  • The half pattern. A fitting symmetric about its centre element can be drawn as half the stretch-out and flipped on a marked centre line, which roughly halves the paper. The mirror is taken on the net line; the method panel names the other seam figure the flipped half takes. Fittings that are not symmetric — offset transitions, eccentric tapers, and any fitting whose seam is not on its centre line — are handed back whole with the reason stated.
  • Prove it. The app carries a self-test you can run on your own handset: it re-solves shipped cases whose answers are known and shows its result against them, so you can check the instrument rather than trust it.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.

Sources & attribution

STRETCHOUT develops every pattern by true lengths — parallel line, radial line or triangulation, chosen per fitting and named on screen — with setback and bend allowance from the neutral-axis method published in FAA-H-8083-31B and AC 65-15A (United States Government work, public domain), and its geometry checked against pattern-drafting plates published by Neubecker (1920, 1922) and the International Correspondence Schools (1901), held by the Internet Archive as not in copyright. The shipped harness re-solves 320 seeded fittings, 408 pieces in all: every published answer is reproduced within its source’s own printed precision, every developed piece is folded back up in three dimensions and measured against the solid it came from, every outline is checked to be cuttable, and every DXF and printed sheet is re-parsed and re-measured at print scale. Round profiles are modelled as 96-sided polygons of exact girth. Every threshold, every deviation and every constant is cited line by line in the app’s design record and its answer key.
