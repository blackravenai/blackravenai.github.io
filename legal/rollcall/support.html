Support

Need help with Rollcall? You're in the right place. BlackRaven AI LLC makes Rollcall, and we read every message.

Contact us

Email support@blackravenai.com and we'll help. We aim to reply within 2–3 business days. To speed things up, tell us your device and OS version and describe what happened.

Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What is free and what is Pro. Free: the average meter and dial, the Sunny 16 card and one roll. Rollcall Pro adds spot metering and lock, ND, filter, bellows, push/pull and reciprocity corrections, calibration, unlimited rolls and gear, and exports.
  • Subscription terms. Rollcall Pro is $7.99 per year or $14.99 once for lifetime access, in US dollars; local prices may differ. The yearly plan opens with a one-week free trial, offered once per Apple account; if you don't cancel before it ends, it becomes a paid yearly subscription. Subscriptions renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel any time in iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions (or from the app's Settings). Cancelling stops the next charge; the period you paid for runs to its end. The lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews and has nothing to cancel.
  • How accurate is the meter? Phone cameras differ. Check a reading against Sunny 16 in full sun (about EV 15 at ISO 100) and set the calibration offset in Settings. Reciprocity figures come from the film makers' published data and are starting points; bracket important long exposures.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.
