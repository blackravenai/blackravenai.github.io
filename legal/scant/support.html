Email support@blackravenai.com and we'll help. We aim to reply within 2–3 business days. To speed things up, tell us your device and OS version and describe what happened.

Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What is free and what is Pro. The instrument is free forever: one draft on the shelf checked as often as you like, all twelve check families at full arithmetic with the working shown on every line, THE MAT drawn to scale for every fabric and every size, your house rules and your own rulings, the on-device self-test, the last five dated checks, and every export — .scant, findings CSV, history CSV and the whole record as JSON — plus importing a cutting list written as prose. Pro adds unlimited drafts on the shelf, the complete dated check history, named house-rule profiles and the release report. Pro never buys a more accurate answer, and export is never gated on any tier.
  • Subscription terms. Scant Pro is $44.99 a year and opens with a 30-day free trial (annual plan only, offered once per account), or $6.99 a month with no trial, or $134.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next charge and the period you have paid for runs to its end.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted or hidden. Every draft already on the shelf still opens, still checks, still draws its mat, still exports and still deletes on the free tier, including shelves far longer than the free tier would let you build today. Your version history is capped at twenty for everyone and is never trimmed by entitlement, and the older check entries the free view windows out are still recorded and still in the free history CSV. A lapse lowers a ceiling on new work; it never takes a record away.
  • How the checking works. SCANT parses the draft you wrote and recomputes it — strips against pieces per strip, sub-cuts against the strip they come from, yardage against the plan, unit and block counts against the layout, borders, binding and backing against the finished size — then reports only the lines whose own arithmetic does not close, with the rule, the value you wrote and the value it comes to set beside each one.
  • Prove it. The app carries a shipped self-test whose answers are known. Open it on your own handset and SCANT re-runs the checks in front of you, so you can check the instrument rather than trust it.
  • What it will not do. SCANT checks a pattern against itself. It does not know whether your design is good, whether your fabric is in print, or whether a number you wrote was the number you meant — only whether the numbers in the draft agree with each other. It also never invents a figure: a prose line whose arithmetic you did not state comes back as a comment naming what to add, not as a guess. Read the draft yourself before you publish.
  • House rules. The usable width of fabric, the seam allowance and the waste percentage are yours, not ours. Set them once and every check runs under them, and every dated check records which rules it ran under.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.

Sources & attribution

SCANT’s twelve check families are measured against an authored answer key built from real published pattern errata: on 39 corrections issued by five publishers, recall is 100.00% against an 80% threshold, and on 51 already-corrected drafts it raises 0.00 false flags per hundred against a threshold of 2. On 240 seeded mutations recall is 100.00% and the parser reads 100% of the case drafts. A deliberately naive free-calculator ablation reaches 10.26% on the same corpus. No third-party pattern text is stored or shipped: each case holds the publisher’s own erratum notice and the numbers it names, with the surrounding draft written from scratch. The method, its thresholds, its ablations, its baseline and its limitations are cited line by line in the app’s design record and its answer key.
