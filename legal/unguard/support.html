Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • Manage a subscription. Subscriptions are handled by your app store. Manage or cancel any time in your App Store or Google Play account settings; uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.
  • A note on what this app is. UNGUARD offers guided practice and educational reading. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or replace care from a clinician — the app itself says this too, and every safety-related piece of reading is free in every state of the app.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.
