Need help with PURRAMID? You're in the right place. BlackRaven AI LLC makes PURRAMID, and we read every message.

Contact us

Email support@blackravenai.com and we'll help. We aim to reply within 2–3 business days. To speed things up, tell us your device and OS version and describe what happened.

Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What ads PURRAMID shows. Family-safe, non-personalised ads from Google AdMob, requested with child-directed treatment and a G content rating for every player. An optional short video gives you +1 life once per tower. An occasional short ad may appear when you choose to stack again — at most every third game, never in your first three games, never within two minutes of the last one, never in the middle of a tower and never on the Daily Stack. Settings › Ad privacy choices reviews consent where the law provides it.
  • Who is playing, and the grown-up check. The first time PURRAMID opens it asks whether a grown-up or a child is playing. Buying, restoring and ad-privacy choices sit behind a short grown-up check. Settings › Who is playing? asks again.
  • No Ads. A single $2.99 purchase (US price; the store shows yours) removes every ad, and +1 life becomes free. It is not a subscription. Restore it in Settings › Restore purchases.
  • Coins are never for sale. Coins are earned by stacking and from Cat Chores, and are spent on coats, hats and trails that change how your cats look, never how the game plays. Nothing paid is random.
  • The first run. Your first tower swings slowly, forgives a miss on the first few cats, and explains a trim and a PURRFECT the first time each happens. Settings › Replay the first-run lessons shows them again.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.
