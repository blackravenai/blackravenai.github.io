Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the purchase screen in the app and choose "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your app-store account and restore for free on your devices.
  • What INTERSTOCK does, and what it never decides. INTERSTOCK reads tables of public law and reports what a dated public source names for a plant and a destination, with the citation, the requirement and the rule’s own words on every line. It does not state what is lawful, does not tell you whether you need a nursery licence, does not issue, hold or replace any certificate or inspection, and gives no legal advice. The destination state’s department of agriculture governs, every table can be out of date, and the shipping decision is yours.
  • Where the tables come from, and how current they are. Three bodies of rule text are bundled. The federal layer is transcribed from the eCFR: 7 CFR 360.200 (174 federal noxious taxa) and the thirteen domestic quarantine subparts of 7 CFR part 301 — a work of the United States Government in the public domain. The state layer is transcribed from the National Plant Board’s state summaries; what is transcribed is the content of state law and the citation to it, and the source documents are never redistributed. The plant-tag layer is transcribed from 35 U.S.C. §§ 161, 163 and 287(a). Every row carries its citation and the date the source itself was published or fetched, and both appear on the cell that used them.
  • Coverage is partial, and the app names the gaps. 30 of 52 jurisdictions carry cited rows today; the other 22 return UNKNOWN by name rather than a guess, and Settings › “What is in these tables” lists them. New Jersey’s summary is a scanned image with no extractable text and is reported as such. Blocks that could not be parsed are counted per state rather than invented. The federal layer can never give an origin verdict: every part-301 subpart delegates its quarantined-area list to the Administrator, so INTERSTOCK returns the program, the regulated article and the section, cited, and refuses to guess at the map.
  • UNKNOWN is not a pass. Where no cited row covers a plant and a destination, INTERSTOCK says UNKNOWN in those words. It never converts an absence of transcribed text into permission.
  • What is free and what is Pro. The instrument is free forever at the same accuracy Pro gets: the full three-layer resolution on 5 plants against 8 destinations, every citation, every source date, every requirement and the rule’s own words on every cell, the plant tag and its patent notice without a limit, 3 recorded shipments in the order book, the Prove-it screen, the tables and coverage screens, and export of everything you have recorded, JSON and CSV, always. Pro adds three things: the whole board — any number of plants against all 52 jurisdictions at once — the whole order book instead of the last three, and the re-check that resolves every recorded shipment again when a bundled table changes version and shows the moved cells with both dates. Pro never buys a different verdict: the resolver is the same resolver on both tiers.
  • Subscription terms. INTERSTOCK Pro is $24.99 a year and opens with a 21-day free introductory offer (annual plan only, offered once per store account), or $4.99 a month with no trial, or $74.99 once for lifetime. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends; the lifetime purchase is a single payment, never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. Cancel a renewing plan any time in your store account — on iOS, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store › Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next charge and the period you have paid for runs to its end, and cancelling during a free trial charges nothing.
  • My Pro plan lapsed. Nothing of yours is deleted, hidden or truncated. Every shipment you ever recorded stays on the device, stays readable, stays editable, stays deletable and stays exportable as CSV and as JSON, however many there are and however far past the free limit. What stops is resolving a new board past 5 plants or 8 destinations, recording a fourth new shipment, and running the re-check. A lapse lowers a ceiling on new work; it never takes a record away.
  • Prove it. Open the Prove-it screen and INTERSTOCK re-runs shipped cases whose answers are known on your own handset, showing its answers beside the expected ones, so you can check the instrument rather than trust it.
  • Does it need a signal at the packing bench? No. The tables and the resolver ship inside the app, so it opens, resolves, builds a tag and exports with the network switched off, in a greenhouse with no bars.
  • Restore purchases. Restore is on the Pro sheet and in Settings; entitlements are re-checked with the store.
  • Reset or delete my data. Your work is stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account to delete.
  • Report a bug or request a feature. Email us with what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot.
