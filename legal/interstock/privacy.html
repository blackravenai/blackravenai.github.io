Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-09-05

INTERSTOCK is a plant-shipping reference tool for small nursery and plant sellers, made by BlackRaven AI LLC. It runs entirely on your device. There is no account and no sign-in, and we designed it to need as little of your information as possible.

The short version

  • No account, no login, no name or email required.
  • Your data is saved on your device, not on our servers.
  • We don't run our own analytics or tracking.
  • INTERSTOCK has no server of its own. It resolves every plant in your box against the destination state summaries and the federal rules, writes the citation, the source date and the rule text, and builds the plant tag on the device, and it works with the network switched off. There is no artificial-intelligence service behind it, and nothing about your shipments, your plants or your customers is sent anywhere to be read. The app makes zero network requests of any kind on every screen. The only exception is buying or restoring a subscription: that is handled by Apple’s or Google’s own purchase system, and the receipt it returns is checked by our subscription provider (RevenueCat) so the app knows whether Pro is active. That check carries an anonymous store purchase identifier and the receipt — never your name, your orders, your plants or your destinations.
  • INTERSTOCK shows no ads. Store versions may offer purchases (via RevenueCat and the app store); those partners have their own privacy policies, linked below.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or photos. We have no user accounts and no server-side profile of you. The only time we see your email is if you choose to email our support address.

Data stored on your device

Your recorded shipments — the plants in each box, the origin state, every destination, every cell’s verdict with the citation, the requirement, the rule text and the date the source carried, the table version that produced it and any re-check kept beside it — together with your default origin state, your usual destinations, your usual plant list and your app settings, are stored locally on your device, in the app’s local storage under the key interstock.save.v2. You can export the whole order book as CSV and the entire save as JSON at any time, on any tier, including after a Pro plan ends. None of it is uploaded or transmitted by the app, and deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising

INTERSTOCK does not show ads and does not include any advertising SDK.

Purchases

If INTERSTOCK offers in-app purchases, the transaction is handled by the app store (Apple App Store or Google Play) and processed for us by RevenueCat, which manages entitlements and receipts. We never see or store your payment card details. RevenueCat may process a purchase/receipt identifier to unlock what you bought and restore it across your devices. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy, the Apple Privacy Policy, and the Google Privacy Policy.

Analytics

INTERSTOCK does not include our own third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. Any limited, aggregated diagnostics you receive come from the app stores' standard reporting (for example crash counts), which is governed by Apple's and Google's policies, not ours.

Children

INTERSTOCK is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will address it.

Your choices and rights

Because we don't hold a personal profile of you, most control lives on your device: you can clear the app's local data. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA over data our purchase partners process; you can exercise those directly with those partners (linked above) or contact us and we'll help point you to the right place.

International users

BlackRaven AI LLC operates the app and support from Wyoming, United States. Our purchase partners may process data in other countries under their own safeguards.
